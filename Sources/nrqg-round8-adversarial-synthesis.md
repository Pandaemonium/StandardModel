# Round 8: Adversarial Review × 3, and the Methodology That Survives

*Three passes of hostile review — a physicist, a methodologist, and a meta-critic — each followed by constructive synthesis. Concessions are made in full where the attacks land; the surviving structure is consolidated in Part IV as the program's unified methodology. The rule for this round: every attack is written to win if it can.*

# Pass 1 — The hostile physicist

## Attack 1.1 — "This is a scrapbook, not a theory."

*The program consists of borrowed theorems (Weinberg, Guido–Longo, Casini–Testé–Torroba, CDP, García-Etxebarria–Montero…) stapled to an unconstructed object (the graph measure). The original mathematical content is: an unproven lattice gap, an elementary Cauchy–Binet identity, and an undergraduate free-fermion numeric. Everything else is interpretation.*

**Verdict: lands, in part.** The concession: the program's honest self-description is *a synthesis scaffold plus a verification pipeline plus a small set of original gates* — not (yet) a theory. The fix is structural: every claim now carries an **originality tag** alongside its epistemic tag: `[orig]` original result, `[comp]` original composition of known results, `[import]` borrowed with attribution, `[interp]` interpretation. Under this tagging, the honest inventory of `[orig]` today is: the tetrahedral null kernel and its gap program (pending), the stencil-corrected discrete QNEC observation (small, real), the Gate F2 orbit formulation of Koide (pending), and the three-J/two-grading disentanglement (housekeeping). The `[comp]` column — the dimension chain, the erasability package, the null-monotone unification — is where the program's actual intellectual bet lives: that the *composition* is novel and load-bearing. Compositions can be real contributions (most of theoretical physics is composition), but only if each is stated with its seams showing.

## Attack 1.2 — "You are double-dipping on Lorentz invariance."

*The node bootstrap (Weinberg soft theorems, BCFW four-particle consistency) and Guido–Longo both require exact Poincaré covariance and S-matrix analyticity. Elsewhere the program insists Lorentz invariance is only statistical, per-sample broken. You invoke exact-Lorentz theorems while denying exact Lorentz. Pick one.*

**Verdict: lands — and forces the round's most important constructive synthesis.** The resolution is not a patch; it is a reorganization of the entire tower into **two columns meeting at a fixed point**:

- **Column A (substrate, upward):** the graph axioms — null edges, node fibers, growth measure, statistical Lorentz invariance. Substrate-level claims may only use substrate-safe mathematics (finite operators, counting, discrete Hodge, correlation matrices).
- **Column B (fixed point, downward):** the exact-symmetry theorems — CDP, node bootstrap, Guido–Longo, spin-statistics, Weinberg–Witten dodges, c/F/a monotones — which are **consistency conditions on the infrared fixed point** the substrate must flow to, not laws of the substrate.

The tower's real claim, restated cleanly: *the substrate ensemble must possess an RG fixed point satisfying Column B, and Column B is restrictive enough to force the Standard Model + GR shape of that fixed point.* All imported exact-Lorentz results move to Column B and get the grade **T|FP** — theorem, conditional on reaching the fixed point. This single move resolves Attack 1.2, correctly re-grades Levels 1, 3.5, and the spin-statistics fill, and makes the program's central open problem explicit: **the Measure Problem** — exhibit a growth measure whose fixed point exists and satisfies Column B. Everything else in the program is either finite mathematics (Column A, Lean-able) or fixed-point constraint analysis (Column B, bootstrap-flavored). The two-column form is *tighter* than the tower it replaces.

## Attack 1.3 — "The null interpretation does no work in Gate C1."

*At the level of the free tetrahedral operator, the k_A are coordinates on a rank-4 torus; nothing Lorentzian, causal, or null is being tested. Relabel the lattice Euclidean and every C1 theorem survives verbatim. Your flagship result does not touch your ontology.*

**Verdict: lands cleanly.** Conceded without reservation, and recorded as a rule: **C1's published claims must be stated in interpretation-free form** (a gapped Hermitian kernel on a rank-4 lattice with a GW release), with the null reading offered as motivation only. The ledger of *where null-ness earns its content* is now explicit: Gate C3 (retarded/path-sum structure), the QNEC/Markov program (null cuts are where the monotones live), and the G-gates. Until those land, "null-edge" is a design principle, not a tested claim — and saying so costs nothing, because the design principle is doing its job as a generator of gates.

## Attack 1.4 — "Mass-as-concurrence is decomposition-dependent poetry."

*A timelike P admits infinitely many null decompositions — any n, any split. A quantity attached to a non-unique decomposition is gauge, not physics.*

**Verdict: lands; fix known.** The invariant statement: $m^2 = \det P$ is decomposition-independent; the concurrence expression $\sum_{i<j}|\langle ij\rangle|^2$ is its evaluation on any decomposition; for the minimal split ($n=2$) the non-uniqueness is *exactly* the little-group SU(2) orbit, and mass is the orbit invariant. Corrected claim: **mass is the orbit invariant of the null-splitting gauge; "concurrence" is its value in any gauge.** For $n>2$ the identity remains true but the entanglement language must not pretend the decomposition is physical. Gate I1's Lean statements already use the invariant form; the essays get errata flags. New failure mode **F-RE (reification of formal structures)** enters the register — with the boost–Gibbs "state" $\rho = P/2E$ as its second listed instance: a formal density matrix's modular flow is a *pun on time* until Gate I2 produces a physical system whose reduced state it is.

## Attack 1.5 — "Your live commitment is inherited, and inherits known damage."

*C4 (everpresent Λ) is causal set theory's prediction, not yours; and the everpresent-Λ literature itself documents serious problems — spatial fluctuations of Λ threaten isotropy unless correlated over super-horizon scales, and the model class is admittedly ad hoc in implementation.*

**Verdict: lands.** C4 is re-annotated: an *inherited, class-level* commitment carrying the known spatial-inhomogeneity problem (**F-SPAT**, registered). The constructive residue: NRQG's additions to the causal-set substrate (edge spinors, transport data) are precisely the degrees of freedom that could correlate Λ fluctuations — whether they *do* is a well-posed question that would, if answered, give the program its first genuinely distinguishing phenomenological parameter. Paper P10's scope is updated accordingly: not "everpresent Λ vs DESI" but "does transport structure cure or worsen the spatial problem, and what does DESI-era data say about the surviving parameter space."

## Attack 1.6 — "Koide derivations die on scheme dependence, and yours will too."

*The relation holds for pole masses. Pole masses include QED self-energy; any symmetry of a high-scale mass matrix should apply to running masses at that scale, where Q ≠ 2/3. Every Koide 'derivation' that ignores this is numerology with extra steps.*

**Verdict: lands; absorbed into the gate.** F2's specification now *requires* a scheme clause: the orbit condition must be stated at a declared scale and scheme, with the mechanism for its survival (or emergence) at the pole-mass level identified — infrared fixed points of the mass-matrix RG flow being the only respectable candidate class. Absent a scheme clause, an F2 "success" is pre-declared inadmissible. This is the correct price of entry, and paying it up front is what separates a gate from a hope.

# Pass 2 — The methodologist

## Attack 2.1 — "Survivorship bias: Round 6's 'miraculous fits' are a selection effect."

*With thousands of theorems available, any framework built from fashionable materials (null surfaces, modular theory, codes) will find that 'the missing piece was already manufactured to spec.' You imported what fit and never counted what didn't. Exhibit A: Weinberg–Witten — a famous theorem lethal to your program's naive form — went unnoticed for five rounds and surfaced only during a deliberate gap hunt. The process was fit-seeking.*

**Verdict: lands, and it is the most important process-level finding of this review.** The WW episode is adopted as the program's standing case study in its own confirmation bias. Two mechanical fixes: (i) **negative-search quota** — every imported supporting theorem must be matched by one documented attempt to import a *refuting* theorem, logged whether or not it succeeds; (ii) the Round 6 coda's "this is how being right feels" is formally retracted as epistemically worthless — coherence with selected literature is evidence of selection, not of truth. What remains admissible as internal evidence: *convergences that were not sought* (the σ/ν_R double demand qualifies; it was found in collision, not curation) and *pre-registered tests that passed* (the QNEC pilot qualifies).

## Attack 2.2 — "Auditor independence is zero."

*The same human-AI collaboration generated the claims, the audits, the upgrades, and the downgrades. The AI has strong synthesis ability, weak adversarial incentive, and — critically — cited dozens of theorems from memory across Rounds 4–7. A hostile referee will check García-Etxebarria–Montero's actual hypotheses, Guido–Longo's exact covariance assumptions, the a-theorem proof's scope, the CDP axiom list. Any one misremembered hypothesis collapses a load-bearing import.*

**Verdict: lands in full, and must be said plainly: this document's own author is the conflicted auditor in question.** Fixes, all mechanical because trust is not a mechanism: (i) a **citation-verification debt ledger** is opened — every theorem imported from memory in Rounds 4–7 (order thirty items) is listed as *unverified-import* until checked against the primary source, and no paper may cite an unverified import; this is a blocking pre-publication sprint. (ii) **External review routing**: each pillar gets sent to the community that owns it (C1 → lattice, the modular chain → AQFT, F2 → flavor, P10 → causal set/cosmology), with hostile review explicitly solicited — the plan from Round 3 (PhysLean entry) is extended from "community entry" to "adversary acquisition." (iii) Mechanical verifiers (Lean, numerics, pre-registration timestamps) are weighted above any narrative agreement between the collaborators, including this one.

## Attack 2.3 — "Tag inflation: 'theorem-adjacent,' 'T-chain,' 'derivation pressure' — weasel vocabulary is metastasizing."

**Verdict: lands.** Replaced by a closed **claim calculus** (Part IV §2): five grades, no neologisms, a chain rule (a composite claim's grade is the *minimum* over its links), and a display rule (conditional claims must show their hypotheses inline, e.g. T|FP, T|H₂(soldering)). Under the calculus several Round 4–7 headlines visibly drop, and the drops are accepted: the dimension chain is T|H(soldering-fundamentalism)·T|FP — a doubly conditional theorem, still valuable, no longer advertisable as "a derivation of 3+1."

## Attack 2.4 — "Lakatos: where is the novel fact?"

*Progressive programmes predict novel facts. Your ledger: postdictions (complex QM, ν-mass scale), inherited predictions (everpresent Λ), and one minor original observation (stencil-QNEC). Grade: proto-progressive at best.*

**Verdict: correct, and adopted as the program's honest self-grade.** The methodology now maintains a **novel-fact ledger** with exactly three current candidates for a first genuine novel fact, ranked by proximity: (1) the interacting discrete-QNEC structure (Gate M1/P3 frontier — measurable, owned, nobody else is looking); (2) the F2 orbit result with scheme clause (would be novel if it survives its own kill-conditions); (3) a transport-sector modification of everpresent-Λ phenomenology (P10 scope). The programme commits to being judged on this ledger.

## Attack 2.5 — "The Ω-layer contaminates the science."

*'The scheduler prices demand' appears in Round 6 as if it explains the equivalence principle. Ontological narrative is bleeding into technical claims.*

**Verdict: lands.** The **Ω-firewall** becomes a rule: Ω-language may motivate, may appear in essays, and is *banned from gate statements, proofs, and papers P1–P10*. Audits include a contamination check. The essays remain part of the program — bold interpretation is a declared output, not a shame — but the firewall keeps the two currencies from being confused, in either direction.

# Pass 3 — The meta-critic

## Attack 3.1 — "Falsifiable as a class, not as an individual."

*Strip the ledger: C2, C7, C8 test quantum mechanics and unitarity, not NRQG. C1, C6 are generic to whole families. C4 is causal set theory's. What single observation kills NRQG and nothing else?*

**Verdict: lands; the concession is formalized rather than resisted.** The commitment ledger is partitioned into **class-level** commitments (shared with the discrete/informational family the program belongs to) and **individual** commitments (discriminating). Today the individual column contains: the C1 gap theorem's fate (internal), the stencil-QNEC structure (weakly), and nothing observational. The honest statement, now a fixed part of the program's public description: *NRQG is currently falsifiable as a member of its class; individual observational falsifiability awaits the Measure Problem's solution.* A program that says this about itself cannot be accused of hiding it — and the statement doubles as the precise definition of what "solving the Measure Problem" must deliver: at least one measurable number no sibling theory shares.

## Attack 3.2 — "Eight rounds of documents, one compiling theorem."

*The ratio of prose to proof is growing. Rounds are cheap; gates are dear. The program risks becoming a genre.*

**Verdict: lands as a warning, answered with a budget.** The methodology fixes a **prose-to-proof ratio rule**: no new synthesis round until the gate queue has advanced (a gate closed, piloted, or formally killed since the last one). Round 8 satisfies it via Rounds 5–7's executions (QNEC pilot, control run, Koide pilot); Round 9 will owe its entry fee in closed gates, not pages.

# Part IV — The Unified Methodology (v1.0)

What survives all three passes, consolidated into the program's constitution.

**§1. Architecture: two columns, one problem.**
Column A (substrate): finite, discrete, statistical-symmetry mathematics — everything Lean-able. Column B (fixed point): exact-symmetry consistency conditions the IR must satisfy — everything bootstrap-flavored. They meet at the RG fixed point, and the single central open problem is the **Measure Problem**: exhibit a growth measure over decorated null graphs whose fixed point exists and satisfies Column B. All other open problems are officially subordinate to, or independent of, this one.

**§2. Claim calculus (closed vocabulary).**
Grades: **T** (theorem, source-verified) · **T|H** (theorem conditional on displayed hypotheses; T|FP for fixed-point conditionality) · **M** (program-internal result: Lean-verified or reproducible numerics) · **C** (pre-registered conjecture with gate and kill-condition) · **Ω** (interpretation; firewalled). Orthogonal originality tags: [orig] / [comp] / [import] / [interp]. **Chain rule:** a composite claim's grade is the minimum over its links; its originality is [comp] at best. **Halo ban:** no claim may cite the verification stack unless its content is what was verified. **Interpretation-free statement rule:** flagship results (C1 first) are published in ontology-free form.

**§3. Gate lifecycle.**
Propose (statement + kill-condition + scheme/scale clause where applicable, timestamped) → pilot (cheapest falsification first) → verify (Lean or reproducible numerics) → publish or file the null in the public ledger. A gate without a kill-condition is not a gate. Nulls are deliverables.

**§4. Evidence hygiene (AI-era protocols).**
(i) Citation-verification debt: no unverified import reaches a paper; the Rounds 4–7 debt (~30 items) is a blocking sprint. (ii) Negative-search quota: one refutation hunt per supporting import, logged. (iii) Auditor independence: mechanical verifiers outrank narrative coherence; external hostile review is solicited per pillar; the WW episode is the permanent internal case study. (iv) Convergence evidence is admissible only when unsought; coherence with curated literature is inadmissible.

**§5. Falsifiability taxonomy.**
Commitments are labeled class-level or individual. The program's public self-description includes the current emptiness of the individual-observational column and the definition of the Measure Problem as the requirement to fill it. The novel-fact ledger (three candidates: interacting discrete QNEC; F2-with-scheme-clause; transport-modified Λ phenomenology) is the program's Lakatos scoreboard.

**§6. Consolidated risk register (delta).**
Adds: F-RE (reification of formal structures — boost-Gibbs, decomposition language), F-CB (confirmation-bias process risk — WW case study, negative-search quota as control), F-SCHEME (Koide/F2 scheme dependence), F-LOR (Lorentz double-dipping — *resolved* by two-column, retained as a check), F-SPAT (everpresent-Λ spatial inhomogeneity, inherited into C4/P10), F-IND (auditor non-independence — controls in §4). All prior F-codes retained.

**§7. Priorities (re-derived, mostly unchanged, one addition).**
C1 by day — now to be *written up* in interpretation-free form. Verification-debt sprint before any submission. Gate I1/F2 by night (same finite-algebra species). P3's interacting extension (M1) as the novel-fact frontier. P10 rescoped per F-SPAT. External review routing begins with PhysLean (P2) and the lattice community (P1).

**§8. The elevator statement that survives this round.**
*NRQG is a research program, not yet a theory: a two-column framework in which a discrete informational substrate is constrained from below by finite, machine-verifiable mathematics and from above by the exact consistency theorems its continuum limit must satisfy. Its original content is a small set of gated results; its bet is a specific composition of known theorems; its central open problem is the growth measure; its current falsifiability is class-level and it says so; and its method — pre-registration, kill-conditions, mechanical verification, filed nulls, adversarial quotas — is designed so that if the bet is wrong, the program will be the first to publish the fact.*

## Coda

Three passes, eleven attacks, nine landed in whole or part, zero fatal. The casualties were mostly adjectives: "derivation" became "conditional theorem," "miraculous fit" became "selection effect," "prediction" became "inherited class commitment," "mass is concurrence" became "mass is the orbit invariant." What was never touched: the finite mathematics, the passed pilots, the gate discipline, and the two facts that survive every hostile reading — that the program's claims are now individually addressable, and that its strongest habit is the one on display in this document. A framework is not made robust by winning reviews. It is made robust by metabolizing them, and the metabolism is now written down.
