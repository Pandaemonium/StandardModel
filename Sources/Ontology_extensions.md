## 2. Krein positivity: three graded routes, one of which is greenfield and near

Two bonuses: the finite-dimensional proof is honest linear algebra, and there is no Krein/Pontryagin theory in Mathlib at all — this would be a genuinely citable formalization independent of the physics, and it directly feeds your "indefinite spectral triples, formalized" line.
