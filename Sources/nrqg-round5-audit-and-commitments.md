## 1. The adversarial audit: strongest objection, current defense, repair

### 1.1 The dimension chain (R4-1) — *audited down, then re-armed*

**Strongest objection.** The chain "local tomography ⟹ ℂ ⟹ Herm₂(ℂ) ⟹ 3+1" smuggles a premise: that spacetime *comes from* the amplitude algebra via the rank-one soldering. That is an NRQG postulate [M], not a theorem. A referee will say: you derived the dimension of a structure you first assumed generates spacetime.

**Verdict: correct.** R4-1 is hereby relabeled a **conditional derivation**: *given* soldering-fundamentalism (spacetime = statistics of rank-one edges), the dimension is a theorem of information axioms. What survives unconditionally is still substantial: (i) the conditional itself is precise and formalizable (Gate D1 unchanged); (ii) the premise is independently supported by Gate I1's success — the soldering is not an arbitrary choice but the unique structure under which mass, proper time, and the little group become linear algebra; (iii) the *contrapositive commitments* are unconditional and bold: discovery of geometric extra dimensions (KK modes, sub-millimeter gravity deviations of the compactified type) falsifies the premise, hence the tower. The repair converts a circularity accusation into a kill-condition. Entered in the ledger (§2, C1).

### 1.2 The erasability package (R4-3) — *audited up: C → T-adjacent*

**Strongest objection.** "Anomaly freedom mandates ν_R" is false as stated: the 15-Weyl Standard Model already cancels all *perturbative* (local) gauge and gravitational anomalies. ν_R is optional at that level.

**Verdict: correct — and the repair is a strict upgrade.** The defensible statement is *global*: the Standard Model carries a ℤ₁₆ global (Dai–Freed–type) anomaly, and its cancellation requires 16 Weyl fermions per generation — i.e. exactly ν_R — **or** an exotic gapped topological sector in its place (García-Etxebarria–Montero; Wang–Wen's nonperturbative SM definition and Wang's "ultra-unification" TQFT alternative). This is now literature-grade mathematics, and it is *precisely* the erasability logic of v2 §5.3 in its sharpest known form: the mirror/completion sector must carry zero protected information mod 16. The package claim becomes: **the tower mandates either three right-handed neutrinos or a topological dark sector** — a genuine dichotomy, both horns of which are bold, testable-in-principle, and dark-matter-relevant. R4-3's confidence rating rises (4/10 → 6/10) and its bin moves from C toward P.

### 1.3 The gravity chain (v2 §6) — *the type-mismatch repair sketch*

**Strongest objection (F-M2, standing).** The DPI→QNEC machinery is proved in type III von Neumann algebras; finite graphs are type I; "take the continuum limit" is not an argument.

**Repair sketch (new).** The modern crossed-product results (Chandrasekaran–Longo–Penington–Witten and successors) show that *including gravity and an observer* converts the type III algebra of a horizon/subregion into type II — an algebra with a trace, well-defined density matrices, and finite entropies. Type II sits *between* the graph's type I and the naive continuum's type III. The repaired conjecture: **the continuum limit of the null-graph algebras is the type II crossed product, with type III recovered only in the strict G→0 limit.** This turns F-M2 from "unbridgeable gap" into a specific, modern, respectable target: exhibit the graph → type II flow on the simplest half-strand algebra. Hard, but now shaped like mathematics rather than like hope.

### 1.4 The Past Hypothesis dissolution (R4-4) — *audited and sharpened*

**Strongest objection.** Penrose's actual puzzle is not "why was total entropy low" but "why was the *matter* sector thermal (near-maximal) while the *gravitational* sector was absurdly sub-maximal (smooth geometry)." A bound $S \le O(N)$ does not touch the sectoral imbalance.

**Repair — and the repaired claim is bolder.** In the growth ontology the early universe was not a low-entropy state of a big system; it was a **small system at full capacity**: matter thermal because the allocated memory was saturated, gravitationally smooth because clumping degrees of freedom *did not yet exist* — the graph had not yet allocated the horizon-scale channels whose occupation is gravitational entropy. The arrow exists because **capacity has grown faster than entropy production ever since** (the Egan–Lineweaver-style budget: today's entropy ~10⁴ Planck-areas' worth against a ceiling of ~10¹²²). Restated: *there never was a low-entropy state; there was a small universe with no room, and room has been outrunning disorder for 13.8 Gyr.* The mystery becomes quantitative — the growth-vs-production race — and lands in the same sector (growth dynamics, Gate G2′) as the inflation slot. Two mysteries, one missing measure.

### 1.5 The generation triple (R4-2) — *audited, kept on probation*

**Strongest objection.** The J₃(𝕆) programs (Todorov–Dubois-Violette, Boyle) have known unresolved difficulties matching the full SM representation content; and Gate F1 as stated ("compute 3-tangles from CKM") is under-determined — there are many inequivalent embeddings of flavor data into 2×2×2 tensors, so a positive result could be numerology and a scan could be noise.

**Verdict: correct.** F1 is downgraded from "run this month" to "sharpen, then run": the gate now requires a *pre-registered canonical construction* (fixed before looking at numbers) with its transformation properties under rephasing proven first — the Jarlskog invariant's defining property. The QNEC pilot won this round's execution slot precisely because it has no embedding ambiguity (§3). R4-2 stays at confidence 3/10; honesty is cheaper than enthusiasm.

## 3. Gate P3 calibration pilot: executed

The discrete QNEC protocol (Round 3, A3) was run today on the 1+1d free-fermion chain. Setup: Peschel correlation-matrix entropies; null deformations mapped through the Lorentz-invariant vacuum ($S = S(\sqrt{x^+x^-})$), giving the null curvature $\partial_+^2 S = \tfrac14[S'' - S'/L]$; parity-2 stencils to cancel Friedel oscillations. Quantities: plain QNEC $K(L) := \tfrac14[S''-S'/L] \le 0$; CFT-improved (c=1) $Q(L) := K + \tfrac{3}{2}S'^2 \to 0$ (vacuum saturation).

**Results.**

1. **Calibration exact.** Fitted central charge from $S(L) = (c/3)\ln L$: $c = 1.0000$.
2. **Plain discrete QNEC: zero violations.** $K(L) < 0$ at every point, for massless and both massive runs ($m = 0.05, 0.2$; the handful of $+10^{-15}$ entries at saturated massive tails are float noise on a constant, reported as numerical zero).
3. **Improved bound: saturation, resolved to the stencil.** The raw improved combination shows a positive $O(L^{-4})$ tail — but a control run applying the identical finite-difference stencil to the *exact* continuum entropy $(1/3)\ln L$ reproduces that tail to all displayed digits. After stencil correction, the physical residual is **negative at every resolvable point** ($-1.9\times10^{-7}$ at $L=8$, $-4.5\times10^{-10}$ at $L=16$, below machine noise past $L \approx 64$): the improved discrete QNEC holds and saturates *from the allowed side*, to machine precision.
4. **Massive case: strictly inside the bound** through the crossover at $\xi \sim 1/m$, with entropy saturating exactly as the concurrence-mass picture predicts.

**What the pilot bought.** (i) The discrete QNEC conjecture survives first contact — C10 enters the ledger as a live commitment with evidence. (ii) A lemma-shaped methodological discovery: *naive* discretizations of the saturated bound sit on the violating side by an exactly computable stencil error; the discrete QNEC must be stated stencil-aware, and then saturation is exact. That observation is publishable content for P3 on its own, and it is precisely the kind of discretization-vs-physics separation the paper's referees would demand — now pre-empted. (iii) P3's risk rating improves (F: medium → medium-high); the interacting/excited-state runs are the remaining science.
