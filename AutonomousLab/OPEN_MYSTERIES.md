# Open mysteries portfolio

AFPL cannot credibly attack every mystery at once. These questions enter the
portfolio only through dependency-ready work items with a conventional
baseline and discriminating gate.

| Mystery | Five-year AFPL question | Earliest dependency | Honest success |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quantum gravity | Can one coupled finite quantum system recover both local QFT observables and a dynamical GR limit? | P2 continuum QFT plus P4 random geometry | Controlled common limit or a no-go identifying the incompatible axioms |
| Cosmological constant/dark energy | Which physical count or variable is conjugate to Lambda, what fixes sign and fluctuations, and do observations permit them? | Lorentz ensemble, gravity response, scale dictionary | Derived stochastic/mean response with constraints, or decisive rejection of everpresent Lambda |
| Origin and hierarchy of mass | Does the Pluecker/operator mechanism select scales and ratios without inserting them? | Derived dynamics, RG, Higgs/QCD interfaces | At least one non-fitted mass relation or a theorem proving the missing dimensional input |
| Dark matter | Does the integrated theory require new stable sectors, modify gravity, or remain neutral? | Stable many-body sectors and gravity limit | A constrained candidate/exclusion and an observable signature, not a label for unexplained residuals |
| Neutrino masses and mixing | Are Dirac/Majorana structure, family rank, and CP phases forced by the finite algebra/dynamics? | Chiral gauge matter and generation mechanism | Derived representation/mass structure with a held-out relation |
| Matter-antimatter asymmetry | Can the theory supply CP violation, out-of-equilibrium dynamics, and number violation consistently? | Interacting QFT, anomalies, cosmology | Quantitative mechanism satisfying the required conditions or a formal obstruction |
| Higgs and hierarchy problem | Is the scalar sector primitive, collective, geometric, or absent, and how is its scale stabilized? | Gauge QFT and mass selection | Correct degrees of freedom, couplings, vacuum, and radiative behavior with fewer unexplained inputs |
| QCD confinement and mass gap | Can closure/holonomy structures connect to a controlled nonabelian continuum theory? | Gauge covariance, locality, RG | Recognized finite-volume/strong-coupling theorem connected to continuum physics, without claiming the Clay problem prematurely |
| Black holes and information | Do the theory's causal, entropy, and modular structures reproduce horizon thermodynamics and unitary information flow? | Coupled quantum geometry | Benchmark entropy/first-law/information result with a stated semiclassical limit |
| Measurement and classicality | Does the decoder/constraint framework derive stable records and probabilities? | Complete finite operational theory | Born probabilities and stable records from stated dynamics, with no observer hidden in definitions |

## Priority rule

Quantum gravity, the cosmological constant, and mass-scale selection are the
first flagship mysteries because they lie closest to the program's claimed
distinctive mechanisms. Neutrinos, Higgs, QCD, dark sectors, baryogenesis,
black holes, and measurement enter as dependencies mature.

The Visionary may propose a moonshot on any row. The Lab Manager may activate
it only if the cheap falsifier, dependency map, and opportunity cost are
recorded.
