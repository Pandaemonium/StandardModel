# Ten-day grand-challenge campaign (2026-07-21 to 2026-07-31)

This is the active campaign overlay for the Autonomous Fundamental Physics Lab
while the Research Director is away. It refines, but does not erase, Fable's
original `TEN_DAY_AMBITIOUS_ROADMAP_2026-07-21.md`. Repository `AGENTS.md`, the
AFPL charter, governance, evidence model, and operating system remain superior.

Executors are Codex/Sol and interactive Claude Code/Fable as co-equal research
families, with Aristotle as proof strategist and prover. The campaign is judged
by how far the honest scientific frontier moves, not by theorem, file, or page
count.

## 1. Campaign thesis

In ten days, attempt to turn the strongest finite null-edge results into two or
three connected, externally legible theorem chains:

1. a scoped but complete account of relativistic mass representation, followed
   by the hardest available dynamical mass-selection and measurement gates;
2. a local-unitary 3+1 theory that advances beyond the landed free continuum
   limit toward gauge covariance, physical-band transport, second quantization,
   and an observable interaction;
3. a nonvacuous finite-to-continuum geometry chain that distinguishes causal
   order, conformal structure, scale data, and the Einstein-equation limit.

Two additional high-risk lanes test the program against established physics:
a correctly hypothesized Lorentz-randomness/Lambda theorem, and an internal
Standard Model derivation lane. Mature E8, octonion, and continuum results are
packaged and reproduced rather than repeatedly rediscovered.

The ambition is a candidate architecture, not a promise of completion. A sharp
no-go that kills a favored route is a crown-jewel result when its hypotheses,
witnesses, and physical scope are exact.

The campaign runs no more than six active scientific programs and no more than
three executing items per interactive model. Approximate effort allocation:

## 5. Crown jewel I: origin of mass, complete within a declared scope

### Scientific claim to earn

Every future-directed nonspacelike four-momentum, and therefore every value of
the Poincare quadratic mass invariant, admits a finite null-edge Pluecker
representation. Within an explicit mechanism grammar, the program then states
which additional structures produce fermion gaps, vector masses, scalar radial
modes, composite poles, and gravitational source terms. Representation,
mechanism, measurement, and numerical scale remain distinct layers.

### Rungs

1. **Kinematic capstone.** Compose cone positivity, null-edge bundle coverage,
   Pauli soldering, mass surjectivity, and Lorentz-Casimir uniqueness into one
   guarded theorem with a nonzero rational witness and a massless boundary
   control.
2. **Taxonomy theorem.** Define the scope of "all forms of mass" using
   Poincare representation theory and a finite mechanism grammar. Prove an
   exhaustiveness theorem for the grammar or exhibit a minimal missing class.
   Do not infer dynamical completeness from kinematic completeness.
3. **Measurement bridge.** Connect a gauge-invariant composite observable to a
   transfer/resolvent pole and positive gap in one nonabelian finite model, or
   prove precisely why the existing data do not determine the pole. A gap,
   response pole, and assigned readout may not be silently identified.
4. **Mass selection.** Construct one natural finite action, ensemble, or RG
   flow with a stable nonzero Pluecker scale whose value is not merely renamed
   from an inserted mass. If impossible, strengthen the homogeneous-scale
   collapse result into a useful no-go class and identify the minimal new
   dimensional datum.
5. **Equivalence-principle gate.** Derive inertial response and gravitational
   source from independently motivated finite constructions and compare them.
   A definition making them equal by construction does not pass.
6. **Species audit.** Close the arbitrary-generation Majorana/Takagi algebra
   that survives audit, and grade fermion, gauge, scalar/Higgs, composite, and
   binding-energy rows separately.

### Gate

A single reproducible claim map links every promoted mass statement to an exact
theorem, witness, convention, mechanism class, measurement status, and scale
status. The P1 manuscript can then say "complete kinematic representation"
without saying "all observed masses derived."

### Kill conditions

- If the taxonomy requires Standard Model dynamics not present in the model,
  state only a representation theorem and a mechanism inventory.
- If a selected scale is an input parameter in disguise, classify it as fitted
  or supplied.
- If the composite observable and pole do not meet, publish the obstruction.
- If inertial and gravitational mass share only a definition, leave the
  equivalence principle open.

### Publication target

Release-ready P1 origin-of-mass manuscript plus a compact formal supplement and
an explicit "derived / represented / supplied / fitted / open" table.

## 6. Crown jewel II: 3+1 beyond the free continuum theorem

### Scientific claim to earn

The landed HNU construction is a strictly local, exactly unitary 3+1 regulator
with a controlled free massive Dirac limit. The ten-day goal is to show which
additional physical structures can be added without losing locality, unitarity,
band control, or the continuum theorem.

### Rungs

1. **Spectral completion.** Finish the exact Cayley characteristic determinant,
   rank-two physical band, gap domain, continuity, and endpoint controls. State
   what is global and what holds only away from band collisions.
2. **Moving-band theorem.** Prove a quantitative finite adiabatic transport or
   leakage bound for the canonical physical projector. If topology blocks a
   global frame, formulate transport on projectors or local charts rather than
   forcing a global eigenbasis.
3. **Gauge-covariant walk.** Add U(1) link transport to the live HNU update,
   prove exact finite gauge covariance and a conserved finite current, then
   attempt a changing-lattice continuum limit to the minimally coupled Dirac
   equation on smooth compactly controlled backgrounds.
4. **Many-body lift.** Second-quantize the actual local HNU update, proving
   vacuum, one-particle, number/parity, unitarity, and inherited finite
   propagation locality. Add one local even interaction that is not a disguised
   one-particle update.
5. **Observable consequence.** Compute one exact finite scattering phase,
   bound-state shift, defect mode, response coefficient, or selection rule in
   the interacting/gauged model. A fitted dispersion curve is not enough.
6. **Curved/variable-coframe stretch.** Compare the construction with known
   grouped/encoded quantum walks for 3+1 curved-spacetime Dirac equations. A
   variable-coframe extension is promoted only with a precise continuum
   theorem and convention map.

### Gate

At least one of the following complete chains lands:

- local gauge-covariant walk -> exact covariance/current -> covariant continuum
  Dirac limit;
- local one-particle HNU update -> local fermionic second quantization ->
  nontrivial many-body observable;
- canonical physical band -> quantitative transport theorem -> topological
  obstruction or protected response.

### Kill conditions

- Do not call finite-range support Lorentz invariance.
- Do not call retardedness a determinant-level no-doubling theorem.
- Do not identify a continuously chosen projector with a global eigenbasis.
- If gauge covariance destroys the existing error estimate, land the exact
  finite theorem and state the missing analytic estimate.
- If an interaction is quasi-free or acts trivially outside a selected toy
  subspace, say so explicitly.

### Novelty audit

The Archivist compares every headline against at least the Bialynicki-Birula
QCA, Arrighi-Forets-Nesme convergence theorem, Arrighi-Facchini grouped curved
walk, and published gauge-coupled Dirac-walk constructions. Merely reproducing
their qualitative result is a benchmark, not a novelty claim.

### Publication target

A focused 3+1 theorem paper with a standalone reproducer, not another broad
program survey.

## 7. Crown jewel III: causal order, scale, and gravity

### Scientific claim to earn

A finite null-information structure can encode causal/conformal data, acquire
scale only through explicit decorations or counting data, and support a
nontrivial finite variational geometry whose controlled limit is tested against
Einstein dynamics.

### Rungs

1. **Malament split.** Package the exact division between causal-order
   reconstruction of conformal structure and the additional datum that supplies
   volume/scale. Ban prose that attributes the scale to order alone.
2. **Nonvacuous Palatini witness.** Test the concrete vacuum-Weyl-jet carrier
   against all five `FinitePalatiniBoundaryFlux` predicates. Land either the
   witnessed interior theorem or the exact failed predicates and minimal repair.
3. **Weak/averaged convergence.** Replace a pointwise residual demand, where
   appropriate, by a preregistered averaged or weak residual modeled on the
   Regge-calculus literature. Prove a finite averaging lemma before attaching a
   continuum interpretation.
4. **Nonflat benchmark.** Run the formal action and residual on one explicit
   curved solution or local jet with nonzero curvature and an independently
   checkable continuum target.
5. **Matter coupling.** Couple one already-audited null-edge or Higgs/link
   source to the finite geometry and prove a conservation or covariance law.
6. **Scaling theorem stretch.** State a changing-complex refinement map and
   prove a weak convergence result, or isolate the exact compactness/coercivity
   theorem that prevents it.

### Gate

The chain contains a nonzero curvature witness, a displayed scale datum, a
finite variation, and a continuum comparison norm or distributional test. Each
component has an independent control.

### Kill conditions

- Failure of any structural predicate keeps the Einstein theorem conditional.
- Pointwise Regge residual failure is not automatically continuum failure; the
  tested topology must be named.
- Finite covariance is not general relativity.
- A bare graph is not said to canonically supply a tetrad unless proved.

### Publication target

A rigorous finite-geometry note centered on one theorem or no-go, with the
larger gravity ontology clearly separated.

## 8. High-risk lane IV: Lorentz randomness and Lambda

### Corrected target

Do not attempt to prove that Lorentz invariance alone forces a Poisson process.
Instead classify the additional hypotheses under which a noncompact orbit,
integrable covariance, independent increments, exact number-volume
correspondence, or frame-blind finite statistic forces decorrelation or Poisson
counting. Separate point-process law, two-point covariance, and regional
variance.

### Rungs

- formalize the no-finite-invariant-measure statement for a genuine noncompact
  orbit or an exact finite exhaustion;
- derive correlation suppression only from explicit orbit-integrability or
  summability assumptions;
- prove Poisson uniqueness only from Poisson-characterizing assumptions such as
  independent increments or the appropriate void/count law;
- compare Poisson, mixed-Poisson, clustered, and hyperuniform controls;
- state the cosmological-constant implication conditionally on the stochastic
  and geometric dictionary.

### Gate and kill

The theorem must contain a noncompact witness and a non-Poisson control. If the
extra hypothesis already contains the conclusion, the result is hollow. If no
nontrivial bridge to Lambda survives, publish the probability theorem or no-go
without cosmological gloss.

## 9. High-risk lane V: Standard Model internal derivation

### Targets

- derive the weak-isospin ladder and `W+`, `W-`, `T3` action from the stated
  quaternionic/complex-octonionic operator algebra rather than importing a
  particle table;
- complete arbitrary-generation symmetric-mass/Takagi structure with explicit
  assumptions and convention bridges;
- connect finite chirality, gauge legality, and anomaly cancellation without
  using the desired chirality projector as an input;
- test whether three-generation or Yukawa structure is selected or remains a
  moduli problem.

### Gate and kill

Every target table entry must be an output of the operator action. Relabeling a
supplied basis is not a derivation. Three generations remain open unless a
rank-fixing theorem or physical axiom excludes the other ranks.

## 13. Stagnation, outage, and recovery rules

- After 90 minutes without a new proof state, counterexample, source fact, or
  sharper statement, split or pivot the item.
- After three failed attacks on the same statement, commission a no-go or
  missing-hypothesis analysis.
- After six hours blocked, move the blocker into a precise handoff and take the
  next dependency-ready item.
- If one interactive family disappears, continue in degraded or intentional
  solo mode but queue its independent reviews; never self-certify them.
- If Aristotle is unavailable, prepare statements, sources, tests, and context
  packs. Do not weaken theorems.
- If Neo4j or Zotero is unavailable, use direct primary-source search and log
  degraded provenance.
- If the Lean build is unavailable, nothing is promoted as landed.
- After repeated supervisor crashes, stop automatic restart, preserve logs,
  and leave a Director-queue incident rather than creating a restart storm.

## 14. Role cadence

Periodic roles are work contracts, not decorative personas:

- Scientist: continuous execution.
- Skeptic: every promotion gate and at least one live flagship audit.
- Visionary: every 3 hours, with one queue-changing synthesis or explicit
  recommendation to stay the course.
- Lab Manager: every 3 hours, emphasizing state freshness, leases, costs,
  failures, and build health.
- Archivist: every 6 hours per active literature-dependent cluster; update
  Zotero/Neo4j without duplicates and cite primary full text.
- Impact Strategist: every 6 hours; ask what independent community problem the
  theorem solves and what artifact makes it legible.
- Educator and Phenomenologist: every 12 hours; the Phenomenologist must demand
  an observable dictionary, and the Educator may simplify only graded claims.
- Reproducer: triggered for every release candidate and on Day 9.

Once daily, the Skeptic writes a short "claim obituary" attempting to kill the
most cherished current interpretation.

## 15. Evidence and claim gates

A result is promoted only when all applicable boxes are checked:

- exact Lean statement matches intended mathematics;
- kernel and pinned-toolchain build pass;
- build-enforced assumption guard passes;
- nondegenerate witness and degenerate control are explicit;
- source provenance and convention map are recorded;
- independent model-family semantic review is complete;
- another agent can reproduce the command from a clean-context packet;
- manuscript wording uses the program's T / T|H / M / C / Open grades and
  originality tags;
- representation is not called explanation, mechanism is not called scale
  prediction, covariance is not called dynamics, and a toy observable is not
  called an experimental prediction;
- all stronger superseded phrasings are removed from abstracts, captions,
  tables, results overviews, and general-audience prose.

For headline results, explicitly audit vacuity, hollow telescoping,
docstring-outruns-kernel, false shape, hidden conventions, and supplied-answer
circularity.

## 16. Success levels

### Floor: a successful honest campaign

- state and job backlog reconciled;
- two substantial theorem chains or no-gos integrated and independently
  audited;
- one release-ready manuscript and one clean reproducer;
- all touched guards and builds green;
- spend within cap and failures fully recorded.

### Target: outstanding ten-day campaign

- origin-of-mass kinematic/taxonomy capstone plus one genuine measurement or
  selection advance;
- one complete 3+1 successor chain from Section 6;
- one nonvacuous gravity or Lorentz-randomness theorem/no-go;
- two focused manuscripts or formal supplements ready for external review;
- one conventional benchmark and one observable calculation.

### Stretch: field-shaping campaign

- three crown-jewel chains survive hostile reproduction;
- at least one theorem solves a recognizable problem independently of the
  null-edge ontology;
- the combined architecture yields a held-out coefficient, protected response,
  or falsifiable scaling law not freely fitted;
- the program overview can be rewritten around these results without weakening
  any evidence grade.
