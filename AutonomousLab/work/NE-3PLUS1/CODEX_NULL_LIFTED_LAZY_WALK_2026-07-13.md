# Null-lifting a doubler-suppressed lazy Dirac walk

Date: 2026-07-13
Role: Archivist / Visionary / Skeptic
Status: literature-informed construction target; no equivalence theorem yet

## New opening

Gupta and Short, *Fermion Doubling in Dirac Quantum Walks*,
arXiv:2601.15885v2 (2026), construct a parameterized family of 1+1 and 3+1
Dirac quantum walks. Their abstract and introduction state that suitable
parameters remove ordinary doublers and high-quasienergy pseudo-doublers while
retaining the Dirac continuum limit. The family is obtained by relaxing the
conventional requirement that the walker has zero probability of remaining at
the same site during one lattice tick. In 3+1 dimensions they still report a
small number of additional low-energy solutions that are not ordinary Dirac
doublers.

This is directly relevant to the null-edge obstruction. The current minimal
architecture imposed literal movement on every coarse tick. The new family
shows that the zero-stay condition, rather than strict locality or unitarity
alone, may be carrying much of the doubling burden.

Source:

## Lateral proposal

Do not interpret a coarse stay amplitude as a primitive process that waits.
Refine one coarse tick into two or more null microticks and realize the stay
branch as a coherent null excursion that returns to its starting site:

    (t, x) -> (t + epsilon, x + n)
           -> (t + 2 epsilon, x).

Each microscopic segment is lightlike. The coarse endpoint is timelike. This
is the same distinction already used by the checkerboard ontology, now applied
to the regulator term that suppresses doubling.

Introduce a finite route register recording the outgoing null direction. A
coarse walk `W_lazy` is null-liftable if there are local unitary microsteps
`U_1, ..., U_r` on the enlarged route space and an encoding `E` such that

    U_micro = U_r ... U_1,
    U_micro E = E W_lazy,

and every position-changing matrix element of each `U_j` follows an allowed
null edge. The encoded subspace must be exactly invariant; postselection is not
allowed.

## Why this may evade the current no-go

The live minimal no-go concerns a particular four-channel, range-one,
single-factor architecture with no stationary coefficient. The proposed lift
relaxes all three architectural restrictions in a controlled way:

- it adds a finite route register;
- one physical tick contains several null substeps;
- an effective stationary coefficient is generated by a closed null history.

It does not relax microscopic unitarity, finite propagation, or null support.
The no-go result remains valid for its stated class.

## Smallest exact theorem: return-channel dilation

Start with a one-dimensional finite cycle and an arbitrary onsite coin `C`.
Define conditional right and left null shifts on an auxiliary route qubit.
Construct a two- or four-substep unitary `U_return` and an explicit isometric
encoding `E` such that

    U_return E = E C,

while every microstep has only unit-speed position-changing support. Prove:

1. exact unitarity of every microstep;
2. exact invariance of `range E`;
3. exact equality of compressed and target coarse evolution;
4. strict micro-causal support;
5. a nontrivial witness in which the encoded coarse state has nonzero stay
   amplitude although no primitive transmitted segment waits.

This theorem tests the ontology/mechanics bridge without importing the full
Gupta-Short symbol.

## 3+1 composition target

After the dilation lemma, transcribe the paper's 3+1 walk with its conventions
and parameter restrictions. Clean-room formalize the following chain:

1. `W_GS(q)` is unitary for all Brillouin momenta.
2. Its low-momentum tangent is the desired Dirac generator.
3. In the stated parameter regime it has no ordinary doubler and no
   high-quasienergy pseudo-doubler in the authors' definitions.
4. The reported residual low-energy solutions are classified exactly.
5. There is a finite null lift `U_null(q)` and an invariant encoding `E` with
   `U_null(q) E = E W_GS(q)`.
6. The Pluecker rest operator can replace the scalar mass parameter without
   changing the spectral proof.

Items 3 and 4 must use the paper's exact definitions. An eigenvalue census at
only the zero/pi corners is not enough if residual solutions occur elsewhere.

## Decisive kill tests

1. **No finite exact dilation.** The required stay coefficient cannot be
   realized on an invariant encoded sector by finite local null microsteps.
2. **Hidden non-null primitive.** A purported microstep contains a stationary
   transmission rather than only an onsite coin or a null shift.
3. **Residual physical modes.** The additional 3+1 low-energy solutions couple
   to the same local observables as the target Dirac sector.
4. **Pluecker incompatibility.** Replacing the paper's mass parameter by the
   Pluecker rest operator breaks unitarity or the spectral bounds.
5. **Interaction instability.** Second quantization or local interactions
   reactivate the removed pseudo-doubler sector or destabilize the vacuum.
6. **Continuum mismatch.** The finer microtime scaling changes the emergent
   light speed or Dirac normalization.

## Relationship to the decoded-code route

The null-lifted lazy walk is the preferred constructive route because it starts
from a published family already designed against both doublers and
pseudo-doublers. The decoded-code route remains the fallback if the residual
3+1 solutions cannot be eliminated but can be shown to lie outside a stable
physical sector.

The even-right rank-four Clifford projector may provide the required invariant
four-component encoding, but this is a hypothesis. The next intertwiner theorem
must be stated against the explicit Gupta-Short walk rather than an abstract
symbol if it is to carry physical weight.
