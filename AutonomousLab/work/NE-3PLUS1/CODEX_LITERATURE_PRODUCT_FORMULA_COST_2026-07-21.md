# Literature pass: product-formula cost for the HNU continuum theorem

Date: 2026-07-21
Role: Archivist / Research Scientist
Work item: `QCA-3PLUS1-001`

## Question

Is the exponential-in-momentum envelope in the current massive HNU adaptive
schedule a physically meaningful obstruction, or can unitary product-formula
estimates certify a polynomial schedule on changing momentum windows?

## Primary sources

1. A. M. Childs, Y. Su, M. C. Tran, N. Wiebe, and S. Zhu, "A Theory of
   Trotter Error," arXiv:1912.08854, later Phys. Rev. X 11, 011020 (2021).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.08854

   The paper develops product-formula bounds controlled by commutators rather
   than only by sums of generator norms. For real-time unitary evolution this
   is the relevant proof architecture: propagator factors have norm one, so a
   generic Banach-algebra exponential envelope is needlessly pessimistic.

2. P. Arrighi, M. Forets, and V. Nesme, "The Dirac equation as a quantum walk:
   higher dimensions, observational convergence," arXiv:1307.3524.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.3524

   Their convergence architecture separates the finite-time walk-versus-flow
   estimate from sampling and reconstruction of continuum wave functions. The
   paper supports treating the adaptive step count as a regulator accuracy
   choice, not as a momentum-dependent physical clock.

3. A. M. Childs and Y. Su, "Nearly optimal lattice simulation by product
   formulas," arXiv:1901.00564.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.00564

   The local-error representation shows why product formulas can have much
   better scaling than a global exponential-norm estimate suggests. It is a
   useful successor reference if the HNU proof is later lifted from each
   finite momentum fibre to local many-body dynamics.

## Consequence for the live theorem

The current coefficient `massiveCEnvelope M R` contains
`(R+M)^2 exp(R+M)`. With the changing window `R_N = 3(N+1)`, the current common
step count is therefore exponentially large in `N`. This is a sufficient
certificate, not evidence for an exponential physical hierarchy.

The HNU endpoint has more structure than the generic estimate uses:

- its kinetic part is an ordered product of eight exact unitary exponentials;
- their skew-Hermitian generator norms add to at most `qAbs q`;
- the Pluecker mass coin is one further exact unitary exponential with
  generator norm `norm z`;
- the exact comparison flow is generated by their sum.

The literature therefore motivates the target

```text
one-step error <= C * (qAbs q + norm z)^2 * eps^2,
many-step error <= C * (R + M)^2 * t^2 / n.
```

For `R_N = 3(N+1)`, choosing `n` proportional to
`(R_N+M)^2 (N+1)` gives a cubic schedule for fixed `M` and `t`. This would turn
the continuum theorem into a quantitatively credible polynomial certificate.

## Kill conditions

- The eight-factor HNU word does not equal the claimed exponential word under
  the live sign and half-step conventions.
- The generators do not sum to the landed kinetic tangent.
- A block embedding or Dirac-basis conjugation introduces an uncontrolled
  norm factor.
- The Pluecker coin is not the exact mass exponential at nonzero `z`.

The first failing item, if any, should replace the polynomial claim with a
precise obstruction. No empirical or physical conclusion should be drawn from
the current exponential envelope until this sharper audit is complete.
