## Ranked impact opportunities

### 1. Complete the exact continuum dynamics chain

Current rung: **Impact ladder 2**, reusable exact formal machinery.

The exact L2 time group, strong continuity, Schwartz preservation, Fourier
symbol theorem, and continuous position Dirac operator are now separate landed
rungs. The decisive next result is a single theorem identifying the derivative
of the exact group with the position-space Dirac operator on an explicit dense
domain, followed by the changing-lattice convergence theorem that derives this
flow from the finite walk.

Why a serious reader cares: it converts a finite-update construction into a
controlled continuum evolution and exposes every sign, `2*pi`, domain, and
topology choice to kernel checking.

Benchmark: the standard free Dirac unitary group on Schwartz or Sobolev data.
The program adds value only if the finite walk converges to that benchmark with
an explicit error or if a precise obstruction is proved.

Kill condition: if only pointwise multiplier differentiation can be proved,
publish it as infrastructure and do not call it a PDE theorem.

### 2. Make Pluecker phase operational rather than decorative

Current rung: **Impact ladder 2**, exact finite covariance and response
fixtures.

The arbitrary-phase S2 capstone proves phase-covariant thermodynamic structure,
and the equal-magnitude interferometer gives a finite phase-sensitive fixture.
The high-impact result is a gauge-invariant preparation/measurement protocol
that distinguishes two equal-`|z|` configurations and cannot be reproduced by
an ordinary inserted complex mass with the same allowed connection data.

Why a serious reader cares: this is the shortest answer to the reparametrization
objection. Without it, Pluecker origin explains where a parameter comes from but
does not yet change observable physics.

Benchmark: a two-site or ring interferometer with the same spectrum and local
magnitudes but different invariant holonomy.

Kill condition: if every allowed probability agrees after local basis changes,
demote constant and locally removable phase to bookkeeping and retain only
patched-defect or interaction claims.

### 3. Turn the `3+1` difficulty into a clean classification theorem

Current rung: **Impact ladder 2**, scoped exact no-go results.

The strict `3+1` job should end in one of two memorable forms: an exactly
unitary, finite-range, alias-free successor with a Dirac tangent, or a lower
bound proving which resource must increase beyond the minimal architecture.

Why a serious reader cares: lattice fermion doubling and causal unitary
discretization are recognized problems. A resource lower bound is publishable
even if the preferred construction fails.

Benchmark: full Brillouin-zone determinant/Floquet spectrum, not corner
sampling.

Kill condition: a construction checked only near zero momentum or a no-go that
restates the assumptions without an escape threshold does not clear the gate.

### 4. Use the quantum Klein equality theorem as infrastructure, not novelty

Current rung: **Impact ladder 2**, reusable formal information theory.

The equality theorem is mathematically substantial and correctly handles
degenerate eigenspaces without a permutation assumption. The theorem itself is
standard mathematics; its likely contribution is a clean CFC-free Lean route.
The impact upgrade is the strict noncommuting Gibbs variational principle and a
genuine pinching-channel data-processing theorem using the same API.

Why a serious reader cares: together these would make the repository a reusable
finite quantum-information foundation for dynamics and gravity claims.

Honesty gate: do not present formalization novelty as a new physical entropy
law, and do not call measurement outcome monotonicity arbitrary CPTP DPI.

### 5. Convert decomposition classification into a naturality theorem

Current rung: **Impact ladder 2**, exact mathematical classification.

The S3 theorem proves that permutation-invariant quadratic selectors form a
two-parameter family and that only one transverse coefficient affects a
fixed-total selector. This sharpens nonuniqueness. It does not yet explain why
aperture, closure, turn, and soldering are physically preferred.

The next publishable theorem must add an independently motivated axiom such as
local additivity, channel monotonicity, operational distinguishability, or an
information-geometric metric and prove either uniqueness or a residual moduli
space.

Kill condition: another symmetry that merely renames coefficient equality does
not establish physical naturalness.

## Centerpiece recommendation

Concentrate Paper D around one theorem chain:

```text
finite local unitary walk
  -> exact momentum multiplier approximation
  -> strongly continuous exact L2 group
  -> Schwartz-domain generator identity
  -> changing-lattice position-space convergence
```

This chain has the clearest route to impact ladder 3: resolving a recognized
technical obstacle. It is also the best external proof of the lab's method,
because formal verification catches normalization and domain mistakes that are
easy to gloss over in continuum-limit arguments.
