# Impact strategy: mass origins, spectral meaning, and 3+1

Date: 2026-07-20
Role: Codex Impact Strategist
Work item: `MASS-ORIGIN-001`

## External test

A skeptical researcher will not be moved by another finite identity saying a
matrix eigenvalue can be called mass. The work becomes consequential when it
does at least one of the following:

1. closes a semantic bridge that competing formalizations leave implicit;
2. proves a regulator-wide result, rather than a low-momentum sample;
3. classifies what is possible and impossible under explicit assumptions;
4. produces an operational response or observable, not only a relabeling;
5. makes a standard physical construction machine-checkable without hiding its
   supplied parameters.

## Ranked high-impact results

### 1. Full-zone HNU theorem plus changing-lattice continuum limit

This is the flagship. A local unitary `3+1` dynamics with a complete zero/pi
crossing census, global micromotion balance, stable massive gap, and a
position-space continuum theorem would turn the project from finite kinematics
into a genuine regulator proposal. Either a positive construction or a sharp
minimum-resource no-go would matter to quantum-walk, QCA, lattice-fermion, and
formalization communities.

Required before promotion:

- no unaccounted low-energy mirror;
- full Brillouin-zone, not corner-only, classification;
- local and dynamically invariant physical-sector encoding;
- explicit sampling/interpolation and ultraviolet-tail control;
- stable massive perturbation.

### 2. Positive transfer-correlation reconstruction theorem

This is the most important origin-of-mass semantic bridge. Prove that a
positive finite transfer operator, selected vacuum, and gauge-invariant
observable with nonzero overlap yield a positive exponential spectral sum and
that the large-time decay extracts exactly the visible transfer gap.

Why it matters: it states, in a kernel-checkable finite setting, when an
operator gap becomes an observable mass estimator. It also gives every later
QCD, Higgs, defect, and many-body mass claim the same acceptance test.

The theorem must include an observable-overlap witness and an orthogonality
control. A gap invisible to the chosen observable is not an extracted mass.

### 3. Scoped origin-of-mass classification with an overlap law

A mechanism matrix becomes publishable mathematics only if it fixes an
admissible action/operator class and proves exhaustiveness or supplies a
counterexample. The QCD source audit contributes a strong non-double-counting
law: composite rest mass is one Hamiltonian response, while its quark/gluon,
explicit-mass, and anomaly pieces form a subordinate scheme-locked budget.

The standout claim would be:

> Relative to a displayed Standard Model plus neutrino and finite-transfer
> action class, every admitted quadratic/rest-gap term factors through one of a
> classified set of response maps, with explicit overlap and reconstruction
> laws.

That claim is not yet proved.

### 4. Shared-Higgs-data reconstruction

This is valuable infrastructure, but not by itself a paradigm result. To avoid
looking like a conjunction of textbook formulas, it should prove a common API
and independent controls:

- the same vacuum vector enters all sectors;
- the gauge kernel is exactly the stabilizer;
- the radial direction is normal to the gauge orbit;
- the Yukawa matrix remains a separate equivariant input;
- setting one coupling to zero kills only its sector;
- setting the vacuum to zero kills all vacuum-induced responses.

Its manuscript value is precision: the project can say exactly what is unified
and exactly what remains arbitrary.

### 5. Nonabelian finite composite-mass witness

An explicit `SU(3)` gauge-invariant observable, positive transfer operator, and
correlation-decay mass estimator would remove the largest species-level gap in
the origin-of-mass story. A two-state or abelian toy no longer upgrades the
claim. The finite result need not solve continuum confinement, but it must make
the remaining limit precise.

## Best near-term claim upgrade

The cheapest externally meaningful upgrade is not "all mass is derived." It is:

> The project gives one explicit, machine-checked acceptance ladder from finite
> causal data to an observable mass response: geometric/operator gap, positive
> evolution, visible spectral support, and controlled continuum interpretation.

To earn it, land the finite transfer-correlation theorem and attach one
nondegenerate example from the shared-Higgs or HNU sector. The phrase "physical
mass" remains conditional until the continuum step is supplied.

## What to deprioritize

- additional capstones that merely conjoin existing lemmas;
- more low-momentum expansions before the full-zone HNU census closes;
- new verbal decompositions of proton mass without a scheme-locked operator;
- claims that Pluecker area predicts absolute scales;
- detailed manuscript polishing before the theorem grade changes.
