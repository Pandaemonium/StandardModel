## The honest problem

Both halves of BRIDGE-AE-001 are therefore at high risk of the lab's named
over-claim modes:

- The "canonical active-sector map" is, concretely, "restrict `Kop` to the
  2-dimensional pair sector and read off the matrix." That is precisely a
  **chosen coordinate identification** -- the exact thing the kill condition
  names. `kop_highPair/lowPair` already state the only non-hollow content
  (the block entries are `z, z̄`), and they live where they belong (supporting
  DYN-MODULAR), not as a standalone "bridge."
- The "rank or representation obstruction to full-sector equivalence" is, at
  face value, "`Kop` acts on 16-dimensional Fock space while `B_z` is 2x2." A
  bare dimension mismatch is a **hollow (non-invariant) obstruction** -- also
  named by the kill condition.

## Recommendation

**Lean toward KILL/FOLD, not build.** Concretely:

- **FOLD** the genuine content into what already holds it: the block
  identification stays as the DYN-MODULAR bridge lemmas; the shared closure
  stays as CubeLawTripotent (with its existing anti-unification disclaimer).
- **KILL** BRIDGE-AE-001 as a standalone target *unless* the owner can name a
  specific **invariant** obstruction, e.g. one of:
  1. *Grading-respecting non-isomorphism*: no `*`-algebra map intertwining the
     CAR-Fock representation generated by `Kop` with the `2x2` matrix algebra of
     `B_z` that also respects the total-number (or chirality) grading -- an
     invariant statement, not a dimension count.
  2. *Rank-as-invariant*: `Kop` as a Fock endomorphism has a basis-independent
     rank/kernel structure (image 2-dimensional, kernel 14-dimensional) whose
     interaction with the number grading forbids any number-graded extension of
     the `B_z` action. This needs the grading to make it invariant; without it,
     it is the hollow dimension count.

If neither invariant is establishable with genuine content, the honest verdict
is that "A and E share a cube-law block and nothing more canonical," which
CubeLawTripotent already says. That is a legitimate SCOPE finding, consistent
with the charter's "a kill is a first-class result."
