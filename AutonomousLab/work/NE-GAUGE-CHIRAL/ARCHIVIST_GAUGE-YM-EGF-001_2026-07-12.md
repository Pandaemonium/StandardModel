## What the 2007 proof supplies

The proof has two global stages.

1. A partition scheme turns the alternating connected-graph sum into a sum over
   admissible spanning trees. For hard-core interactions, the Penrose identity
   is exact; the traditional tree-graph inequality is the weaker fallback.
2. A monotone nonlinear map generates all planar rooted trees by iteration. Its
   expansion attaches a symmetric vertex factor `c_s / s!` at a vertex with
   `s` children. Only after summing the positive series over all planar rooted
   trees does the proof forget branch order. The multiplicity of labelings of a
   planar rooted shape is

       V! / product_v (s_v!).

   This is equation `(rel1)`. Substituting that multiplicity converts the planar
   sum `(fin.2)` into the global labeled-tree sum `(fin.3)` and then
   `(r.15.1b)`. For the Kotecky-Preiss specialization, sibling constraints are
   discarded only at the vertex-function level, producing `(r.26)` and the
   exponential map `(r.27)`.

The order of operations is load-bearing: the label multiplicity is valid for a
complete rooted-tree shape sum. It is not a bound on one chosen forest atom,
one child-size class, or one fibre of a root-deletion classifier.

The exact live residual is
`PhysicsSM/Draft/NullEdge/GateYM/PolymerKPConclusion.lean`:

- `rhs_forest_expand2` is the finite ordered-forest expansion corresponding to
  the positive planar-tree expansion before branch order is forgotten.
- `pairSum_le_expBound` is the remaining global inequality.
- `perPair_absWeight_bound`, `fiber_value_bound`, and
  `fiber_card_mul_le_factorial` are local arithmetic tools only. They cannot
  prove the target by atomwise domination.

The failed size-seven case is a mandatory regression control. For one
self-incompatible polymer and child sizes `(3,3)`, the relevant fibre has size
90 and

    90 * 2! * (3!)^2 = 6480 > 7! = 5040.

The entire child-size class also overshoots its matching right-hand class, while
the complete degree-seven coefficient remains below the complete right-hand
coefficient. Any proposed recurrence that compares matching atoms or matching
child-size classes is therefore false even if its final total happens to be
true.

## Recommended theorem decomposition

Before another Aristotle proof job, specify and test these finite lemmas.

1. `global_planar_tree_expand`: reindex the full nonnegative left-hand pair sum
   by planar rooted tree shapes, polymer labels, and symmetric vertex data. The
   equality or inequality must be over the complete finite sum.
2. `planar_label_multiplicity`: for a fixed complete planar rooted shape, prove
   the finite labeling multiplicity `V! / product_v (s_v!)`, or an equivalent
   factorial identity avoiding division in `Nat`.
3. `global_unorder_branches`: use symmetry of the vertex weights to pass from
   the full planar sum to the full labeled rooted-tree sum. Do not expose a
   fibrewise inequality in this statement.
4. `kp_vertex_map_expand`: identify the unconstrained KP vertex function with
   the finite exponential partial sum already displayed by
   `rhs_forest_expand2`.
5. `pairSum_le_expBound`: compose the preceding global statements without
   changing its hypotheses or right-hand side.

The recurrence alternative is the monotone-map version of the same argument:
define the finite truncations generated by `(r.15.2)`, prove that one iteration
adds every planar rooted tree of the next permitted depth exactly once, and use
monotonicity plus the displayed supersolution inequality. This may formalize
more cleanly than a single large reindexing theorem, but it must still sum
globally before comparing coefficients.
