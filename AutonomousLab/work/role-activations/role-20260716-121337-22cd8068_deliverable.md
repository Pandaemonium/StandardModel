## Ranked gate 1: graph-native local geometry or a no-canonicity theorem

**Objective.** Determine whether one protected core canonically supplies a
stable four-dimensional Lorentzian probe sector, up to the gauge freedom that
continuum GR actually permits.

**Present limitation.** The program has equivariant finite operators,
rank-four projector interfaces, and an actual protected-core overlap theorem,
but the projector, eigengap, Lorentzian inertia, and restricted injectivity are
still supplied. R5 asks only whether enough compatible chart candidates can be
selected; it cannot derive local geometry.

**Novel mechanism.** Apply a graph-derived symmetric probe operator on each
protected core, select an isolated four-dimensional spectral cluster by a
threshold determined from intrinsic spectral gaps rather than a target basis,
and compare its range under carrier refinement and overlap restriction. Treat
degenerate eigenspaces as gauge or as a no-canonicity certificate, never by an
arbitrary eigenvector tie-break.

**Dependency chain.** R5 growing-atlas decision -> protected-core internal
operator -> equivariant spectral cluster -> exact rank/signature -> overlap
liftability and injectivity -> pair transition.

**First cheap test.** On the six spent R4-D sprinklings, compute the complete
spectrum and multiplicities of the already-defined intrinsic probe pairing on
every nonempty beta-1.25 core. Archive the fourth/fifth gap, Lorentzian inertia,
and overlap-restriction rank without using them to tune R5. In parallel, ask
Aristotle for the finite theorem that an isolated spectral cluster is carried
equivariantly by conjugate self-adjoint operators.

**Five-year payoff.** This is the first route from bare order and counts to a
gauge-relative local tangent/coframe sector that does not insert a tetrad.

**Kill and pivot.** Kill canonical spectral selection if gap probability tends
to zero, rank or inertia is unstable, or automorphisms force inequivalent
choices. Pivot to a theorem that the graph determines only a higher-rank bundle
plus a symmetry-breaking field, and state that extra field as new ontology.

**Conventional alternative.** Causal-set d'Alembertian and tangent-space
estimators with an externally chosen mesoscopic scale and continuum embedding.

## Ranked gate 2: a genuine overlap cocycle and finite spin lift

**Objective.** Upgrade pairwise equal-image transitions into a bundle-like
object on the selected atlas nerve, then test whether its Lorentz transitions
admit a coherent spin lift.

**Present limitation.** Pair transitions are exact only after supplied local
and shared projectors satisfy intertwining, liftability, and injectivity.
Pairwise maps alone need not satisfy a triple-overlap cocycle. Existing central
sign and face-obstruction theorems start after the nerve, local lifts, and face
attachments are supplied.

**Novel mechanism.** Define restriction to literal triple protected-core
overlaps. Prove that uniqueness of common observations forces
`T_jk * T_ij = T_ik` when all three restriction maps are injective and have one
shared image. Then feed the resulting finite nerve transitions into the exact
`ZMod 2` face-defect quotient and the spiral-layer Bargmann holonomy calculus.

**Dependency chain.** Gate 1 projectors -> pair compatibility -> triple-overlap
cocycle -> metric preservation and orientation/time-orientation -> local
`SL(2,C)` lifts -> central-sign obstruction class -> refinement stability.

**First cheap test.** Formalize the common-observation triple-transition
cocycle in a standalone Mathlib package. Separately, on any R5 development
atlas that reaches target cardinality, count occupied triple overlaps and the
rank of restriction to them before fitting any Lorentz transformation.

**Five-year payoff.** A graph-derived good-cover nerve with a checked spin-lift
obstruction would turn the current finite sign algebra and chiral spiral phases
into one geometric transport object.

**Kill and pivot.** Kill the finite-rank transition route if triple-overlap
restriction generically loses rank, cocycle residuals stay order one, or the
required transition dimension grows with density. Pivot to a groupoid or
sheaf of variable-rank observations rather than calling the result a tetrad
bundle.

**Conventional alternative.** Supply a tetrad and spin structure as continuum
background data, using the graph only as a discretization.

## Ranked gate 3: one refinement family for curvature, matter, and dynamics

**Objective.** Put the existing conditional G4-G8 results on one graph-derived
refinement family and test the Einstein equation as a coefficient-matched
continuum statement rather than as several compatible finite identities.

**Present limitation.** The project separately has nonzero holonomy-curvature
limits, Bianchi contractions, a finite Dirac-square architecture, coframe
responses with density/pressure/flux, FLRW variations, and weak-field constant
normalization. Their geometric inputs and action coefficients are supplied,
and no theorem identifies them on one family.

**Novel mechanism.** Once Gates 1-2 produce metric and spin transport, freeze a
single nested atlas sequence. On it compare operator Bochner curvature,
connection curvature, loop holonomy, and the curvature block of the Dirac
square. Define the matter response by arbitrary compactly supported coframe
variations on the same cells. Only after those agree should an interval-count
action coefficient be matched to the Einstein-Hilbert and weak-field
normalizations.

**Dependency chain.** Gates 1-2 -> graph-derived areas and shrinking loops ->
principal-symbol and curvature convergence -> local coframe variation ->
Noether/Bianchi conservation -> Einstein-Hilbert coefficient -> Newtonian and
cosmological held-out controls.

**First cheap test.** Build one finite shared-data schema that requires every
G4-G7 estimator to reference the same chart, overlap, area, coframe, transport,
and measure identifiers. Failure to populate a field is recorded as a missing
bridge rather than replaced by a fixture.

**Five-year payoff.** This is the point at which the framework could derive a
controlled GR limit with constants and make regulator-dependent deviations
testable.

**Kill and pivot.** Kill the GR interpretation if the three curvature
estimators converge to different tensors, the matter response is not local or
conserved, or the required Einstein coupling is channel-dependent. Retain the
finite algebra as a non-GR causal operator model.

**Conventional alternative.** Regge calculus, causal dynamical triangulations,
or causal-set effective actions where the gravitational action is specified at
the microscopic level and tested for the same continuum controls.

1. The intrinsic probe operator has a stable isolated rank-four sector rather
   than a symmetry-protected degeneracy of larger or varying rank.
2. Protected-core overlaps are large enough for injective rank-four
   restriction while sparse enough to avoid universal-apex trapping.
3. Pairwise graph-derived sectors admit one finite-dimensional common image on
   triple overlaps and remain compatible under refinement.
4. The scale fixed by counts agrees with metric volume and loop area, leaving no
   unfixed Weyl mode.
5. One local matter variation and one graph action produce a universal coupling
   rather than channel-specific effective constants.
