Date: 2026-07-13
Role: Codex Visionary

Work item: QCA-3PLUS1-001

## Plain-language objective

Stop asking only how to manufacture one Weyl-like mode.  Ask where every
construction places the compensating degree of freedom required by locality,
unitarity, topology, and anomaly consistency.  A viable 3+1 architecture must
make that compensator explicit and explain why it is absent from the intended
low-energy observations without deleting it by definition.

## Present limitation

The closed three-dimensional one-particle lattice target combines several
requirements that are individually natural but jointly obstructive.  Existing
no-go results already rule out the broad spin-blind schedule class and show
that global finite transport traces cancel.  The exact HNU endpoint escapes
the scalar collapse, but its winding, real-space null realization, and physical
boundary sector remain separate gates.

## Novel synthesis: a compensator conservation interface

The routes suggest a reusable interface rather than a single favorite model.
For each finite architecture define:

1. a declared target projector;
2. a compensator projector;
3. a conserved total signed charge or exact finite cancellation theorem;
4. a localization/energy scale separating target and compensator;
5. an observable algebra on which the compensator becomes inaccessible;
6. a limit theorem showing that inaccessibility persists as the regulator is
   removed.

The scientific claim would not be that the compensator disappears.  It would
be that it is forced into a controlled sector whose influence on specified
observables is quantitatively bounded.  This interface can compare Floquet,
domain-wall, mirror-gapping, and Wilson/overlap constructions without
conflating their invariants.

## Dependency chain

1. Complete the exact HNU winding and real-space-symbol jobs.
2. Complete the domain-wall finite kernel/gap job.
3. Complete the `3-4-5-0` many-body finite gap or obtain its sharp no-go.
4. Complete the anomaly-moment versus unweighted-index ledger.
5. Define a common `TargetCompensatorSplit` finite API only after steps 1-4
   identify the genuinely shared predicates.
6. Prove one nontrivial observable-decoupling bound in two distinct routes.
7. Compare scaling behavior numerically before funding a common continuum
   theorem.

## First cheap tests

- HNU: verify whether every real-space substep has an all-moving realization;
  a held-fixed projector branch is an immediate ontology mismatch.
- Domain wall: reject any kernel caused by a disconnected zero row.
- Mirror gapping: reject diagonal chemical potentials and assumed spectra.
- Anomaly ledger: test whether vanishing charge moments imply vanishing
  unweighted boundary count; an explicit counterexample kills the naive bridge.
- Cross-route: compute the smallest finite observable whose target expectation
  changes when the compensator is removed by hand.  If no such observable can
  be stated, the split is probably definitional.

## Five-year payoff

Success would yield a constructive explanation for why chiral matter can look
unpaired at accessible energies while the exact microscopic theory remains
local, unitary, and globally balanced.  The result would be stronger than one
lattice model: it would classify the admissible locations of compensating
degrees of freedom and identify which location is compatible with the
null-information ontology and the Standard Model anomaly ledger.

## Kill conditions and pivots

- Kill the strict null-edge realization if every faithful HNU or domain-wall
  construction requires stationary primitive branches or nonlocal substeps.
- Kill isolated-Floquet priority if `W = 1` fails or no boundary observable is
  protected.
- Kill symmetric mirror gapping as the primary route if no local interaction
  maintains a volume-uniform gap without symmetry breaking.
- Kill the proposed common interface if the routes share only an abstract
  direct-sum decomposition and no common decoupling theorem.
- Pivot to Wilson/overlap or algebraic-QFT reconstruction if those conventional
  controls meet the evidence gates with fewer unsupported assumptions.

## Recommendation

Do not choose a winner before the four active Aristotle jobs return.  Require
each route to fill the same compensator ledger.  Fund the first route that
proves both a nonzero target invariant and a quantitative compensator-
decoupling statement; theorem count alone should not decide the architecture.
