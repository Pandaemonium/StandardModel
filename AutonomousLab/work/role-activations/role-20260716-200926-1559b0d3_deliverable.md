## Portfolio verdict

The gravity program has crossed one real threshold and reached one decisive
fork. The corrected causal-operator pairing can carry exact Lorentzian inertia
on a concrete order-defined finite carrier, and that witness now reaches the
selected-sector API. This is a finite existence theorem, not yet a universal
frame selector. The old whole-carrier and raw polynomial-selector routes are
excluded away from their narrow witnesses, while the first larger-carrier
spectroscopy run found no isolated top-four cluster.

The next architecture should therefore stop asking one scalar operator to
provide four undifferentiated coordinate modes. Causal order already singles
out a time orientation. The highest-information successor is a derived
`1+3` split:

```text
one order-native causal-time functional
+ three order-native spatial modes on a mesoscopic causal neighborhood
= a candidate local rank-four probe sector.
```

The corrected pairing then supplies the Lorentzian form on that sector;
overlap maps supply candidate local Lorentz gauge transformations; and the
matter action, including scalar/Higgs edge differences and local Yukawa
blocks, supplies the source only after variation with respect to the same
reconstructed frame.

## Ranked decisive gates

### Gate 1: order-native local `1+3` sector

**Horizon:** next quarter. **Rank:** 1.

**Objective.** Construct, on a preregistered class of manifoldlike causal
carriers, one canonical time probe and three canonical spatial probes whose
corrected-pairing Gram form has persistent `(+---)` inertia.

**Present limitation.** The five-event production witness proves existence but
does not select a sector on larger carriers. Whole-carrier rank four is blocked
by cardinality. Direct retarded polynomial projectors are trivial. Exploratory
top-spectrum data show broad quasi-degeneracy rather than a four-mode gap.

**Novel mechanism.** Use the existing endpoint-contrast causal-time functional
for the time direction. Build an order-only mesoscopic spatial neighborhood
using causal-set spatial-distance machinery, then extract three nonconstant
spatial harmonic or heat-kernel coordinates. This respects the Malament split:
order supplies conformal causality, while count density and neighborhood scale
supply the metric scale still owed by the construction.

**Dependency chain.** Order-only neighborhood -> count-derived mesoscopic scale
-> canonical spatial graph or kernel -> stable three-mode subspace -> direct-sum
with causal time -> corrected-pairing inertia -> overlap injectivity.

**First cheap tests.** First formalize the exact sign profile of one coherent
mode per interval layer. For the active four-layer coefficients it is expected
to give split `(2,2)`, which would kill that tempting S2 shortcut before a large
run. In parallel, run a small preregistered sprinkling experiment comparing
three selectors: top-four corrected spectrum, layer-coherent modes, and the
`1+3` time-plus-spatial-harmonic sector. Require permutation equivariance,
rank four, a stable three-dimensional spatial cluster, `(+---)` inertia, and
held-out refinement persistence.

**Five-year payoff.** A successful gate gives a genuinely derived finite tetrad
sector and a reusable local-frame API for gravity, Dirac propagation, gauge
transport, and stress-energy variation.

**Kill condition and pivot.** Kill the selector if no mesoscopic schedule gives
a stable three-dimensional spatial sector and persistent Lorentzian inertia
without target coordinates or target metric data. Pivot to decorated
tetrad/spin-frame data and state explicitly that bare order reconstructs only
the conformal class.

**Conventional alternative.** A supplied tetrad on a causal set, Regge complex,
or discrete exterior-calculus mesh.

### Gate 2: overlap connection and curvature consistency

**Horizon:** one to two years. **Rank:** 2.

**Objective.** Turn overlapping selected sectors into a gauge-covariant local
Lorentz connection whose holonomy and contractions converge to continuum
connection and curvature controls.

**Present limitation.** Exact overlap restriction, component characters,
spin-lift boundaries, and finite curvature algebra exist separately. No theorem
yet shows that selected frames persist under refinement or that overlap
transitions converge to Levi-Civita transport.

**Novel mechanism.** Restrict probes to overlaps, solve a Lorentzian analogue of
orthogonal Procrustes alignment, and compose transitions around protected-core
loops. Use connection-Laplacian/vector-diffusion methods only as a Riemannian
control and numerical template; the Lorentzian indefinite problem needs its own
stability theorem. Compare the resulting holonomy contractions against both
Benincasa-Dowker scalar curvature and continuum constant-curvature controls.

**Dependency chain.** Gate 1 selector -> overlap rank/injectivity -> canonical
component choice -> spin lift where available -> loop holonomy -> curvature
contraction -> refinement and gauge-covariance theorem.

**First cheap test.** On flat and constant-curvature sprinklings, preregister
vanishing/nonvanishing holonomy observables, gauge-conjugacy invariants, density
scaling, and a negative control with randomly rotated local frames. Require the
same mesoscopic schedule to pass flatness and curvature tests.

**Five-year payoff.** This is the bridge from a Lorentzian vector space at each
carrier to an actual curved geometry. It also provides the spin and gauge
parallel transport needed by the spiral, chiral, and continuum programs.

**Kill condition and pivot.** Kill the intrinsic connection claim if overlap
alignment is nonunique at generic carriers, if holonomy depends on arbitrary
basis choices beyond conjugacy, or if no shared scaling window converges on
flat and curved controls. Pivot to supplied discrete connection data and retain
only the finite algebraic identities.

**Conventional alternative.** Regge deficit angles, discrete Cartan geometry,
or discrete exterior calculus with a supplied coframe and connection.

### Gate 3: variational closure with matter

**Horizon:** five-year decisive gate. **Rank:** 3.

**Objective.** Derive both sides of a local gravitational field equation from
one finite action on one reconstructed geometry, with a discrete Noether or
Bianchi identity and a controlled GR limit.

**Present limitation.** Exact scalar stress-response, FLRW conditional
identities, super-Dirac square terms, and finite curvature quantities are not
yet one action. Scalar budgets do not determine a stress tensor, and naming a
quadratic curvature expression does not make it the Einstein tensor.

**Novel mechanism.** Vary a shared selected coframe and overlap connection in a
finite action containing: causal/null kinetic terms; gauge holonomy terms;
vertex scalar fields with covariant null-edge differences; local Higgs/Yukawa
blocks; a scalar potential; and a curvature action. The Higgs vacuum is a
vertex-local internal reference section, not a moving particle. Higgs
excitations propagate through the edge kinetic term, while Yukawa chirality
flips remain local zero-order vertices. The physical source is the full
coframe variation of all these terms.

**Dependency chain.** Gates 1 and 2 -> finite invariant measure and scale ->
matter action -> coframe/connection variation -> symmetric source and
conservation identity -> gravity variation -> weak-field and FLRW controls ->
continuum convergence with constants fixed independently.

**First cheap test.** Use one homogeneous finite cell and one inhomogeneous
scalar/Higgs-gradient cell. Demand that lapse, scale, and shift variations of
the same action reproduce energy density, pressure, and momentum flux; then
check that the gravity side has a matching identity without inserting the
target stress tensor. Track every input parameter, especially `G`, `Lambda`,
the Higgs vacuum value, quartic coupling, and Yukawa maps.

**Five-year payoff.** Passing this gate would turn the program from finite
Lorentzian kinematics into a candidate gravitational theory with matter. It is
also where a genuinely new parameter relation or held-out prediction must
appear; recovery by fitted inputs alone does not satisfy the charter.

**Kill condition and pivot.** Kill the derived-GR claim if target metric,
stress tensor, connection, or physical constants must be supplied in the same
form later claimed as outputs; if the finite conservation identity fails; or if
different matter sectors require incompatible scale maps. Pivot to effective
field theory on a supplied causal geometry and preserve the impossibility map.

**Conventional alternative.** Causal-set effective field theory on an embedded
or supplied geometry, Regge calculus with lattice matter, or continuum GR with
discrete numerical regularization.
