## Executive judgment

The global retarded count-band shell should not remain the proposed local
carrier. Stage A3c shows that its support grows strongly with the infrared
diamond volume while every local scale is fixed, and
`RetardedShellInfraredNoGo.lean` constructs arbitrary shell cardinality at one
fixed minimal count band and nonzero abundance threshold. Pending the required
Claude review, this branch should be recorded as a useful impossibility result.

The best successor is not another global source-interiority rule. It is an
order-equivariant ensemble of compact Alexandrov germs, using the existing
`AlexandrovGermPacking`, `AlexandrovGermPairEstimand`, and
`AlexandrovGermInternalOperator` APIs. This changes the GR program from trying
to choose one preferred local frame to proving that a complete ensemble of
finite order-derived carriers has stable local statistics, compatible overlaps,
and eventually a rank-four Lorentzian low-complexity sector.

Three ranked gates should control the next five years of this architecture.
They are ordered by dependency and information gain, not by conceptual size.

## Gate 1: compact-germ carrier and refinement stability

**Horizon allocation:** 50 percent, next-quarter tractable.  
**Rank:** 1, immediate dependency gate.

**Plain-language objective.** Derive a genuinely local finite carrier from the
bare causal order without choosing a preferred embedding, endpoint pair, or
frame. When no single carrier is canonical, average over the complete
order-equivariant eligible-germ ensemble.

**Present limitation.** The global shell is infrared divergent in the continuum
and admits arbitrary cardinality in a fixed local count band at finite size.
The existing compact-germ formalization proves internality and relabeling
covariance, but it does not yet prove that refinements contain enough protected
marks, that overlap maps are stable, or that a rank-four probe sector is typical
rather than cardinality-selected.

**Proposed mechanism.** For each mark, use all eligible nested Alexandrov
brackets whose closed carriers satisfy preregistered intrinsic count-depth
conditions. Evaluate retarded support only in each induced finite order. Treat
the complete ensemble, rather than one selected bracket, as the covariant
object. Compare nested carriers by restriction maps and overlapping carriers by
their common induced suborder. The first target is carrier compatibility, not a
tetrad.

**Dependency chain.** Independent disposition of `GRAV-ORDER-SUPPORT-001` ->
fresh compact-germ work item -> relabeling-covariant eligible-bracket ensemble
-> protected-mark and rank-capable coverage -> nested restriction stability ->
overlap cocycle and inertia stability -> only then generalized-cluster probes,
tetrad reconstruction, and curvature.

**First cheap experiment.** On fresh Poisson causal orders and finite controls,
enumerate every eligible compact bracket around randomized marks. At fixed local
count bands, report: protected-mark coverage; carrier-size distribution; the
fraction with at least four retarded-visible independent probes; nested-carrier
principal-angle drift; overlap disagreement; and dependence on outer diamond
volume. Include chains, layered orders, and duplicated-disjoint-germ controls.
No coordinates may be used after relation construction, and no eigensolver is
funded until the order-side coverage gate passes.

**First theorem rung.** Define the nested-carrier restriction map against the
existing `ClosedCarrier` and `inducedOrder` APIs. Prove that internal interval
counts and the protected cutoff agree on the common causally convex subcarrier,
and that the complete eligible-bracket statistic is invariant under finite-order
isomorphism. This can be typechecked now and should carry a standard axiom guard.

**Five-year payoff.** A passing gate supplies the missing local carrier on which
scale reconstruction, low-complexity rank, Lorentzian inertia, tetrad/spin
compatibility, curvature convergence, and matter coupling can be stated without
silently importing an embedding. A failing gate maps a precise impossibility
frontier for bare-order local geometry.

**Kill condition and pivot.** Kill the bare-order compact-carrier route if every
order-equivariant bracket ensemble either has vanishing protected coverage,
inherits outer-volume dependence, or lacks stable restrictions and overlaps
without embedding-tuned anchors. Pivot explicitly to one of two honest theories:
an ensemble-level nonlocal geometry, or a decorated causal order with supplied
tetrad/scale data. Do not rename supplied decorations as emergent.

**Conventional alternative.** Benchmark against a causal-set d'Alembertian with
an explicit nonlocality scale and against Regge/discrete-exterior-calculus cells.
The null-edge route earns leverage only if order-derived compact germs fix data
that these controls must supply or if it produces a sharper no-go.

## Gate 2: Lorentz-covariant operator law in distribution

**Horizon allocation:** 30 percent, one-to-two-year architecture.  
**Rank:** 2, begins only after Gate 1 has a nondegenerate carrier ensemble.

**Plain-language objective.** Recover Lorentz symmetry at the law and continuum
operator levels even though no finite nonzero directional support can be
invariant under the noncompact Lorentz group.

**Present limitation.** `L0-BOOST-001` rules out finite invariant support for an
explicit rational boost. `L0-DIST-001` records exact Lorentz invariance of the
Poisson sprinkling law as nearest literature, but no theorem yet transports the
decorated compact-germ operator law, scale estimator, or spin data. The
changing-lattice continuum results do not prove Lorentz restoration.

**Proposed mechanism.** Make the random compact-germ ensemble the symmetry
object. Require the joint law of causal order, eligible carriers, normalized
operator, and overlap data to be equivariant under transformed sprinklings.
Compose finite relabeling covariance with a configuration-space cylinder-event
invariance theorem. Then prove convergence of operator moments or resolvents to
a Lorentz-covariant continuum Dirac/causal operator on a displayed domain.

**Dependency chain.** Gate 1 carrier ensemble -> honest point-process and
cylinder-event API -> measurable decorated-operator statistic -> finite
relabeling covariance -> distributional Lorentz covariance -> changing-cell
operator convergence from `NE-CONTINUUM` -> spin/tetrad compatibility on
overlaps.

**First cheap experiment.** Generate boost-related sprinkling ensembles before
discarding coordinates, then compare held-out distributions of intrinsic carrier
statistics and normalized operator moments. Preregister a two-sample tolerance
and run a regular-lattice negative control. This is only a falsifier for preferred
frame leakage, not a proof of Lorentz invariance.

**First theorem rung.** Prove that the finite complete-germ observable commutes
with order isomorphisms and that its pushforward distribution is unchanged under
an explicitly supplied measure-preserving configuration equivalence. Keep the
actual Poisson/Lorentz cylinder theorem as a separate analytic item.

**Five-year payoff.** A passing gate replaces the impossible demand for one
finite invariant frame by an exact symmetry statement about the physical random
law and its continuum operator. It also gives a clean interface between the
Lorentz and continuum programs.

**Kill condition and pivot.** Kill Lorentz recovery from the proposed bare-order
law if operator or scale statistics retain a preferred frame under refinement,
or if equivariant decorations require selecting finite directions by external
coordinates. Pivot to explicit Lorentz-breaking effective theory with bounded
violations, or to a continuum-first covariant regulator. Do not call small
numerical anisotropy exact invariance.

**Conventional alternative.** Run the same continuum and anisotropy tests for a
hypercubic Wilson/domain-wall regulator and for established causal-set
operators. The comparison target is symmetry recovery rate and required input
structure, not shared notation.

## Gate 3: physical Einstein response with constants

**Horizon allocation:** 20 percent, genuine moonshot.  
**Rank:** 3, forbidden as a headline until Gates 1 and 2 pass.

**Plain-language objective.** Derive a conserved physical stress-energy source
and an Einstein-limit response, including the normalization of Newton's
constant and the declared cosmological term, from null-edge dynamics rather
than inserting the target metric equation.

**Present limitation.** The landed entropy data-processing and strong
subadditivity theorems are generic finite information inequalities. They do not
select a gravitational coarse-graining channel, define physical stress-energy,
produce curvature, or yield Einstein dynamics. The current GR note still owes
scale, tetrad/spin structure, curvature convergence, and a matter observable
dictionary.

**Proposed mechanism.** Define a local action or response functional on compact
germ ensembles from intrinsic causal-operator and matter-state data. Derive its
finite variation, prove an exact discrete conservation/Bianchi identity, and
show that its weak-field expectation converges to a curvature term plus a
separately derived matter response. Constants must arise from a displayed
count-to-unit transmutation law and held-out calibration, not dimensional
matching.

**Dependency chain.** Gates 1-2 -> physical scale estimator -> rank-four
Lorentzian carrier and compatible tetrad/spin atlas -> curvature estimator with
convergence and variance control -> matter Hamiltonian/state and operational
stress-energy -> finite variational identity -> conservation -> weak-field and
cosmological controls -> Einstein-limit theorem with constants.

**First cheap experiment.** On compact causal diamonds with preregistered flat,
weak conformal, and weak anisotropic perturbations, measure the response of the
intrinsic germ action and a separately defined matter observable. Fit no tensor
equation. Test whether one normalization predicts held-out perturbations and
whether the response obeys an approximate discrete conservation identity.

**First theorem rung.** Before any continuum claim, prove an exact finite
variation identity and conservation statement for a nonconstant, nonvacuous
germ action with an explicit witness. Audit that the proposed stress-energy
conjugate is not encoded in the hypotheses.

**Five-year payoff.** A passing gate is the first result that would justify
calling the program a gravity theory rather than a causal-operator
reconstruction. A failing gate still isolates whether the missing ingredient is
scale, local geometry, matter conjugacy, or dynamics.

**Kill condition and pivot.** Kill the claimed GR derivation if the continuum
metric, physical stress tensor, Newton normalization, or cosmological response
must be supplied as target data; if the finite variation is hollow telescoping;
or if weak-field coefficients cannot predict held-out perturbations. Pivot to a
kinematic causal reconstruction with explicit gravitational dynamics added as
an independent postulate.

**Conventional alternative.** Compare with the Benincasa-Dowker causal-set
action and Regge gravity coupled to standard lattice matter. The null-edge
proposal must derive at least one normalization, conservation law, or response
that the conventional control does not simply receive as input.

## Cross-project opportunity map

1. `NE-GRAVITY-SCALE` should consume the existing compact-germ APIs rather than
   build another carrier abstraction. Its first new reusable interface is a
   nested/overlap carrier map.
2. `NE-LORENTZ` should own the configuration-space and distributional covariance
   theorem. The finite-support no-go becomes a design constraint, not a reason
   to search for one invariant finite frame.
3. `NE-CONTINUUM` should export operator-convergence hypotheses and domains in a
   carrier-agnostic form. GR may compose them only after the compact-germ random
   operator has been identified with the continuum multiplier.
4. `NE-DYNAMICS` supplies a matter-state and modular-flow control, but generic
   entropy monotonicity must not be promoted to gravity. The shared interface is
   an explicit finite state/channel and its response conjugate.
5. The conventional control program should replay every gate with established
   causal-set or lattice operators using the same held-out metrics.

## Assumptions most at risk

- Bare order may determine only a conformal causal class; scale may remain
  irreducible decoration or law-level statistical data.
- An order-equivariant complete carrier ensemble may exist while no stable
  rank-four local sector or overlap atlas exists.
- Poisson law invariance may not survive operator decorations, conditioning,
  finite-volume windows, or scale selection.
- Operator convergence may hold while curvature estimators have unacceptable
  variance or nonlocal tails.
- A finite variational identity may be mathematically exact but physically
  hollow unless the matter conjugate and constants have independent witnesses.
- A weak-field fit may merely reconstruct the supplied perturbation family and
  fail out of sample.
