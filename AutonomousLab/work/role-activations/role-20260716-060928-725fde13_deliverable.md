## Strongest honest lede

> A retarded source shell selected only by fixed causal-interval count bands is
> not a local finite carrier: its availability grows with the infrared diamond
> volume at fixed local scales, and a kernel-checked family realizes arbitrary
> shell cardinality while preserving the same minimal local interval count and
> nonzero two-sided abundance threshold.

This is the strongest defensible sentence after the independent review. It is a
finite obstruction plus a numerical boundary control. It is not a derivation of
dimension, Lorentz signature, tetrads, curvature, Einstein dynamics, or physical
stress-energy.

## Actual impact rung

**Current rung: 2, reusable theorem and diagnostic tool.**

`RetardedShellInfraredNoGo.lean` gives a reusable exact counterexample family,
and the sparse A3c experiment gives a deterministic way to distinguish local
support from finite-boundary truncation. The pair is stronger than a failed
simulation because it explains why apparent support improves with outer volume.

The result does not yet reach rung 3, resolution of a recognized technical
obstacle. The finite theorem is intentionally narrow, and the nearest-work
comparison has not yet shown whether this precise interval-count shell proposal
has appeared in the causal-set literature. It can reach rung 3 by proving a
broader classification or by showing that the compact-germ replacement passes a
recognized causal-set locality and covariance benchmark.

## Nearest-work comparison

The recognized neighboring problem is the noncompactness of Lorentzian
constant-proper-time surfaces and the resulting infinite-valency/nonlocality
pressure in causal-set constructions. Established causal-set operators handle
this with explicit nonlocality scales, layer sums, finite spacetime regions, or
law-level averaging. The present result should be compared with that machinery,
not advertised as a new discovery that Lorentzian hyperboloids have infinite
volume.

The candidate contribution is narrower and executable:

1. It isolates one proposed graph-internal locality rule: a retarded shell
   defined by bounded open-interval counts plus two-sided interior abundance.
2. It supplies a fixed-density four-volume ladder showing that rank-capable
   support rises strongly with the infrared cutoff while local inputs stay
   fixed.
3. It supplies an exact finite family with arbitrary shell cardinality under one
   fixed count band, closing the inference that cardinality or eventual rank
   certifies locality.
4. It identifies a concrete replacement architecture already present in the
   repository: compact causally convex Alexandrov carriers and their complete
   equivariant packing ensemble.

Before external use, the Archivist must verify primary sources for causal-set
infinite valency, nonlocal d'Alembertian scales, finite-volume truncation, and
Lorentz-invariant sprinkling. Until then, the comparison is a research map, not
a novelty claim.

## Target community

The primary technical audience is causal-set and discrete-Lorentzian-geometry
researchers interested in local operator construction, finite-region effects,
and covariant neighborhood selection. A secondary audience is formalized
mathematics and machine-checked mathematical physics, because the finite no-go
is guarded and replayable in Lean.

The result is not ready for a general-relativity phenomenology audience. It has
no weak-field observable, gravitational constant, cosmological prediction, or
physical source law.

## Decisive result needed for excitement

The smallest result that would raise the work to rung 3 is a two-branch theorem
and experiment package:

> Any shell predicate whose membership depends only on a fixed finite set of
> pairwise interval-count bands and fixed local abundance thresholds either
> admits arbitrarily large finite shell multiplicity, or requires an explicitly
> compact carrier input; the complete compact Alexandrov-germ ensemble then
> passes preregistered outer-volume, refinement, and overlap controls.

The first clause would turn the current witness family into a structural
classification. The second would show that the proposed repair is not merely a
change of words. A weaker but still useful rung-3 result is a sharp theorem for
the exact count-band API now used by the manuscript, together with an
independently reproduced compact-germ successor benchmark.

The compact-germ benchmark should require all of the following on held-out
seeds:

- stable protected-mark coverage at fixed local inputs as outer volume grows;
- nonzero median rank-capable support without coordinate-selected marks;
- bounded nested-carrier drift and overlap disagreement;
- a chain/layered-order negative control;
- comparison with an established causal-set operator using its declared
  nonlocality scale.

No generalized eigensolver result is exciting if the eligible carrier itself
fails these controls.

## Centerpiece versus infrastructure

**Centerpiece.** The exact arbitrary-cardinality no-go, the fixed-density A3c
volume ladder, and the split conclusion that boundary truncation is real but
cannot certify locality.

**Infrastructure.** Sparse interval counting, dense-equivalence tests, JSON
archival, schedule-threshold algebra, import wiring, and standard axiom guards.
These make the centerpiece reproducible but should not dominate the title or
abstract.

**Future centerpiece.** A passing compact-germ refinement/overlap theorem and
benchmark. Tetrad, spin, curvature, and Einstein-response claims remain future
dependencies rather than paper sections padded around the current no-go.
