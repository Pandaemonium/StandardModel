## The real-space HNU step operator (from `HNURealSpaceCore` / `HNURealSpaceBridge`)

- **State space:** `Pos = Site L` x spin, where axes 1,2 have `L` sites, axis 3
  has `2L` sites (finer, so the axis-3 half-step `k3/2` is a nearest-neighbor
  hop). Amplitude `psi : Pos -> (Fin 2 -> C)`.
- **Primitive (`condShift P Q sigma`):** `psi(x) |-> P .(psi (sigma x)) + Q .(psi
  x)` - the `P`-sector reads from the shifted neighbor `sigma x`, the `Q`-sector
  stays. With `P = Pplus s`, `Q = Pminus s` for a Pauli `s`; `sigma` a
  nearest-neighbor lattice shift. (Verbatim: `HNURealSpaceCore.condShift`.)
- **Step operator `U` (8 substeps, `schedule`):** the real-space realization of
  `HNUExactCore.endpoint`, one `condShift` per factor of
  `Um s1(k0) . Um s3(k2/2) . Um s2(k1) . Up s3(k2/2) . Up s1(k0) . Um s3(k2/2) .
  Up s2(k1) . Up s3(k2/2)`, with the axis shifts `shPlus/Minus{1,2,3}` and spin
  projectors `Pplus/Pminus(sigma_j)` as fixed in `HNURealSpaceBridge`
  (`substep_*_symbol`, `schedule_symbol`). On a plane wave, `U` acts by the exact
  `endpoint(kR k)` symbol - this is the periodic (closed) benchmark.

## The half-space modification (the ONE convention to pin - and the risk)

Open exactly one axis; keep the other two periodic. Two defensible choices, both
to be RUN (they answer different physics):

- **Choice A - open axis 3 (the drive/half-step axis).** Most physical for the
  AFAI framing (the boundary is transverse to the anomalous half-step). Replace
  `shPlus3`/`shMinus3` (cyclic on `Fin (2L)`) by the TRUNCATED shift on
  `Fin (2L)` - exactly the `HalfSpaceDefectIndex.unilateral` boundary rule: the
  `P`-sector hop off the last/first site is absorbed (no wrap). This is the direct
  analogue that reduces to the landed `+1` in the pure-shift limit.
- **Choice B - open axis 1 (a spatial axis).** Boundary parallel to the drive; the
  edge mode, if any, is a spatial edge state. Same truncation rule on `shPlus1`.

Boundary rule (pinned, matching `HalfSpaceDefectIndex`): at the open end, the
`P`-sector of `condShift` that would hop past the boundary is DROPPED (absorbing),
so the truncated step `U_open` is a genuine non-unitary corner - its
`U_open^H U_open - U_open U_open^H` is the boundary defect. THIS DROP RULE IS THE
DECISIVE CONVENTION: an alternative (reflecting/unitary boundary) would test a
different physical edge and must be labelled separately, not silently substituted.

## Observable and discriminator (reuse `halfspace_window_defect.py` harness)

- Compute `D = U_open^H U_open - U_open U_open^H` (over exact rationals extended to
  `Q[i]`, since the HNU symbol is complex; or exact cyclotomic for the phases).
- `Q_window(K, N)` = trace of `D` over the near-boundary window (sites 0..K on the
  open axis, summed over the transverse periodic axes and spin).
- **Discriminator (the Gate-1 fork):**
  * `Q_window` stabilizes to a single additive `+1` (per spin/channel), size-
    independent above `N > K` => the HNU boundary carries ONE unremovable chiral
    defect with a quiet interior => single-edge-Weyl route SUPPORTED.
  * `Q_window = 0`, or an in-window PAIRED `+1,-1` (two opposite defects), or
    size-dependent => doubling / no isolated edge Weyl => single-edge-Weyl route
    FALSIFIED (decisive kill, honorable charter Sec2.2 outcome).

## Controls (mandatory, before trusting the verdict)

1. **Periodic control:** with all axes cyclic, `D = 0` exactly (U unitary) - if
   not, the step-operator reproduction is wrong; stop.
2. **Pure-shift reduction:** set spin projectors to `P = 1, Q = 0` (or a single
   axis-3 substep) and confirm `Q_window` reproduces the landed
   `HalfSpaceDefectIndex` `+1` - if not, the truncation rule is mis-encoded.
3. **Transverse-momentum resolution:** diagonalize in the periodic transverse
   momenta; the boundary defect should be an integer summed over transverse modes,
   not a continuous drift.
