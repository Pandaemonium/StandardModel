## The mathematics (oracle-verified exactly before this submission)

Work on Fock (Fin 4) = (Finset (Fin 4) -> Complex) with the module's
pairForward, pairBackward, pairKick, fockInner, lowPair, highPair.
Define the Hermitian generator and the exact one-parameter family:

  Kop (z : Complex) : Fock (Fin 4) -> Fock (Fin 4) :=
    fun psi => z . (pairForward psi) + (conj z) . (pairBackward psi)
  (equivalently by the module's _apply lemmas:
   (Kop z psi) lowPair = z * psi highPair;
   (Kop z psi) highPair = conj z * psi lowPair; else 0.)

  Uop (c s : Real) (z : Complex) (m : Real) : Fock -> Fock :=
    fun psi S =>
      if S = lowPair then c * psi lowPair - Complex.I * s * (z/m) * psi highPair
      else if S = highPair then c * psi highPair - Complex.I * s * (conj z / m) * psi lowPair
      else psi S
  (the closed form of exp(-i alpha Kop) at cos = c, sin = s, |z| = m).

Theorem ladder (hypotheses: hm : (m:Complex) ^ 2 = z * conj z or the
real form m^2 = normSq z with m > 0; hcs : c^2 + s^2 = 1 where needed):

T1 generator_hermitian: fockInner (Kop z psi) phi =
   fockInner psi (Kop z phi)  (Hermitian w.r.t. the module's inner).
T2 generator_cubed: Kop z (Kop z (Kop z psi)) = (z * conj z) . Kop z psi
   (pointwise scalar action; K^3 = |z|^2 K - the finite closure that
   makes the exponential exact).
T3 group_law: Uop c1 s1 z m (Uop c2 s2 z m psi) =
   Uop (c1*c2 - s1*s2) (s1*c2 + c1*s2) z m psi, given hm and the two
   circle relations (angle addition at coefficient level - pure algebra,
   no trig functions, no analysis).
T4 unitary: fockInner (Uop c s z m psi) (Uop c s z m phi) =
   fockInner psi phi, given hm, hcs.
T5 DERIVATION (the headline): Uop 0 1 z m = pairKick (-Complex.I * z / m)
   given hm, m > 0 - i.e. the module's phase kick with unit coefficient
   u = -i z/|z| IS the quarter-period member of the exact unitary group
   generated by the Hermitian quartic word; and |u| = 1
   (unit_coefficient lemma: (-I*z/m) * conj (-I*z/m) = 1 under hm).
