## Audited outcome

Development is `INADMISSIBLE`, with no chosen cap. All 18 result-bearing
beta-`1.25` cells have the sole reason `random-feasible control shortfall`;
each of the six cap/density summaries is exactly three inadmissible, zero pass,
zero fail. The run was resource-clean: no timeout, memory event, candidate
ceiling, or tripwire failure occurred. Held-out seed `2026071613` was never
constructed and is retired unconsumed in the 210-byte retirement record.

The useful descriptive results are:

1. The beta-`1.25` complete-family intersection is empty on all six fresh
   sprinklings, confirming R4-D out of sample.
2. Near-apex hubs remain strong, at `92.3%` to `99.5%` of each family.
3. Smaller cores unlock the first observed growth beyond the multiplicity cap.
   At `N=12000`, every greedy cell exceeds its cap; cap `12` reaches `17-19`
   charts against `K_N=21`, with no full selected intersection. One `N=6000`,
   cap-`12` cell reaches `13` charts.
4. The five random-priority controls trap harder and never reach `K_N`
   (maximum `17`), so the frozen W2 rule correctly makes the result
   inadmissible rather than a selector failure.
5. The beta-`1.00` negative control remains decision-inert and behaves as
   preregistered: the `N=6000` complete-family certificates are nonempty in
   all three fresh sprinklings.

## Claim boundary

This rules out the frozen R5 combination of complete count-band family,
bulk-first constrained greedy, caps `{5,8,12}`, and
`K_N=ceil(2 N^(1/4))` reaching its target at beta `1.25` on these seeds. It
also refutes the hypothesis that merely emptying the complete-family common
intersection suffices for this selector to reach `K_N`.

It does not rule out a graph-native atlas in general. The observed `17-19` of
`21` growth makes the remaining obstruction a margin rather than the R4 wall;
fractional-dual certificates, redesigned candidate families, and other growth
laws remain open. Rank four, tetrads, spin structures, curvature,
stress-energy, and Einstein dynamics remain closed and untouched.
