# Q8 — Second quantization on a finite Krein complex: verdicts, constructions, and the exact location of infinity

## 0. Verdicts first

**V0 (the loudest flag, answering the success criterion's escape clause).** The anticipated failure — "second quantization forces infinite dimensionality somewhere" — does **not** occur in the matter sector. Fermionic Fock space over a finite one-particle space is the exterior algebra, dimension 2^n. The entire chain — Fock functor, Gupta–Bleuler quotient, BRST cohomology, S-matrix, path sum, canonical four-fermion interactions, one RG step — is finite and kernel-shaped. Infinity enters at exactly two doors, both nameable with precision: **(i) dynamical bosons** (Sym Fock over any nonzero space is infinite-dimensional; L²(G) per link is infinite-dimensional for continuous G), and **(ii) the refinement limit**, where the finite invariant to control is the Hilbert–Schmidt norm in the Shale–Stinespring implementability condition. Section 6 makes both precise. Until decorations become dynamical, your QFT is a quasi-free fermion theory and it is finite, period.

**V1.** The Fock functor is Λ (exterior algebra), it lifts Krein structure functorially, and it **commutes with the Gupta–Bleuler quotient**: the radical of the second-quantized form is the ideal generated by the one-particle radical, and Λ(V′)/rad ≅ Λ(V′/N) isometrically. This is pure finite multilinear algebra — a first-rate Lean target (T-F2 below).

**V2.** Finite Kugo–Ojima is a four-line theorem: for a J-self-adjoint nilpotent Q on a nondegenerate finite Krein space, the induced form on H(Q) = ker Q / im Q is **automatically nondegenerate**, because (ker Q)^{⊥J} = im Q. Quartet completeness is *free* in finite dimension. Only **positivity** is open — and it is literally your Q1 crux, transported unchanged. Second quantization adds no new obstruction.

**V3.** Unitarity is an exact finite theorem with three hypotheses: J-unitarity of the one-particle evolution, constraint preservation, quotient positivity (= Q1). Pseudo-unitarity S^# S = 1 holds *unconditionally*. One presupposition of your question 2 is malformed: there is no interaction picture with t → ∞ on a finite complex; the correct S-object is the total (boundary-to-boundary) evolution, in the general-boundary sense.

**V4 (flagging a false presupposition in question 3).** The path-sum equality is **provable** at the finite 1+1 level (word expansion + functoriality of Λ + Cauchy–Binet + Lindström–Gessel–Viennot). But the crossing-sign structure is the *planar shadow* of the exterior algebra, not the origin of statistics. Antisymmetry is carried by the functor Λ; in ≥2+1 the crossing parity of zigzag families is not even well-defined while the determinant side remains perfectly well-defined. Spin-statistics does not "enter through crossings"; I propose below where it *can* enter as a pre-registrable finite conjecture (positivity selects Λ over Sym).

**V5 (repairing a presupposition in question 4).** "D² cross terms" cannot generate 4-point functions as stated: dΓ(D²) is still a one-body (quadratic) operator. The correct and better statement: **squaring does not commute with second quantization**, and the discrepancy is a canonical two-body operator,

```
dGamma(D)^2  =  dGamma(D^2)  +  2 dGamma_2( Lambda^2 D )
```

whose pair kernel is the **wedge of the carrier** — resonating exactly with Layer K (mass = pairwise wedges; interaction = pairwise wedge of D). Quartic couplings are Gram-squares of the linear couplings: NJL from turn², Thirring-type from transport², a mixed derivative-Yukawa channel. Gauge *self*-coupling is **not** generated by the fermionic square; it must be added (plaquette = the Q_C slot) or induced by the fermion determinant (Kazakov–Migdal mechanism).

**V6.** One coarse-graining step on the quasi-free theory is the **Schur complement** of the carrier. Krein-self-adjointness and chirality-oddness are RG-stable; per-edge null nilpotency is **not** — coarse-graining null structure generically produces effective non-null (mass-shaped) terms. Your central physical thesis ("mass is what trapped light looks like from far away") reappears as a finite RG fact.

## 1. The Fock functor on Krein data

**Construction (FINITE IDENTITY targets throughout).** Let (V, J) be a finite Krein space, h(x,y) = ⟨x, J y⟩, dim V = n, inertia (p, q), κ = q. Define Fock(V) := ΛV = ⊕_k Λ^k V, dim 2^n. Creation a†(f) = f ∧ (·); annihilation a(f) = antiderivation; CAR {a(f), a†(g)} = ⟨f, g⟩ with respect to the *auxiliary Hilbert* product. The Krein structure lifts as ΛJ = ⊕_k Λ^k J, and the covariant (Krein) adjoint is

```
a^#(f) := (Lambda J) a(f)† (Lambda J) = a†(J f),     {a(f), a^#(g)} = h-pairing.
```

**T-F1 (Fock functor, KERNEL-shaped).** (a) ΛJ is a fundamental symmetry on ΛV; the negative sector of Λ^k V has dimension Σ_{m odd} C(p, k−m) C(q, m); in particular Fock(V) is a genuine Krein space iff κ ≥ 1. (b) Λ is functorial and multiplicative: Λ(UW) = Λ(U)Λ(W); if U is J-unitary then Λ(U) is ΛJ-unitary. (c) Fields built from a, a^# are η-Hermitian in the Kugo–Ojima sense with η = ΛJ. Comment: unlike CCR, indefinite metric costs nothing representation-theoretically for CAR in finite dimension — the algebra is M_{2^n}(C) either way; the Krein structure only redesignates the adjoint. There is no analog of the non-Fock pathology of indefinite-metric bosons.

**T-F2 (Fock commutes with Gupta–Bleuler — the theorem you asked for).** Let (W, h) be a finite Hermitian-form space with radical N (in the application W = V′, the constraint kernel). Then:

1. rad(Λh) = I(N) := span{ n ∧ ω : n ∈ N, ω ∈ ΛW } — the two-sided ideal generated by N;
2. the canonical algebra surjection ΛW → Λ(W/N) has kernel exactly I(N) and is an **isometry** of induced forms: (ΛW)/rad(Λh) ≅ Λ(W/N);
3. hence: h ≥ 0 with kernel N implies Λh ≥ 0 with kernel I(N); h > 0 on W/N implies the physical Fock form is positive definite. **H_phys = Fock(V′/N), exactly.**

Proof skeleton (all finite): Gram determinants — Λh on wedge bases is det[h(u_i, v_j)]; a row vanishes if any u_i ∈ N, giving rad ⊇ I(N); for the converse choose a splitting W = W₀ ⊕ N with h nondegenerate on W₀ and use invertibility of compound matrices (Cauchy–Binet: the k-th compound of an invertible Gram matrix is invertible). Note the pleasant strengthening: **no positivity is needed** for (1)–(2); the radical of an exterior power of a form is the ideal of the radical, full stop. This is the cleanest possible answer to "does second quantization commute with the quotient": *yes, at the level of forms, spaces, and (by functoriality, see T-U1) dynamics*. Grade: theorem-shape T, M-target of moderate effort (Mathlib has `ExteriorAlgebra`/`exteriorPower`; the Gram-form API on exterior powers will need building).

**T-F3 (finite Kugo–Ojima; quartet completeness is automatic).** Let Q be nilpotent (Q² = 0) and J-self-adjoint (Q^# = Q; the sign Q^# = ±Q is immaterial) on nondegenerate finite (Ṽ, J̃). Then im Q ⊆ ker Q, and

```
rad( h restricted to ker Q ) = ker Q ∩ (ker Q)^{perp_J} = ker Q ∩ im Q^# = im Q,
```

using the finite-dimensional identity (ker Q)^{⊥J} = im Q^# (proof: ⟨x, Q^# y⟩_J = ⟨Qx, y⟩_J plus double-perp). Hence the induced form on H(Q) = ker Q / im Q is **canonically nondegenerate**, and any J̃-unitary K commuting with Q descends to a form-unitary on H(Q). Consequence, stated loudly: *in finite dimension the entire quartet mechanism — the statement that zero-norm states in ker Q are exactly the Q-exact ones — is a free theorem.* What Kugo–Ojima had to argue via asymptotic completeness is automatic here. The **only** thing that is not automatic is positivity of the nondegenerate induced form, and that is precisely Q1. Grade: T, four-line proof, superb first Lean target.

**T-F4 (quartets without ghosts; the abelian packaging).** Since h is nondegenerate on the ambient V and N ⊂ V′ is isotropic, a Witt-type decomposition exists: V = W₀ ⊕ (N ⊕ N*) with h nondegenerate on W₀ and N ⊕ N* hyperbolic (dual pairing). The pairs (n_i, n_i*) are your longitudinal/scalar doublets — the "parents and daughters" — and V′/N ≅ W₀-part. In the abelian case Route A (no ghosts) fully suffices, and I recommend it as the Lean path. The ghost route (Q = Σ c_i χ_i on V ⊗ Λ[c]) is the Koszul complex of the commuting constraints; its known finite pitfall is cohomology doubling from the ghost-number pairing (H⁰ pairs with H^r, so the form restricted to ghost number zero alone degenerates), standardly repaired by a nonminimal sector. Grade: T|H for the Witt packaging (H: h nondegenerate on V — you have it); STRATEGY for the ghost dressing; the nonabelian case (closure slot as constraint algebra) is a genuine C, pre-register it with T-F3 as the invariant that must survive.

## 2. Dynamics and unitarity

**The malformed piece, replaced.** On a finite complex there are no Møller operators and no t → ∞. The honest S-object is the **total evolution**: the J-unitary map U (product of layer transfer matrices, or exp(iD) if you prefer a single-generator picture — note (e^{iD})^# = e^{−iD^#} = e^{−iD}, so Krein-self-adjointness of D *is* J-unitarity of the flow, a one-line M-target) from past-boundary decorated data to future-boundary data. This is precisely Oeckl's general-boundary stance, and your retarded-transport structure is what orders it. "Free = transport, interaction = turn" remains available as a *factorization* of U (Section 3's word expansion is exactly the Dyson series of that split, exact and finite), but unitarity should be stated for U itself.

**T-U1 (physical unitarity, the exact finite theorem).** Hypotheses:

- (H1) U is J-unitary on (V, J) — equivalently its generator is Krein-self-adjoint;
- (H2) U V′ ⊆ V′ — the dynamics preserves the constraint kernel ("gauge invariance of S");
- (H3) the induced form h̄ on V′/N is positive definite (the Q1 prize).

Then: (a) U N ⊆ N automatically (N = V′ ∩ V′^{⊥J} and J-isometries preserve both constituents); (b) the induced [U] on V′/N is h̄-unitary, hence a genuine unitary of a finite Hilbert space; (c) Λ[U] is unitary on Fock(V′/N) and, via T-F2, coincides with the operator induced by ΛU on ΛV′/rad. **"S is unitary on the physical quotient" holds with exactly (H1)–(H3) and nothing else.**

**Where Krein threatens, and what exactly saves it.** J-unitaries form the noncompact group U(p, q); eigenvalues come in (λ, 1/λ̄) pairs and can leave the unit circle, so norms can blow up — but only in directions that mix negative-norm modes. What saves unitarity is the *pair* (H2)+(H3): the constraint subspace confines the dynamics, the quotient kills the radical, and positivity compactifies the residual group to U(r). Meanwhile the unconditioned statement — worth stating because it is the finite skeleton of Kugo–Ojima's "pseudo-unitarity + quartet decoupling ⇒ physical unitarity" — is:

```
S^{#} S = 1 on Fock(V) always (given H1);   physical unitarity = pseudo-unitarity + T-F2/T-F3 quotient.
```

**Positive energy is a different axiom, and here is its exact finite role.** No positive-energy condition is needed for T-U1. It is needed for the *vacuum and particle interpretation*: choose the polarization V′/N = P₊ ⊕ P₋ by the sign of the induced generator's spectrum on the physical quotient (permitted language there, since (H3) makes it a genuine self-adjoint operator; everywhere else I keep to forms per your Section 6 discipline) — this is the finite Sorkin–Johnston prescription, canonical on a finite causal complex, no asymptotics required, and continuous with your existing SJ usage in the P9 branch. Zero modes obstruct canonicity: the vacuum is 2^{dim ker}-fold degenerate, and by your McKean–Singer index theorem the index-protected part of that degeneracy **cannot be lifted by any decoration**. Forced vacuum degeneracy from the index is a feature: it is the finite seed of symmetry breaking / 't Hooft-vertex physics. Grade: T-U1 is T, Lean-ready; the SJ-vacuum packaging is STRATEGY; the degeneracy interpretation is labeled interpretation.

## 3. The path sum: prove, with one demolition

**T-P1 (word expansion; the checkerboard is a binomial identity).** For A, B ∈ End(V): (A+B)^N = Σ over words in {A, B}^N. With A = chirality-preserving null transport and B = Γφ (turn), each word *is* a zigzag configuration with weight (transports along the word) · φ^{#turns}. The one-particle checkerboard sum is this identity, exactly, at finite N. KERNEL-trivial, but it anchors everything: it is also the exact finite Dyson series for the split of Section 2.

**T-P2 (second-quantized amplitudes are determinants).** By functoriality and Cauchy–Binet, the k-particle amplitude is

```
< f_1 ^ ... ^ f_k , Lambda^k(T^N) g_1 ^ ... ^ g_k >  =  det [ <f_i, T^N g_j> ].
```

Composing with T-P1: **k-particle amplitudes = determinants of zigzag sums.** T (classical), M-target of moderate effort.

**T-P3 (crossing signs; Lindström–Gessel–Viennot).** On the 1+1 checkerboard DAG with boundary points ordered along spacelike slices, the determinant expands as the signed sum over *families* of zigzags with sign (−1)^{#pairwise crossings}, and by LGV this equals the unsigned sum over **vertex-disjoint** families. Pauli exclusion appears at the path level as non-intersection. T (Lindström 1973; Gessel–Viennot 1985; probabilistic ancestor Karlin–McGregor 1959); the LGV involution proof is finite and Lean-able.

**The demolition.** Your phrasing "is that where spin-statistics enters a null-edge world?" presupposes the crossing sign is the *mechanism*. It is not. In ≥2+1, crossing parity of path families is not well-defined, yet the determinant side of T-P2 is untouched. Antisymmetry is carried by the **choice of the functor Λ**; the crossing-sign picture is its planar shadow, a 1+1 luxury. Where spin-statistics *can* genuinely enter your program is as a positivity selection: **C-Q8-SS (pre-register):** on the checkerboard complex with the Krein carrier, symmetric (bosonic) quantization of the odd carrier admits no dynamics-compatible constraint pair (V′, N) with positive quotient, while Λ does — i.e., statistics is forced by the same crux that forces everything else (this is the finite skeleton of Pauli 1940). Kill-condition: exhibit a Sym-quantized checkerboard with a positive invariant quotient.

**Smallest checkable instance (explicit).** V = C^L ⊗ C² with L = 4 sites, open boundaries; T = (right-shift on right-movers) ⊕ (left-shift on left-movers) + m·(flip); N = 2 time steps; two particles injected as right-movers at sites 1, 2; read out both boundary configurations. Left side: det of the 2×2 matrix of one-particle T² amplitudes (each entry a sum of ≤ 4 zigzags, by T-P1). Right side: the ≤ 16 zigzag pairs with crossing signs; LGV reduces to the vertex-disjoint families. Treat m as a formal variable: the equality is an identity in Q[m], decidable by kernel computation (`Polynomial`/`decide`-adjacent). This is a one-afternoon Lean exhibit and I would do it before T-P3 in general form.

## 4. Interactions beyond Yukawa: the canonical quartic

**T-I1 (squaring does not commute with dΓ; the discrepancy is the interaction).** With dΓ(D) = Σ a†_i D_{ij} a_j, the CAR give the exact finite identity

```
dGamma(D)^2 = dGamma(D^2) + 2 dGamma_2( Lambda^2 D ),
```

where dΓ₂(K) is the two-body second quantization of the pair operator K on Λ²V, and Λ²D (u∧v) = Du ∧ Dv. (Sanity check, dim V = 2, D = diag(d₁, d₂), state e₁∧e₂: (d₁+d₂)² = (d₁²+d₂²) + 2 d₁d₂. ✓) FINITE IDENTITY, KERNEL-shaped, basis-free statement available.

Consequence, joined to Move 1: second-quantizing the *square* rather than squaring the second quantization yields

```
4 dGamma(D)^2 = dGamma(Q_A) + dGamma(Q_C) + 4 dGamma(Q_T) + 4 dGamma(E) + 8 dGamma_2(Lambda^2 D)
```

— the four one-particle channels **plus one canonical two-body vertex whose pair kernel is the wedge of the carrier**. Note the resonance with Layer K, and it is not decorative: mass is Σ|ψ_i ∧ ψ_j|² on pairs of edges; the preferred interaction is D∧D on pairs of particles. Expanding D = D₀ + Γφ gives the channel content: Λ²D₀ (transport⊗transport: current–current, Thirring-shaped), D₀ ∧ Γφ (mixed derivative-Yukawa), Λ²(Γφ) (turn⊗turn: NJL-shaped scalar-channel four-fermion). **Qualitative coupling relations, which is what you asked for:** every quartic coupling is a Gram product of linear couplings — four-fermion strengths go as (Yukawa)², (transport)², and the geometric cross term, with ratios fixed by the complex's Gram data, *no new constants*. That NJL — the paradigm of dynamical mass generation — appears as turn² is thematically exactly right for "mass from turning" and worth a pre-registered numerical probe. Grade: identity FINITE; physics reading C/STRATEGY.

**Gauge self-coupling: not generated; two honest origins.** The fermionic square cannot produce boson self-interaction because transports are decorations, not operators on ΛV. Either (i) **add** the plaquette action — which is precisely reading Q_C as the gauge action, where your strong-coupling area-law asset already lives; or (ii) **induce** it: integrate out fermions; the hopping/character expansion of det(carrier) on a gauged complex produces Re tr U_P at leading nontrivial order (Sakharov-style induction; lattice ancestor: Kazakov–Migdal). Smallest kernel target: a single square plaquette, expand det(1 + t · holonomy-hopping) in t and exhibit the O(t⁴) plaquette term as an exact polynomial identity. Grade: C with a computable check.

A complexity remark your oracle-first methodology should exploit: the quasi-free layer is exactly *fermionic linear optics / matchgates* — classically computable in polynomial time (Terhal–DiVincenzo; Valiant) — so oracles scale; the canonical quartic is precisely what breaks matchgate simulability, i.e., where genuine quantum complexity enters your world.

## 5. Renormalization as coarse-graining

**T-R1 (one RG step = Schur complement; exact and finite).** Split V = V_c ⊕ V_f (coarse/fine, J and Γ block-diagonal for the split). Grassmann/Berezin integration of the fine variables is exact:

```
det D = det(D_ff) · det( D_cc − D_cf D_ff^{-1} D_fc ),      D_eff = Schur complement,
```

and the conditional Berezin integral of exp(ψ̄ D ψ) over fine modes yields exp(ψ̄_c D_eff ψ_c) up to det(D_ff). T (classical); Mathlib already has block-determinant infrastructure; Berezin needs building but is finite. Companion fact for states: restricting a quasi-free state to the coarse CAR subalgebra compresses the covariance (T, standard) — integration is Schur, restriction is compression; state both, they are different operations.

**What is RG-stable (small theorems, big physics).** If D^# = D and ΓDΓ = −D with block-compatible J, Γ, then D_eff is again Krein-self-adjoint and chirality-odd (odd·odd⁻¹·odd = odd; the # computation closes similarly). T|H, Lean-quick. What is **not** stable: per-edge null nilpotency. The Schur complement generically produces effective terms that are not sums of nilpotent edge pieces — coarse-grained light propagates as if massive. State it as the finite theorem it is and then say the sentence: *the program's thesis is an RG fact — mass terms are what null microstructure Schur-complements to.* Also note the induced longer-range couplings: the RG-stable object is the operator class {finite, Krein-s.a., Γ-odd}, not the null-edge normal form; your continuum question (Q6 category) should be posed in that class.

**C-R2 (pre-registered one-loop matching, 1+1 Yukawa toy).** Make the toy *fully finite* by fermionizing the scalar: put an Ising/Z₂ spin on corners (Jordan–Wigner keeps the total space at dim 2^{#modes}), coupling as the turn amplitude. Claim: one decimation layer (Schur on alternate slices) reproduces the standard lattice one-loop flow of (m, g) at O(g²). Kill-condition: explicit mismatch on the 2-layer, L = 4 lattice — computable exactly on both sides. Anchor for expectations: rigorous fermionic RG *exists and converges* (Gross–Neveu via convergent expansions, Gawędzki–Kupiainen; Gram–Hadamard bounds; Salmhofer) — fermion-only finiteness is exactly the regime where RG rigor is a solved art. Divergence lives with bosons; see Section 6.

## 6. The handoff map: exactly where infinity enters

1. **Dynamical bosons.** Promoting φ or the transports to quantum fields requires Sym Fock (infinite-dimensional for even one mode) or L²(G) per link (infinite-dimensional for continuous G). Finite escapes, each with a price: finite gauge groups; quantum-group truncations (Turaev–Viro / Levin–Wen style); and in 1+1, exact fermionization (bosonization), which is why the Yukawa toy above stays finite. Pre-register which escape the program takes; "background decorations integrated with a finite measure" is also honest but makes bosons classical.
2. **Refinement-limit implementability.** In the continuum, Bogoliubov transformations are implementable on Fock space iff the particle–antiparticle mixing block is Hilbert–Schmidt (Shale–Stinespring; Ruijsenaars). Every finite complex trivially satisfies this; the continuum gate is a **refinement-uniform HS bound** — a sequence of finite numbers your oracles can compute today. Bonus: implementability failure is the rigorous face of anomalies (Schwinger terms; Carey–Ruijsenaars), so HS-norm growth along refinements is a *finite anomaly diagnostic*, and your chiral-index calculus is its kernel-level shadow.
3. **Nothing else.** Vacuum choice (SJ polarization) is finite; unitarity is finite; interactions are finite; one RG step is finite.
