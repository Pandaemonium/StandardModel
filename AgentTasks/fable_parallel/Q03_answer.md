## Q1. Coleman–Mandula

**What the theorem says, precisely.** (T) Coleman–Mandula 1967: let G be a connected symmetry group of the S-matrix of a relativistic QFT in ≥ 2+1 spacetime dimensions such that (H1) G contains a subgroup locally isomorphic to the Poincaré group; (H2) particle finiteness: for any M > 0, finitely many particle types below mass M; (H3) scattering occurs: elastic amplitudes are analytic in s, t near the physical region and nonvanishing at almost all energies; (H4) a technical distribution-kernel assumption on the generators. Then G is locally isomorphic to (Poincaré) × (internal), with all conserved charges beyond P_mu and M_munu transforming as Lorentz *scalars*. Haag–Łopuszański–Sohnius 1975 (T): admitting graded Lie algebras, the unique extension is supersymmetry, with fermionic charges in spinor representations satisfying {Q, Qbar} ~ P. Known escapes: d = 1+1 (no scattering angle; integrable towers of higher-spin charges), massless/conformal case (Poincaré extends to conformal, still factorized), spontaneously broken charges, gauge redundancies (not S-matrix symmetries), quantum-group/nonlocal charges (not Lie algebras of the assumed type).

**Does it apply to the finite carrier?** No, and not marginally: on a finite complex there are no asymptotic states, no S-matrix, no Poincaré subgroup, no analyticity. CM is a theorem about a structure that does not yet exist in your program. Your 1+1 checkerboard bridge is doubly exempt (d = 2).

**Does it apply to the continuum limit?** If the program succeeds — an emergent S-matrix with emergent Poincaré invariance, some analyticity, particle finiteness — then yes, fully, and here is the crucial reframing: **CM constrains the output, not the mechanism.** What CM forbids is a symmetry whose conserved charges carry both Lorentz indices and internal charges, acting on asymptotic multiparticle states. Your claim is an *operator identity*: one carrier whose Weitzenböck decomposition has four channels. That is a statement about the organization of the dynamics, precisely analogous to the Lichnerowicz formula in Connes' spectral Standard Model — one Dirac operator whose fluctuations contain gravity, gauge, and Higgs — which is fully CM-compliant, as are Kaluza–Klein and string theory. CM killed *symmetry-based* unification of spacetime and internal quantum numbers; it is the historical reason decomposition-based unification is the surviving strategy, not a threat to it.

Where it does bite: (a) if the decorated complex possesses exact symmetries mixing soldering data (the spacetime-to-be) with color/turn decorations, those symmetries must, in the limit, either break, act trivially on asymptotic states, or be gauge redundancies. This is a *checkable derivation target*, and proving it would be a selling point: the program would *explain* the Poincaré × internal factorization as forced structure rather than assume it. (b) The hypothesis most at risk in your limit is (H3), analyticity — which for a theory defined by refinement of finite Krein complexes is downstream of unitarity + locality + causality of the physical sector. That is open problem #1 again. Every road leads there.

**Is the Γ-grading secretly the SUSY escape?** It is honestly, non-secretly, worldline SUSY. An odd operator D on a Z_2-graded space (which requires the bookkeeping hypothesis that phi is Γ-odd so that Γφ anticommutes with Γ — display this; your block form [[0, D_-],[D_+, 0]] presupposes it) is the algebraic skeleton of N = 1 supersymmetric quantum mechanics: supercharge Q = D, Hamiltonian H = D², and your finite McKean–Singer family is *literally* the statement that ind(D) is the Witten index (Witten 1982), invariant under every deformation of potential and transport. That is why index protection works. But HLS spacetime SUSY requires the odd charges to be spinors of the *emergent* SL(2,C) with {Q, Qbar} ~ P; your Q is the Dirac operator itself, and SUSY QM on a target never implies target-space SUSY (the entire SUSY proof of the index theorem — Alvarez-Gaumé; Friedan–Windey — runs on this without the target being supersymmetric). Verdict: no escape needed, no escape provided, and the grading is correctly spent on index protection.

## Q2. Weinberg–Witten

**Precise statement.** (T) WW 1980, second theorem: in a theory with exact Lorentz invariance admitting a conserved, Lorentz-covariant stress tensor T^munu (as an operator with the standard normalization <p|T^munu|p> → p^mu p^nu), there are no massless one-particle states of helicity |h| > 1. (First theorem: covariant conserved current J^mu forbids charged massless |h| > 1/2.) Proof mechanism: the collinear-limit matrix element <p', h|T^munu|p, h> must carry little-group helicity weight 2h, but a rank-2 tensor supplies at most 2; the charge normalization forbids the matrix element from vanishing. GR itself evades: diffeomorphism invariance means gravitational energy is a pseudo-tensor (gauge-variant); no covariant conserved T containing the graviton's own charge exists.

**Loophole taxonomy and occupancy.** (i) *Composite graviton in the same Hilbert space as a covariant T*: forbidden — this is the theorem's actual kill zone (graviton as photon bound state, etc.). (ii) *No covariant conserved T*: GR's own loophole; any diff-invariant limit. (iii) *No exact fundamental Lorentz invariance*: lattices, analog gravity, condensed matter; the theorem is silent, but you pay in Lorentz-violation phenomenology. (iv) *Holography*: the covariant T lives in a theory whose spectrum contains no massless spin-2; the graviton lives elsewhere (bulk). (v) *Gapped spin-2*: trivially allowed. (vi) *Sakharov induced gravity*: the metric is fundamental (kinematics given), only its dynamics is loop-induced; no composite graviton.

Your construction occupies **(iii) at the finite level, and must land in (ii) in the limit.** The finite carrier has no Lorentz group — SL(2,C) is emergent via the celestial structure — so WW says nothing about the complex. But (iii) alone is a Pyrrhic loophole: if the limit restores Lorentz invariance to experimental accuracy while possessing a covariant conserved T, the WW argument reapplies to the effective theory and forces the E-mode to be gapped or absent. Therefore the load-bearing statement is: **the refinement limit of the E-slot must generate its own gauge redundancy** (discrete diffeomorphisms / local translations, natively teleparallel: local translations are exactly the gauge group of TEGR), such that on the physical quotient no covariant conserved stress tensor exists. Note how this is not an extra wish: it is the same structure your Gupta–Bleuler strategy already needs — the constraint kernel V' and radical N are where the diffeo redundancy must live. WW converts "positivity via constraint quotient" from a strategy into an obligation with a kill-condition: *if the quotient theory possesses a local covariant conserved T^munu together with a gapless spin-2 excitation, the program's gravity claim is dead as stated, and the honest retreat is a gapped E-mode.*

**The sharper modern cousin — include it in the ledger.** Marolf (PRL 2015): emergent gravity requires kinematic nonlocality — a gravitating theory's Hamiltonian is a boundary term on-shell (gravitational Gauss law), while a local nongravitational theory on a fixed kinematic background has a bulk Hamiltonian; no amount of dynamics converts the latter into the former. Your carrier's "Hamiltonian" is a sum over edges/plaquettes — bulk-local on the complex. So either the limit is not gravity in Marolf's sense, or the constraint structure must reorganize the generator into a boundary term on the physical sector. Your P9/harmonic-cohomology work on the diamond bookkeeping cochain is the natural place this shows up: harmonic/cohomological pieces are exactly "boundary-supported" data. I would elevate this to a named conjecture: *on-shell, the generator of refinement-time translations on V'/N is cohomologically supported.* If you can't get that, the E-slot is producing analog gravity (emergent metric kinematics, no Einstein dynamics — the explicitly documented limitation of the Barceló–Liberati–Visser program), and you should say so early.

**Positioning against precedents.** Sakharov induced gravity is your closest dynamical relative — the E-slot induces the gravitational action — but you are better positioned than Sakharov in one respect: the soldering *is* a dynamical decoration, so the metric kinematics is not assumed. You are worse positioned in another: Sakharov never had to produce diffeomorphism redundancy from a fixed complex. Jacobson 1995 (Einstein equation as equation of state) is the model of a claim-shape that survived: it derives field equations without claiming a graviton in a fundamental Hilbert space. Keep that shape available as the fallback statement of the E-slot result.

## Q3. Nielsen–Ninomiya

**The finite-complex statement you asked for.** (T|H, cover form) Let X be a 2-complex with a free Z^d (or Z_N^d) action with finite quotient, and let the carrier D commute with translations, with (H1) finite hopping range (locality), (H2) translation invariance (Bloch fibers over a Brillouin torus T^d), (H3) Hilbert Hermiticity structure (anti-self-adjoint symbol; equivalently gamma_5-Hermiticity), (H4) exact chirality {Γ, D} = 0. Then the signed count of gapless Weyl points on T^d vanishes: the symbol is a real vector field on the torus, its zeros carry Poincaré–Hopf indices = chiralities, and their sum is chi(T^d) = 0 (Friedan 1982 is the rigorous version). Corollary in your language (ladder item L4, kernel-checkable): **on any periodic complex, dim M_+ = dim M_- fiberwise** (translation gives the isomorphism), so ind(D) = 0. Hence your unbalanced (2,1) exhibit with ind = 1 is only possible because the complex is *not* a periodic cover — it is a defect. This is exactly Kaplan's 1992 domain-wall mechanism in embryo: net chirality lives where translation invariance fails (boundaries, interfaces, defects), and the "extra dimension" of domain-wall fermions is one systematic way to manufacture the imbalance. Your index-protection family is the finite, decoration-independent form of that statement. Slogan-grade: *on a finite complex, doubling is not violated; it is localized into the cochain structure. ind(D) = dim M_+ − dim M_- says a net Weyl fermion is a property of the complex, not of the carrier.*

**Which hypothesis does each asset delete?** The four hypotheses form a square, and every known escape deletes exactly one: SLAC deletes (H1) locality; domain-wall deletes (H2) (defect/boundary); Wilson and overlap delete (H4) — Wilson brutally, Ginsparg–Wilson/overlap surgically, replacing exact chirality with Lüscher's exact deformed symmetry {Γ, D} = a D Γ D, which retains an index via ind = (a/2) Tr(Γ D). **Retardedness deletes (H3)** — and nothing else. Decompose the forward transport: nabla^fwd = nabla^sym + (1/2) L_e, where L_e is the edge second-difference. Because the extra piece enters *with* the Clifford factor c(alpha_e), it anticommutes with Γ: exact chirality is untouched. What breaks is anti-Hermiticity: the forward difference's Hilbert adjoint is the backward difference. So retardedness is **neither a Wilson term** (Hermitian, scalar, chirality-breaking) **nor an overlap kernel** (Hermitian in the gamma_5 sense, chirality-deforming) **but a third mechanism: exact chirality retained, Hermiticity abandoned** — and your Krein structure is the only reason this can be principled rather than sick, because Hilbert Hermiticity is replaced by J-self-adjointness (your # slot). The folklore objection to one-sided lattice derivatives — loss of unitarity/reflection positivity — is precisely your open problem #1 wearing a lattice hat. **The NN escape and the positivity crux are the same problem.** This is the audit's single most important sentence for Q3.

**Why retardedness is weaker than deletion (F2, the mathematics).** On a translation cover, the Weyl-block symbol is sigma(k) = Sum_e z_e(k) c(alpha_e) with winding coefficients z_e(k) = exp(i k·e) − 1. A one-line extension of your KERNEL Layer-K identity (bilinearity of det in rank-one summands; ladder L1) gives, for the 2x2 block,

  det sigma(k) = Sum_{e<f} z_e(k) z_f(k) |psi_e wedge psi_f|^2 .

At z = 1 this is your mass identity; here it is the **dispersion polynomial**, and doublers are its extra zeros. Three consequences. (a) *d = 1 works*: z(k) = exp(ik) − 1 vanishes only at k = 0; the winding phase is why the checkerboard is clean. (b) *d ≥ 2 generically fails*: with exactly two edge directions, det sigma = z_1 z_2 |w_12|^2 vanishes on the entire union of the codimension-1 tori {k·e_1 in 2 pi Z} and {k·e_2 in 2 pi Z} — *lines* of gapless modes, worse than point doublers. Richer soldering shrinks the zero set (zeros = intersection of the complex quadric Sum z_e z_f |w_ef|^2 = 0 with the torus image of k → z(k), generically codimension 2), but existence is a model-by-model finite question — a system of polynomial-trigonometric equations, decidable, pre-registrable. (c) *The topological obstruction survives non-Hermiticity in signed form* (ladder L6): det sigma is a C-valued function on T^d; the preimage of a generic ray from 0 to infinity is a (d−1)-chain whose boundary is the zero set Z; hence [Z] = 0 in H_{d−2}(T^d; Z). NN's Poincaré–Hopf pairing (real vector field, index sum = 0) is replaced by an argument-principle pairing (complex function, zero set null-homologous). **Retardedness cannot delete the doubling partners; it can only move them off the unitary shell** — into decaying/growing modes with the wrong residue structure, invisible as asymptotic states of a retarded evolution. Which means: when you pass to the physical sector V'/N to restore unitarity, you must check the quotient does not drag them back on-shell. Also note the near-origin expansion is a gift: z_e z_f = −(k·e)(k·f) + O(k^3) is *real* to leading order, giving det sigma(k) = −g(k,k) + O(k^3) with g^{mu nu} = Sum_{e<f} e^{(mu} f^{nu)} |psi_e wedge psi_f|^2 — the **emergent inverse metric as wedge-weighted soldering Gram**, an exact finite identity (ladder L2) that ties the doubling analysis, Layer K, and the E-slot's metric into one formula.

**The honest final form.** NN in its deepest modern reading is 't Hooft anomaly matching: a single Weyl fermion with anomalous symmetry cannot arise from a local, symmetric, translation-invariant, unitary finite system with onsite symmetry; the anomaly must be carried by a defect/boundary (domain wall — your imbalance), a non-onsite symmetry (Ginsparg–Wilson — your Lüscher asset), or removed entirely (symmetric mass generation for anomaly-free content — your flagged Gate-C1 escape; note SMG requires anomaly-free representations, which is precisely where the 16 of Spin(10) enters, adjacent to your long-standing interests). Recommended posture for the charter: **GW descent is the load-bearing evasion; retardedness is demoted to the physical constraint selecting forward data and shaping which zeros are on-shell.** Kill-condition: if the physical quotient is Hermitian, local, translation-covariant with exact (undeformed) chirality and claims ind ≠ 0, something is wrong by the cover lemma.

## Q4. Connes' axioms

**The comparison object.** A finite real even spectral triple (A, H, D; J_R, gamma) satisfies: grading (gamma² = 1, [gamma, A] = 0, {gamma, D} = 0); real structure J_R (antilinear, with KO-dimension sign table for J_R² = ±1, J_R D = ±D J_R, J_R gamma = ±gamma J_R); zeroth-order ([a, J_R b J_R^{-1}] = 0) and first-order ([[D, a], J_R b J_R^{-1}] = 0) conditions; orientability (Hochschild cycle representing gamma); Poincaré duality (nondegenerate intersection form). Lorentzian/Krein replacements exist: van den Dungen–Paschke–Rennie (pseudo-Riemannian spectral triples), van den Dungen (Krein spectral triples, J-self-adjoint D, Krein fermionic action), Bizi–Besnard–Brouder (indefinite spectral triples with separate space/time dimensions mod 8). The Euclidean SM triple has the fermion-quadrupling disease (both chiralities and both particle/antiparticle sectors counted as independent Grassmann fields); Barrett 2007 and Connes 2006 fixed it via KO-dimension 6 and a Majorana-type pairing <J_R psi, D psi> over half the fields; in Lorentzian signature the doubling is naturally absent because chirality and reality conditions are compatible in (1,3).

**Cell-by-cell against the carrier.** *Grading*: satisfied, modulo the phi-oddness hypothesis flagged in Q1 — display it. *Krein structure*: satisfied natively; your # antiautomorphism is the Krein adjoint, and your carrier is exactly of the van den Dungen / Bizi–Besnard–Brouder shape. **Terminology warning**: your J (fundamental symmetry / metric operator, linear) and NCG's J_R (real structure / charge conjugation, antilinear) are different objects; never let the notation collide in a document. *Real structure*: **absent**, and this is the serious gap. Without J_R there is no zeroth/first-order condition to state, no KO/(t,s) signature bookkeeping, no Majorana pairing, and no principled fermionic action of the <J_R psi, D psi> type — which is also the gate to the quadrupling question. Concrete candidate (ladder L8): J_R := (edge-orientation reversal alpha_e → alpha_ebar, nabla_e → nabla_ebar) composed with componentwise antilinear conjugation; verify J_R² = ±1, J_R c(alpha_e) J_R^{-1} = ±c(alpha_ebar), and read off the sign table on the kappa = 2 witness. If the signs land on the Lorentzian-resolved side of the ledger, the quadrupling problem never afflicts you; if you cannot build any J_R with consistent signs, that itself is a publishable obstruction. *First-order condition*: **fails, generically and provably**, for any hopping operator relative to the vertex algebra acting pointwise on both sides: [[D, a], b°] picks up hopping × (a(v) − a(w)) × (b(v) − b(w)) ≠ 0 (ladder L9: a finite counterexample is a one-afternoon kernel exercise). Is this fatal? No — and here the literature is squarely on your side: Chamseddine–Connes–van Suijlekom showed that dropping first-order enlarges the inner-fluctuation semigroup and lands on Pati–Salam rather than the bare SM. So the violation is "exactly the freedom" in one sense — **but be honest about the price**: the first-order condition and the spectral action are jointly what let NCG *derive* the SM representation content and the coupling relations (g_3² = g_2² = (5/3) g_1² at the spectral scale). You have neither, and your gauge fields arise from holonomy (Q_C, lattice-gauge mechanism) rather than inner fluctuations — a different and equally respectable mechanism, but one that predicts no coupling unification. Therefore: **never claim NCG-style coupling relations.** What you do have is a seed of a replacement axiom: your KERNEL color-commutant theorem (only color-blind scalar mass terms are color-exact) is precisely the kind of consequence NCG extracts from the order conditions. I would pre-register the search for the axiom of which your color theorem is the first instance: *a decoration-compatibility condition on (phi, transports) that pins the Yukawa/mass sector to the commutant structure*. That is the honest analog of first-order for a holonomy-based gauge mechanism. *Orientability/Poincaré duality*: on a finite 2-complex these become finite (co)homological conditions; your P9 Hodge/harmonic-cohomology machinery is the right toolbox; currently unevaluated, not fatal either way, but Poincaré duality failure would be an early warning that the complex cannot support a nondegenerate fundamental class — worth a cheap numerical probe.

**Verdict.** The carrier is best described as a *Krein finite-difference almost-spectral-triple without real structure*. No NCG axiom violation is fatal to the physics; the missing J_R is fatal only to *making contact with the NCG results you'd want to borrow* (fermionic action, quadrupling resolution, KO bookkeeping). Build J_R; abandon the spectral action without regret; replace first-order with a named decoration axiom or forfeit rep-content predictivity explicitly.

## Q5. The surviving statement — program charter (one page)

**Preamble.** The maximal claim survives with three amendments: (i) no coupling-unification or representation-content claim (those were consequences of NCG axioms we demonstrably do not satisfy); (ii) gravity is claimed only as a gapless spin-2 excitation of a *constrained quotient possessing no Lorentz-covariant conserved stress tensor*, with emergent redundancy as a theorem obligation, not a bonus; (iii) chirality is claimed only through Ginsparg–Wilson descent to the physical sector, with retardedness demoted from mechanism to regulator. Coleman–Mandula closed unification-by-symmetry in 1967; unification-by-decomposition is the door left open, and U1–U3 below are its toll.

**U0 (M, KERNEL — the floor).** One finite Krein carrier D on a decorated 2-complex satisfies 4 D^# D = Q_A^# + Q_C^# + 4 Q_T + 4 E_#; Q_A = invariant mass of the total null momentum; ind(D) = dim M_+ − dim M_- for all Hilbert- and Krein-self-adjoint carriers; the kappa = 2 witness certifies non-vacuous indefinite geometry; the color commutant is scalar.

**U1 (C; gate: GLUE + Gupta–Bleuler).** For the GLUE class, the induced form on V'/N is positive definite and N equals the span of gauge directions. *Kill:* any GLUE model with radical ≠ gauge span, or indefinite induced form. All spectral language stays forbidden until U1 closes. (This is simultaneously: the CM analyticity input, the WW redundancy carrier, and the NN unitarity payment.)

**U2 (C; the redundancy obligation — WW/Marolf).** In refinement families, the E-slot generates first-class constraints; on V'/N no covariant conserved T^{mu nu} exists; the on-shell generator of evolution is cohomologically (boundary-)supported. *Kill:* a quotient with covariant conserved T and a gapless spin-2 mode — then the program publishes "the E-mode is gapped" and retreats to Jacobson-shape claims (field equations without a graviton).

**U3 (C; chirality descent — NN).** The quotient carrier satisfies a GW relation whose Lüscher symmetry carries index ind(D); retarded zeros absent from the unitary shell stay absent after the quotient. *Pre-registered:* compute the zero set of det sigma(k) = Sum_{e<f} z_e z_f |psi_e wedge psi_f|^2 on the covers of record. *Kill:* positive-dimensional gapless sets not removable by GW descent; or any claimed ind ≠ 0 on a periodic cover (excluded by the cover lemma L4).

**U4 (T|H — the limit).** Emergent metric identity det sigma = −g(k,k) + O(k^3), g from wedge-weighted soldering; Lorentz-violating operators irrelevant under the refinement flow (hypothesis displayed). *Experimental kill:* SME / photon-dispersion bounds.

**U5 (C; factorization — CM as output).** Every exact symmetry of the emergent S-matrix factorizes as Poincaré × compact internal; any complex symmetry mixing soldering with internal decorations breaks, gauges, or trivializes on asymptotic states. A derivation target, and if achieved, an explanation of *why* spacetime and internal structure factorize despite living in one operator.

**Explicitly lost:** coupling unification; SM representation content (replacement axiom to be sought; seed = color-commutant theorem); exact fundamental Lorentz invariance; spectral claims pending U1.

One closing synthesis, since the briefing asked for the harshest referee's residue: the four theorem families do not attack four different places.
