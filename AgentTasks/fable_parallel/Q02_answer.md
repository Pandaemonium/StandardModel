Bottom line. The E-slot is not, by itself, a gravitational action — it is gauge-contaminated bookkeeping, and your telescoping conjecture as written is either vacuous or false on an explicit closed-torus probe. But the repair is better than the original: the total trace is exactly invariant at the finite level, invariance plus gradient counting forces Einstein–Hilbert-with-Λ as its two-gradient content, the TEGR coefficients become a finite derivation rather than an ansatz, the corrected telescoping identity yields an honest discrete GHY term, conservation is a Noether-II identity with a computable Mathisson–Papapetrou force, the coupling ratio is computable in the log-det scheme, and dynamics-vs-bookkeeping reduces to one deficit angle and one Hessian pseudo-inverse. The two pre-registered dangers to carry forward: the S-sector trichotomy and the stencil dilemma (telescoping vs doubling) — both of which point, with suspicious insistence, back at your Ginsparg–Wilson layer.
