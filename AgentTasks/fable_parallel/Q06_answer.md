## 1. The benchmark theorem and its ladder

**Setup (fix conventions once).** Work on l2(Z, C^2) in units of the step eps; chirality components psi = (psi_+, psi_-). Shift S: (S psi)_+(x) = psi_+(x-1), (S psi)_-(x) = psi_-(x+1) — each chirality transported one step along **its own null direction, one-sidedly** (this is the retardation decoration). Corner/turn C = exp(i theta sigma_x) = [[cos theta, i sin theta],[i sin theta, cos theta]], acting pointwise, with the checkerboard scaling **theta = m eps** (corner weight i m eps + O(eps^2); the cos theta normalization per straight step is the convention pin-down your corner-convention layer should absorb). Use the **midpoint convention** T = S^{1/2} C S^{1/2}; it is unitarily conjugate to C S by a half-shift, and it is the convention in which the GW structure below is exact. In momentum space:

    T_hat(k) = [[cos theta * e^{-ik}, i sin theta], [i sin theta, cos theta * e^{ik}]],  det T_hat = 1.

Continuum target: H = -i sigma_z d/dx + m sigma_x (up to a trivial sign convention on m), i.e. H_hat(k) = k sigma_z + m sigma_x.

**The ladder** (rungs 0–2 finite, 3–6 analysis; formalize in order):

**R0 (FINITE IDENTITY; extends your existing checkerboard layer).** Path-sum = transfer power: entries of T^N on the size-N diamond equal the sum over checkerboard paths weighted (cos theta)^{#straight} * (i sin theta)^{#corners}. Pure finite combinatorics; this is the bridge from your corner conventions to operator powers.

**R1 (FINITE IDENTITY).** Symbol facts, all provable by 2x2 Cayley–Hamilton and trig identities: (i) dispersion cos omega(k) = cos theta * cos k, eigenvalues e^{+-i omega(k)}; (ii) since det T_hat = 1, T_hat + T_hat^{-1} = (tr T_hat) * 1, hence the **exact Hermitian-part identity**

    Herm(D_hat(k)) = (1 - cos theta * cos k)/eps * 1,   D := (1 - T)/eps.

This scalar is >= 0, vanishes only at (k, theta) = (0,0), and equals ~2/eps at k = pi. **This is the hidden Wilson term, exactly, and it is generated by nothing but one-sidedness of the null shifts.** On the L-site torus this is literally finite; on Z it is a trig identity.

**R2 (FINITE IDENTITY; the GW rung — see Section 3).** Gamma_r T Gamma_r = T^{-1} with Gamma_r = sigma_z composed with x -> -x; hence the exact GW relation and Gamma_r-Hermiticity D-dagger = Gamma_r D Gamma_r.

**R3 (T|H, elementary 2x2 analysis).** Fiberwise consistency: for all k,

    || T_hat_eps(k) - exp(-i eps H_hat(k)) || <= C (k^2 + m^2) eps^2,

by Taylor with explicit integral remainder on 2x2 matrices. Mathlib has matrix exponentials; this rung is feasible now.

**R4 (FINITE IDENTITY, one line).** Telescoping: for unitaries (or contractions) A, B: ||A^n - B^n|| <= n ||A - B||. Induction; kernel-shaped.

**R5 (T|H — the limit rung, no Chernoff needed).** Define T_eps directly on L2(R, C^2) (shifts by eps are unitary on the continuum — this **eliminates identification-operator bureaucracy entirely**; the genuinely-lattice statement is recovered by a band-limited sampling lemma). Then R3 + R4 give per-fiber ||T_hat^n(k) - e^{-i t H_hat(k)}|| <= t C(k) eps for n = floor(t/eps); dominated convergence + Plancherel give

    T_eps^{floor(t/eps)} -> e^{-iHt}  strongly, locally uniformly in t,

equivalently strong resolvent convergence of H_eps := (i/eps) Log T_eps (principal branch; the branch cut at quasienergy pi touches a measure-zero fiber set). Corollary: spectral windows converge; every mode with quasienergy near pi has |H_eps| >= (pi - theta)/eps and escapes every fixed window. **Demolition of the norm version:** at k = pi, T_hat = -C(theta), so sup_k ||T_hat^n(k) - e^{-i t H_hat(k)}|| stays ~2; norm convergence is FALSE. State the theorem in strong topology or not at all.

**R6 (T|H, benchmark target).** Retarded-kernel convergence: for f, g in C_c^infinity(R^{1,1}, C^2), the smeared discrete retarded propagator (path sum of R0, forward-cone support by exact finite propagation speed 1) converges to (f, S_ret g) with rate O(eps) on compacta. This is where the classical literature lands: Arrighi–Nesme–Forets prove O(eps^2) observational convergence in all dimensions; Skopenkov–Ustinov prove the pointwise kernel asymptotics rigorously (solving Feynman's own 1965 problem), including Airy behavior near the light cone.

**What plays the Trotter/Chernoff role.** In the free case: R4, the telescoping inequality — Chernoff degenerates to one line because translation invariance diagonalizes everything. The moment you decorate (external field, position-dependent phi, transports), you lose the fibers and need Chernoff's product theorem proper: consistency on a core of an essentially self-adjoint generator + uniform power-boundedness => convergence. **Warning with teeth:** for J-unitary (rather than unitary) one-step maps, uniform power-boundedness in the Hilbert norm is *not* automatic — it is essentially a uniform-majorant condition. ## 5. The two most likely fatal obstructions

**O1 — Uniform-majorant failure (the Krein limit is not Krein).** Inductive limits of Pontryagin spaces with kappa_n -> infinity have no completion theory; if the physical quotient norms of a fixed coarse observable drift (blow up or collapse) under refinement, condition 3 of Section 2 fails and there is no physical Hilbert space, however perfect every finite level is. **O2 — The celestial point-group problem in 3+1 (marginal anisotropy + double limit).** With a fixed finite set of null directions, the point group of the complex may be too small to forbid **anisotropic operators of dimension <= 4**, which are marginal or relevant and hence do *not* die in the limit; then the continuum limit requires fine-tuned counterterms — precisely the documented disease of minimally doubled (Boriçi–Creutz/Karsten–Wilczek-type) fermions, whose broken hypercubic symmetry forces counterterm tuning. Since your dual-soldered null frame is (by your own audit) Creutz-adjacent, this obstruction has a literature-verified track record. Additionally, curing it by refining the direction set makes the continuum limit a **double limit** (directions D -> infinity jointly with eps -> 0) whose exchange is unproven; and the walk's exact front set is polyhedral at every finite D, so Lorentz invariance can only hold observationally, never uniformly. *Earliest finite computation (kernel-shaped, uses your existing character-theory assets):* for the candidate direction set (e.g. the tetrahedral 4-direction null frame), enumerate by character theory the point-group-invariant fermion bilinears of dimension <= 4 and count the excess over the O(3)-invariant count. **Kill-condition:** excess > 0 means fine-tunings are mandatory and the "one operator, no tuning" reading of the 3+1 limit fails as stated.
