## Claim corrections

- A local trace-preserving channel theorem establishes operational
  no-signaling between chosen tensor factors. It does not prove those factors
  correspond to spacelike-separated regions of an emergent geometry.
- A two-factor tensor model is a first microcausality witness, not a complete
  Haag-Kastler net. Region posets, gluing, covariance, and refinement remain
  missing.
- Finite exact symmetry plus a simple ground state forbids spontaneous breaking
  of the pure density matrix. The physical SSB mechanism therefore requires
  degeneracy and a thermodynamic/refinement limit.
- The dimensional-transmutation identity converts a supplied running coupling
  into an invariant scale. The beta function, closure/QCD identification, and
  measured scale are not derived.
- `Q`, `Q^*Q+QQ^*`, and `D#D` retain their distinct redundancy, harmonic, and
  spectral roles. The complete-process tuple must encode compatibility rather
  than collapse them into one operator.
- Born probabilities remain assumed. Positive-sector selection supplies the
  domain on which probabilities make sense; it does not derive Gleason/POVM
  hypotheses or the experienced selection of one outcome.
