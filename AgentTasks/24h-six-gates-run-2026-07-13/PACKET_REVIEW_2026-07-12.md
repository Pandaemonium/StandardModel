# Review of the program overview packet

Reviewed file:
`Sources/Null_Edge_Program_Overview_Packet_2026-07-12.tex`.

## Overall verdict

The packet succeeds as an orientation document. Its strongest editorial
choice is that Section 7 states the distance from exact finite mathematics to
physics rather than treating the finite results as completion. The six open
items are the right ones, but their starting points are uneven and a few were
already stale by the end of the preceding autonomous run.

The run should not allocate equal effort to all six. The continuum lane has a
nearly complete analytic chain and can plausibly change grade. Lorentz
invariance needs an exact L0 statement and a source-audited finite/no-go split
before a grand theorem is meaningful. Dynamics, scales/gravity/Lambda, and
gauge chirality each have substantial finite cores but no accepted continuum
identification. Unification has several algebraic rungs, yet remains the lane
most vulnerable to hollow composition.

## Section 7 findings

### 7.1 Continuum limit

The wording understated the current frontier. The repository now has a
changing momentum spacing, exhausting physical boxes, a uniform live-walk
multiplier estimate, a normalized cell-average contraction, active-cell
geometry, and a three-term projection bound. The missing step is no longer
"start position-space convergence"; it is the final arbitrary-`L2` projection
epsilon theorem, followed by the live multiplier/interpolation composition and
the inverse-Fourier PDE identification.

### 7.2 Lorentz invariance

This remains the largest structural debt. The packet correctly disclaims exact
Lorentz symmetry of a fixed lattice, but "stated and scheduled" is too vague.
The run must split L0 into:

1. a deterministic finite-support no-go under a noncompact boost;
2. a finite distributional-invariance shadow for count ensembles;
3. a primary-source statement of the actual Poisson-sprinkling theorem;
4. an explicit bridge obligation from finite shadows to a locally finite
   Lorentz-invariant random causal order.

Only item 1 or 2 is plausibly kernel-formalizable in one day. Neither is the
Bombelli-Henson-Sorkin theorem.

### 7.3 Dynamics supplied versus derived

The packet was slightly stale. Static covariance is classified, momentum
dependence forces the flip branch to accompany parity, and both branches act
on the exact ordered two-channel step. What remains is exhaustive symmetry
classification of the step, full `3+1` covariance, and a principle selecting
the state or constraint family that produces the generator.

The best 24-hour target is a finite maximum-entropy/modular selection theorem
for the actual Pluecker pair generator, with uniqueness and a noncommuting
observable. This derives a flow from a declared equilibrium state; it does not
derive why nature chooses that state or constraint.

### 7.4 Absolute scales, gravity, and Lambda

This subsection contains three independent debts and needs three ledgers.

- The scale-free positive Pluecker action has a proved collapse no-go, while an
  exact supplied discrete RG flow has dimensional transmutation. The missing
  bridge must expose every remaining dimensionless coupling and reference
  scale.
- Finite Clausius and sourced-soldering avatars exist. The missing content is
  universality over a separating family of variations and then a continuum
  geometric identification. A general finite universal-variation theorem is
  worthwhile; it is not Einstein's equation.
- The Lambda exponent fork is now a theorem for a fermionic projection count.
  The remaining physical questions are which count is conjugate to Lambda,
  sign, dynamics, covariance, and observational viability. Finite partition
  response identities can sharpen this but cannot choose the physical count.

### 7.5 Gauge-field chirality and anomalies

The free overlap/Ginsparg-Wilson infrastructure is substantial. The immediate
gap is not another arithmetic anomaly sum; it is a gauge-covariant finite
operator whose index is invariant under the permitted gauge transformations or
gapped homotopies, followed by an exact trace/index anomaly identity. Standard
Model anomaly cancellation arithmetic is a separate representation-level
control and must not be presented as a derived gauge-field anomaly theorem.

### 7.6 Unification

The packet is appropriately cautious, but the frontier has advanced. The
Furey exterior-Fock correspondence and representation-level `Z6` kernel now
have kernel-checked rungs. The shared cube law between the rest operator and
pair generator is also formalized. None of this yet proves a common origin.

The best Null-Edge bridge target is an explicit active-sector intertwiner
between the many-null rest operator and pair-transfer generator, together with
an obstruction to extending it indiscriminately. The best Jordan bridge target
must start from a derived Jordan flag subspace, not from a coordinate copy of
`C^2`; otherwise it is dimension matching dressed as unification.
