## Objective

Prove that a scalar-plus-nilpotent real-linear endomorphism has no nontrivial
idempotent polynomial filters. The intended G2 consequence is that the direct
retarded causal wave operator, whose diagonal is scalar and whose strict
causal part should be nilpotent on a finite order, cannot itself produce a
nonzero proper polynomial projector. A successful probe selector would then
need a normal operator, Hermitian dilation, paired retarded/advanced data, or a
genuinely richer constraint.

1. an idempotent `P` for which `P - c I` is nilpotent must be `0` or `I`;
2. every idempotent polynomial in `a I + N`, with `N` nilpotent, is `0` or

Do not weaken the conclusion, add finite-dimensionality unless mathematically

## Proof idea

For the first theorem, idempotence gives
`P(P-I)=0`. If `Q=P-cI` is nilpotent, expanding idempotence in `Q` should force
`c(c-1)=0`: otherwise the identity lies in the nilpotent ideal generated by
`Q`, contradicting nontriviality of the module. Then an idempotent that is
itself nilpotent is zero; if `P-I` is nilpotent, apply the same argument to
`I-P`.

For the polynomial theorem, use divisibility of `p(X)-p(a)` by `X-a` to show
`p(aI+N)-p(a)I` is nilpotent, then invoke the first theorem.
