**Direct answer to the sharpest question (energy conservation).** It is the
commuting-observable triviality, not a genuine dynamical fact — and the Lean
statement says so out loud. The signature quantifies over an *arbitrary*
`E : EuclideanSpace ℂ (Fin 3) →L[ℂ] …` under the explicit hypothesis
`hUE : CommutesWithStep (carrierFlowStep …) E`, and concludes
`observableEnergy E (orbit …) = observableEnergy E psi`. That is exactly
`⟨Uⁿψ, E Uⁿψ⟩ = ⟨ψ, E ψ⟩` because `[U,E]=0` and `U` is unitary — true of *any*
symmetry-of-the-dynamics observable on any unitary flow. Three points:

1. **It is genuine linear algebra, but not "energy conservation."** The word
   `energy` / `observableEnergy` is decorative: `E` is any commuting observable,
   never instantiated at the generator `H = B`. The physically meaningful
   instance (`E = B`, whose conservation would read `⟨ψ_t, H ψ_t⟩ = ⟨ψ_0,Hψ_0⟩`)
   is *not stated*; and even that instance is the triviality `[H, e^{-itH}]=0`,
   not a nontrivial constant of motion. So there is **no** claim of the form
   "the Hamiltonian's expectation is dynamically conserved beyond commutation."
2. **Honesty is carried by the hypothesis, not hidden.** Because
   `CommutesWithStep E` is a visible premise, the theorem does *not* over-claim
   in its statement. The over-reach is only in naming (`energy`) and in the
   module title "energy-conserving orbit."
3. **Not vacuous.** `CommutesWithStep` is satisfiable (identity, `U` itself, and
   `B`), so this is a true non-vacuous triviality, not a false-shape.

Verdict: **MINOR** — genuine as stated (commuting-observable invariance under a
unitary flow), mildly dressed as "energy." Remedy: rename to
`carrier_orbit_commuting_observable_conserved` (or keep the name but add to the
docstring: "this is conservation of an observable already assumed to commute with
the step — the standard unitary invariance `⟨Uψ,EUψ⟩=⟨ψ,Eψ⟩`; it does not derive
a Hamiltonian or a nontrivial constant of motion").

**`carrier6_orbit_norm_conserved` (MINOR).** Its docstring calls this "the
concrete carrier's actual sector evolution, not just a block." Kernel content =
Euclidean-unitarity of `exp(-itM6)` and norm-preservation. The generator is `M6`
under the grade-C "generator-as-Hamiltonian" posit, and `M6`'s Clifford
provenance is docstring/oracle (see below). "Actual sector evolution" outruns
that. Remedy: "Euclidean-unitary evolution generated by the sector form `M6`
(generator-as-Hamiltonian is a grade-C posit)."

The **module docstring is exemplary**: caveats (0)/(C)/`(2,1)` pre-empt almost
every over-read, in particular caveat (0) explicitly concedes *"that the Krein
flow preserves that sector is not proved here."* Hold that thought.

## THE single most load-bearing over-claim

**`SectorGroundMassWitness.sector_krein_form_eq_one`'s docstring claim that it is
"the resolution of 'Krein-unitary ≠ norm-unitary' for `CarrierUnitaryFlow`,
kernel-certified."**

Why it is the most load-bearing (and beats the energy-conservation point): the
program's physical payload is "the *carrier's* time evolution conserves
norm/energy." That payload has two gaps — (i) generator-as-Hamiltonian (grade C,
disclosed), and (ii) the Euclidean flow equals the physical **Krein** flow on the
sector. Gap (ii) is precisely the static-vs-dynamical confusion above:
`sector_krein_form_eq_one` proves the *static* identity `J|_sector = Euclidean` and
is advertised as *resolving* the dynamical equivalence, but the required lemma —
**the Krein flow preserves the `J`-positive sector** — is proved nowhere in these
files and is openly conceded missing by `CarrierUnitaryFlow` caveat (0). So the one
docstring that claims to bridge trivial Euclidean-unitarity to genuine physical
(Krein) evolution over-claims exactly at the load-bearing joint.

By contrast, `carrier_orbit_energy_conserved` is a commuting-observable triviality
but is *stated honestly* (the `CommutesWithStep E` hypothesis is right there and
`E` is never dressed up as the Hamiltonian); its only sin is the decorative name
"energy" — MINOR, not load-bearing.

**Exact remedy.** Edit the `sector_krein_form_eq_one` docstring parenthetical to:
"this is the static metric identity `J|_sector = Euclidean`; it does not show the
Krein flow preserves the sector (open — `CarrierUnitaryFlow` caveat 0), hence does
not by itself identify the Euclidean flow with the physical Krein evolution." No
statement changes anywhere; every theorem's *math* stands. To actually close gap
(ii), add and prove `Jmet`-unitarity of `exp(-itHAC)` **plus** invariance of
`range Piso` under it, then re-derive the sector orbit conservation from that.
