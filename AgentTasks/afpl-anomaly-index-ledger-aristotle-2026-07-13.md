# Aristotle task: anomaly moments versus stabilized boundary indices

## Objective

Build an exact finite ledger connecting, and sharply distinguishing, three
objects:

1. the one-generation Standard Model anomaly coefficients in the project
   convention `Q = T3 + Y/2` with every fermion represented left-handed;
2. oriented channel multiplicities whose localized unilateral-shift defect is
   `+m` or `-m` and additive under stacking;
3. charge-weighted moments of those oriented channels.

## Required outcomes

1. Reproduce the exact gravitational-`U(1)`, cubic `U(1)^3`,
   `SU(2)^2 U(1)`, and `SU(3)^2 U(1)` cancellations with explicit
   multiplicities and rational charges.
2. Define an oriented channel ledger independently of anomaly moments.  Prove
   its unweighted count and its first and cubic charge moments.
3. Determine by theorem whether Standard Model anomaly cancellation forces the
   unweighted oriented boundary count to vanish.  If not, give the explicit
   one-generation counterexample and state the missing datum.
4. Classify the minimal addition of mirror/opposite-orientation channels that
   simultaneously cancels the unweighted boundary count and preserves all
   anomaly cancellations.  Return a rational/integer nonzero witness.
5. If possible, connect the abstract oriented count to the supplied
   `HalfSpaceDefectIndex.stabilizedIndex_eq` and orientation-reversal theorem;
   otherwise state a clean interface theorem whose hypotheses name those exact
   results.
6. Do not claim anomaly cancellation implies a bulk-edge theorem, a physical
   mirror gap, or a Standard Model lattice regulator.  This is a bookkeeping
   and obstruction theorem.
7. No proof placeholders, compiled evaluation, or new assumptions.  Include
   build-enforced axiom guards.

The highest-value answer may be a no-go separating anomaly moments from the
unweighted topological channel index.  Do not force a false unification.
