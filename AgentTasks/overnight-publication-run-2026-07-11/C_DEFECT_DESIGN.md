## Pillar 3 design (submit when 1 and 2 return)

Two candidate routes, to be chosen by what pillars 1-2 return:

### Route A (sign-wall determinant route)
For a REAL z-field on an interval/chain with one sign wall (z = -m left,
+m right; the wall is the collinearity zero), build the finite chiral walk
W(z) (split-step with site-dependent coin). Prove:
- A1: the edge-reversal grading Gamma of GWRetardedTransfer satisfies
  ChiralInvolution for W(z) (chiral symmetry of the decorated transfer).
- A2: det W(z) = -1 for one wall (odd defect count) - the determinant reads
  the wall parity. [The exact mechanism per ChiralZeroModeParity's K6 probe:
  even wall counts give det = +1 with sectored pinning; ODD wall count on an
  open chain / anti-periodic closure is the det = -1 case.]
- A3: apply pillar 2: forced exact -1 (and +1) transfer eigenvector.
- A4: locate the eigenvector: support concentrated at the wall (localization
  statement, possibly via explicit small-L exact eigenvector with rational
  3-4-5 coin data).
- Control: no wall (constant z) gives det = +1 and no forced mode.

### Route B (sectored-index route)
For the even-wall periodic case (physically natural on a cycle), use the
reflection-equivariant sectored index already identified in
ChiralZeroModeParity's header: W commutes with a reflection R, [R,Gamma]=0,
and the two R-sectors carry opposite chiral indices, each pinning one mode.
- B1: formalize the sector decomposition and per-sector det/index.
- B2: per-sector pillar-2 application forces one mode per sector.
- B3: winding link: the pillar-1 derived winding of the z-field equals the
  sector index (the discrete bulk-boundary statement).
- Control: zero-winding field has trivial sector indices.

Route A is shorter and gives the headline "a collinearity wall forces an
exact protected mode, kernel-checked"; Route B is the fuller bulk-boundary
statement. Recommend A first, B as the paper's general theorem if time
allows.
