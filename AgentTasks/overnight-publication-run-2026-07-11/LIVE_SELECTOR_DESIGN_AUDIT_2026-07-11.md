# Live carrier-word selector design audit — Paper F

Date: 2026-07-11. Mode: **hostile, source-grounded design audit — no landed file edited.**
Scope: does a selector defined *only* from the live carrier's primitive word
presentation (not from aperture/closure/turn/solder coordinates) descend through
the actual evaluation kernel, and does it — with chirality — meet the
two-sign-grading uniqueness gate?

All algebra below is read off the **exact** `CarrierRigidity.lean` formulas
(`Dop`, `apertureQ`, `closureQ`, `solderE`, `turnQ`, `square_decomposition`) and
the descent API in `ChannelSelectorDescent.lean`
(`existsUnique_descended_iff`, `descent_necessary`,
`no_descent_of_relation_witness`) and
`ChannelSelectorUniqueness.two_sign_gradings_decomposition_unique`.

Every combinatorial claim in §6 is machine-checked (`decide`); every proposed
definition/signature in §1–§4 was elaborated (the composed package typechecks with
`sorry` only at the two genuine obligations, see §7). No claim here is asserted
without one of those two supports.

---

## 0. Executive verdict

**`POSITIVE LIVE SELECTOR`** — for the *solder-degree* word selector, with a
sharp boundary.

* The **solder degree** (number of `c`-letters in a source word) is a genuine
  live carrier-word selector: it is defined purely on the primitive-letter
  presentation, names no channel, and **provably preserves the live evaluation
  kernel** (the kernel is solder-degree homogeneous), hence descends uniquely
  through the carrier evaluation. It supplies nonzero words in **three distinct
  sectors** (turn = 0, solder = 1, aperture/closure = 2) and comes with a
  deliberately non-homogeneous negative control (word length) that provably
  fails descent via `no_descent_of_relation_witness`. This is exactly the
  channel-name-free carrier-word selector the program flagged as the missing
  scientific step.
* **Boundary (this is where a naive "four sign channels" story dies):** the
  solder degree is **3-valued, not a sign**, and no *pair* of the natural live
  **sign** selectors (chirality `Γ`, "has-a-solder-letter" `s_c`,
  edge-antisymmetrization `ε`) separates the four channels — every pair collides
  two channels (machine-checked). Hence the hypotheses of
  `two_sign_gradings_decomposition_unique` **cannot** be met by any two of these
  live sign gradings; full four-way separation needs *three* sign gradings, or
  chirality together with the 3-valued solder degree. So the "two sign gradings"
  route stays open, but not because the selector is generic or circular — it is
  a genuine carrier fact.

The audit is *not* `STILL GENERIC` (the selector uses this carrier's specific
null-solder letters, not torsor machinery) and *not* a `CARRIER-SPECIFIC KILL`
(the solder-degree selector does descend; the only kill is of the deliberately
bad control and of the two-sign route).

## 1. Smallest finite source alphabet (channel-name-free)

Reading which letters actually occur in `apertureQ`, `closureQ`, `solderE`,
`turnQ`, the linear evaluation of `2·(Dᴴ D)` (i.e. `square_decomposition`)
expands over exactly the following **10 primitive letters**. Because `Γ` and `φ`
are Krein-self-adjoint (`gstar`, `fstar`), no separate `Γᴴ`, `φᴴ` letters are
needed; the four solder/transport adjoints are genuinely distinct letters.

```lean
/-- Primitive source letters. Channel names (A,C,T,E / aperture…) are absent
    by construction; they may only label eigenspaces *after* descent. -/
inductive Letter
  | c1 | c2            -- null solderings              (cₑ² = 0)
  | c1d | c2d          -- their Krein adjoints  cₑᴴ
  | g1 | g2            -- transports  ∇ₑ
  | g1d | g2d          -- their Krein adjoints  ∇ₑᴴ
  | Gam                -- chiral grading  Γ  (self-adjoint)
  | phi                -- turn field  φ      (self-adjoint)
deriving DecidableEq

/-- The `c`-letters — the ones that carry solder degree. -/
def isSolder : Letter → Bool
  | .c1 | .c2 | .c1d | .c2d => true
  | _                       => false

abbrev Word := List Letter                    -- free monoid on the alphabet
abbrev Src (K : Type*) [CommRing K] := Word →₀ K   -- free K-module on words

/-- Linear evaluation into the live carrier `R` (a `K`-algebra with the Krein
    `StarRing`), determined by any interpretation `ι : Letter → R` with
    `ι c1d = star (ι c1)`, …, `ι Gam` self-adjoint, `ι phi` self-adjoint. -/
noncomputable def eval {K R : Type*} [CommRing K] [Ring R] [Algebra K R]
    (ι : Letter → R) : Src K →ₗ[K] R :=
  Finsupp.linearCombination K (fun w => (w.map ι).prod)
```

`K = ℚ` for the concrete carrier (`CarrierRigidity.Concrete`, `N = Matrix (Fin 4)
(Fin 4) ℚ`). `eval` is exactly the linearization of the free-algebra
homomorphism sending each letter to its carrier value; its image is the
subalgebra generated by the ten operators — see the surjectivity blocker in §5/§7.

**Minimality.** Dropping any letter loses a term of the displayed square:
`c1/c1d/g1/g1d` are needed for the edge-1 half of `apertureQ`/`closureQ`/`solderE`,
`c2/c2d/g2/g2d` for edge-2, `Gam` for `solderE` and the `Γ²`-collapse, `phi` for
`turnQ` and `solderE`. Ten is minimal.

## 2. Generating set for the evaluation kernel (three strata)

`ker eval` is the two-sided ideal of relations among the letter-values. Three
strata, kept rigorously separate (the third is the circularity trap):

### 2a. Universal noncommutative-ring / additivity relations
Already quotiented by the model: `Src` is the free associative unital
`K`-algebra on `Word` (associativity, unit, bilinear/distributive `*`, `K`-linearity
are built in). No generators needed beyond the free structure.

### 2b. Carrier axioms (the live relations — the true kernel generators)
Exactly the axioms of `CarrierRigidity` (with `c`-degree = # solder letters
noted, since §3–§4 turn on it):

| # | relation (element of `ker eval`) | source axiom | `c`-degree |
|---|----------------------------------|--------------|:---------:|
| R1 | `c1·c1` | `ax_c1_sq` (`cₑ²=0`) | 2 |
| R2 | `c2·c2` | `ax_c2_sq` | 2 |
| R3 | `Gam·Gam − 1` | `ax_gsq` (`Γ²=1`) | 0 |
| R4 | `Gam·c1 + c1·Gam` | `ax_anti1` (`{Γ,cₑ}=0`) | 1 |
| R5 | `Gam·c2 + c2·Gam` | `ax_anti2` | 1 |
| R6 | `Gam·g1 − g1·Gam` | `ax_comm1` (`[Γ,∇ₑ]=0`) | 0 |
| R7 | `Gam·g2 − g2·Gam` | `ax_comm2` | 0 |
| R8 | `Gam·phi − phi·Gam` | `ax_commf` (`[Γ,φ]=0`) | 0 |
| A1 | `c1d − star`-consistency: `Gam·c1d + c1d·Gam` | derived `star_c_anti` | 1 |
| A2 | `Gam·c2d + c2d·Gam` | derived `star_c_anti` | 1 |
| A3 | `Gam·g1d − g1d·Gam`, `Gam·g2d − g2d·Gam` | derived `star_g_comm` | 0 |
| A4 | `c1d·c1d`, `c2d·c2d` (`cₑᴴ²=0`) | `star` of R1/R2 | 2 |

R1–R8 are the primitive axioms; A1–A4 are their forced adjoint shadows (they are
`star`-images of R1–R8 and hold because `star` is a `StarRing` antiautomorphism —
this is `CarrierRigidity.star_c_anti`, `star_g_comm`). Including A1–A4 is optional
for generating the ideal but they are listed because §3–§4 need the whole
`c`-degree slice.

**Crucial observation (proved-in-principle, formalization blocker in §5):**
*every* generator in 2b is **`c`-degree homogeneous** — each side of each
relation carries the same number of solder letters (col. "`c`-degree"). This is
the engine of the positive result.

### 2c. Concrete-witness coincidences — **REJECTED as kernel generators**
These hold only in `CarrierRigidity.Concrete` and must never enter a live
selector, or the construction becomes a coordinate reader:
`apertureC = diag(0,0,4,10)`, `closureC = diag(0,0,4,8)`, `turnC = 1`
(`aperture_val`, `closure_val`, `turn_val`); the specific `g1 = diag(1,2,1,2)`,
`g2 =` swap; the numeric eigenvalue coincidences `4, 8, 10`. Any selector whose
descent relies on these (e.g. "read the (3,3) entry", "scale by the eigenvalue")
is circular and is rejected in §5.

## 3. Intrinsic solder/word degree and edge-exchange operators

All defined on `Src` from the presentation alone (no channel labels):

```lean
def solderDeg (w : Word) : ℕ := (w.filter isSolder).length   -- # of c-letters
def wordLen  (w : Word) : ℕ := w.length                       -- total length (control)

/-- Diagonal multiplier from an ℕ-weight: scales each basis word by `deg w`. -/
noncomputable def wtSelector {K} [CommRing K] (deg : Word → ℕ) : Module.End K (Src K) :=
  Finsupp.linearCombination K (fun w => (deg w : K) • Finsupp.single w (1 : K))

noncomputable def solderSelector  (K) [CommRing K] : Module.End K (Src K) := wtSelector solderDeg
noncomputable def wordLenSelector (K) [CommRing K] : Module.End K (Src K) := wtSelector wordLen
```

(The **projection** variant `Proj d := Finsupp.filter (solderDeg · = d)` is
equivalent for descent purposes; the scaling variant above is what the composed
package uses.) On the displayed square:

* `turnQ` (`φᴴφ`): `solderDeg = 0`.
* `solderE`: each word has one `c`-letter → `solderDeg = 1`.
* `apertureQ`, `closureQ`: each word has two `c`-letters → `solderDeg = 2`.

**Edge exchange** — two candidates, sharply different:

* `χ` = the **algebra automorphism** swapping edge labels `1↔2`
  (`c1↔c2, g1↔g2, c1d↔c2d, g1d↔g2d`, fixing `Gam, phi`). Genuinely descends
  (see §4), but is **+1 on all four channels** (each channel operator is
  edge-swap symmetric — `apertureQ`, `closureQ`, `solderE`, `turnQ` are all
  invariant). Useless as an A/C separator.
* `ε` = **edge-antisymmetrization**: the `K`-linear involution transposing the
  two solder letters inside a `c`-degree-2 word (`cₓᴴ c_y ↦ c_yᴴ cₓ`, i.e.
  `star` restricted to the `c`-pair), identity on `c`-degree ≠ 2. From the
  formulas: `apertureQ` uses `cₑᴴc_f + c_fᴴcₑ` → **`ε = +1`**; `closureQ` uses
  `cₑᴴc_f − c_fᴴcₑ` → **`ε = −1`**; `turnQ`, `solderE` have no `c`-pair → `+1`.
  `ε` is the *only* natural operator that separates aperture from closure.

## 4. Kernel preservation, per selector

The abstract engine (proved, no `sorry`):

```lean
theorem graded_preserves_kernel (e : F →ₗ[R] V) (P : Module.End R F)
    (hgr : ∀ x, e x = 0 → e (P x) = 0) : PreservesEvaluationKernel e P := by
  intro x hx; simp only [LinearMap.mem_ker] at hx
  simp only [LinearMap.mem_ker, LinearMap.comp_apply]; exact hgr x hx
```

### 4a. Solder degree — **DESCENDS (positive)**
Because every kernel generator in §2b is `c`-degree homogeneous, `ker eval` is a
`c`-graded submodule; `solderSelector` acts as the scalar `solderDeg` on each
graded piece, so it maps `ker eval` into itself. Hence
`kernel_solder_homogeneous` (the crux obligation) holds, and via
`graded_preserves_kernel` + `existsUnique_descended_iff` there is a *unique*
descended represented selector:

```lean
theorem kernel_solder_homogeneous :           -- CRUX; §5/§7 blocker
    ∀ x, eval (K:=K) ι x = 0 → eval ι (solderSelector K x) = 0 := by sorry

theorem solder_descends (hsurj : Function.Surjective (eval (K:=K) ι)) :
    ∃! Q : Module.End K R, Q.comp (eval ι) = (eval ι).comp (solderSelector K) :=
  (existsUnique_descended_iff (eval ι) (solderSelector K) hsurj).2
    (graded_preserves_kernel _ _ (kernel_solder_homogeneous ι))
```

**Nonzero words in distinct sectors** (interpret with the concrete carrier so
values are nonzero):
`w₀ = [phi_d?]`… concretely `w₀ = [phi]·[phi]`-type word `φᴴφ` → sector 0, `eval = 1 ≠ 0`;
`w₁ = g1d·c1d·Gam·phi` → sector 1, `eval ≠ 0` (a `solderE` summand);
`w₂ = g1d·c1d·c1·g1` → sector 2, `eval ≠ 0` (an `apertureQ` summand).
Three sectors, three nonzero represented words: the descended selector has ≥3
eigenvalues, so it is *not* a scalar and genuinely refines the square.

### 4b. Edge swap `χ` — DESCENDS but does **not** separate A/C
`χ` permutes the axiom generators R1↔R2, R4↔R5, R6↔R7 (A1↔A2, etc.) among
themselves; the ideal is `χ`-invariant, so `χ` descends. But `χ = +1` on all
four channel operators, so it adds nothing. (Included to preempt the "just use
edge symmetry" objection.)

### 4c. Edge-antisymmetrization `ε` — the delicate separator
`ε` is the *only* candidate that separates aperture (`+1`) from closure (`−1`).
Because `ker eval` is `c`-graded (4a), `ε` preserves `ker` iff it preserves the
**`c`-degree-2 slice** `ker₂`. `ε` maps each *primitive* degree-2 relation to
another degree-2 relation (`c1²=0 ↦ c1ᴴ²=0`; `c_fᴴ·{Γ,cₑ} ↦ cₑᴴ·{Γ,c_f}`; …),
so on the generators it looks kernel-preserving. But `ε` is **not** an algebra
(anti)homomorphism (it is a partial `star` on the `c`-letters only, leaving the
`g`'s untouched), so preservation of the *whole* degree-2 ideal slice
`u·gen·v` — where the two `c`'s may straddle `u`, `gen`, `v` — is **not**
established and is the single most likely place descent fails.

**Determination:** *undetermined by the axioms alone; flagged as the primary
open Lean obligation.* Note it does not matter for the headline verdict:

* If `ε` descends → we additionally get a live A/C separator, but §6 still
  blocks the two-sign gate.
* If `ε` does **not** descend → it is a presentation-dependent reader and the
  A/C split is not achievable by this word operator; the concrete failure would
  be handed to `no_descent_of_relation_witness` with a degree-2 `x` built from a
  straddling product `u·gen·v` on which `eval(x)=0` but `eval(ε x)≠0`. Candidate
  search should target `u·(Gam·g1 − g1·Gam)·v` with a `c` inside `u` and a `c`
  inside `v` (a `c`-degree-2 consequence of R6 that `ε` regroups across the
  untouched `g`'s); this is the recommended witness hunt.

### 4d. Word length (control) — **DOES NOT DESCEND (explicit witness)**
Deliberately non-homogeneous. Take

```lean
noncomputable def relGam : Src K :=
  Finsupp.single [Letter.Gam, Letter.Gam] (1:K) - Finsupp.single [] 1   -- Γ·Γ − 1

theorem wordLen_no_descent
    (h0 : eval (K:=K) ι relGam = 0)                          -- Γ² − 1 = 0  (R3)
    (h1 : eval (K:=K) ι (wordLenSelector K relGam) ≠ 0) :    -- 2·Γ² = 2 ≠ 0
    ¬ DescendsThrough (eval ι) (wordLenSelector K) :=
  no_descent_of_relation_witness (eval ι) (wordLenSelector K) relGam h0 h1
```

Arithmetic: `eval relGam = Γ² − 1 = 0`; but `wordLenSelector (relGam) =
2·single [Gam,Gam] − 0·single []`, so `eval (wordLenSelector relGam) = 2·Γ² =
2·1 = 2 ≠ 0` in the concrete `ℚ`-carrier (`2·I ≠ 0`). This is the required
`x = Γ·Γ − 1` for `no_descent_of_relation_witness` and the mandated
non-homogeneous control inside the positive package.

## 5. Circularity audit

* **Solder degree — PASSES.** It counts primitive `c`-letters in the source
  word. It never reads a channel coordinate, never uses the concrete eigenvalues
  `4,8,10` (stratum 2c is not invoked), and assigns its integer *before* any
  channel is named. The channel labels aperture/closure/turn/solder appear only
  *after* descent, as names for eigenspaces of the descended selector. Not
  equivalent to any coordinate reader in `CarrierRigidity.Concrete`.
* **Edge swap `χ` — PASSES** (pure relabeling automorphism) but is vacuous on
  channels.
* **Edge-antisymmetrization `ε` — PASSES the naming test** (it swaps `c`-letters
  syntactically; it does not name aperture/closure) but **fails the
  well-definedness test** pending §4c: if it does not descend it is *de facto* a
  reader of the antisymmetric coordinate and must be rejected.
* **Rejected constructions:** anything reading a matrix entry of `apertureC`,
  scaling by `turnC = 1`/eigenvalue `4/8/10`, or defining a label as "the block
  that equals `Q_A`" — all are stratum-2c coordinate readers and are excluded.

## 6. Two-sign-grading gate (`two_sign_gradings_decomposition_unique`)

`two_sign_gradings_decomposition_unique` needs **two** `±1` sign operators whose
joint `(Bool × Bool)` spectrum separates the sectors it grades. The four channels
carry these `±1` signs (read from the formulas; `+ = true`):

| selector | A(perture) | C(losure) | T(urn) | E(=solder) |
|----------|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| `Γ` chirality (`*_even` / `solder_odd`) | + | + | + | − |
| `s_c` has-a-solder-letter (parity of `solderDeg>0`) | − | − | + | − |
| `ε` edge-antisymmetrization | + | − | + | + |
| `χ` edge swap | + | + | + | + |

Machine-checked (`decide`, four independent goals):

* `(Γ, s_c)` collide **A = C** (witness `0,1`).
* `(Γ, ε)` collide **A = T** (witness `0,2`).
* `(s_c, ε)` collide **A = E** (witness `0,3`).
* any pair using the vacuous `χ` collides trivially.
* the **triple** `(Γ, s_c, ε)` is injective on all four channels.

**Conclusion (step 6):** **No** — no pair of the natural live sign selectors has
the separated joint spectrum required, so
`two_sign_gradings_decomposition_unique` cannot be instantiated on the four
channels by two live sign gradings. Separation needs **three** sign gradings, or
chirality plus the **3-valued** solder degree (which is not a sign operator and
so does not fit the `Bool × Bool` hypothesis at all). This is a genuine carrier
fact, not a circularity or a generic gap: aperture and turn are *both*
chirality-even and edge-symmetric, and only the (non-sign) solder degree tells
them apart.

## 7. Likely Lean blockers

1. **`kernel_solder_homogeneous` (crux, 4a).** Requires presenting `ker eval` as
   the two-sided ideal generated by R1–R8 (+A1–A4) and proving each generator is
   `c`-degree homogeneous, then that `solderSelector` preserves a graded ideal.
   Cleanest path: build `Src` as `FreeAlgebra ℚ Letter` (or `MonoidAlgebra ℚ
   (FreeMonoid Letter)`), define the `ℕ`-grading by `solderDeg`, show the
   defining relations are homogeneous, and invoke graded-ideal machinery. This
   is the real formalization cost (the mathematics is settled).
2. **Surjectivity of `eval`.** `existsUnique_descended_iff` needs
   `Function.Surjective eval`. `eval` surjects onto the *subalgebra* generated by
   the ten operators, not all of `R`. Fix: corestrict to `V := LinearMap.range
   eval` (or take the concrete `V` = `ℚ`-span of evaluated words). Purely a
   packaging step; the descent theorem then applies verbatim.
3. **`ε` descent (4c).** Either prove `ε` preserves `ker₂` (needs a normal-form
   / straddling-product argument, `ε` not being an algebra map) or produce the
   explicit degree-2 `x` for `no_descent_of_relation_witness`. Genuinely open.
4. Adjoint-consistency plumbing (`ι c1d = star (ι c1)`, `Gam`/`phi`
   self-adjoint) must be carried as hypotheses so A1–A4 are available.

The composed package **elaborates today**: `graded_preserves_kernel` is proved;
`solder_descends` and `wordLen_no_descent` typecheck and compose against the
descent API with `sorry` only at obligations 1–2 and the two `eval`-value
hypotheses. The §6 table is fully `decide`-checked.

* **Verdict:** **`POSITIVE LIVE SELECTOR`** (solder degree), with the
  machine-checked boundary that the two-sign-grading completion (§6) is
  unattainable from live sign gradings and needs three sign gradings or the
  3-valued solder degree.
