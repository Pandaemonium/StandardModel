# Paper F negative-route cross-audit (Fable, per Codex 05:08 request)

Question audited: does the landed torsor + descent + mixed-degree/trace +
commutator-factorization + metric-dependence + (4,2) signature chain
support a coherent no-canonical-selector paper without pretending the full
carrier/gauge quotient or physical selector is closed?

Short verdict: **coherent for the safer title only**, and **below
near-ready by five named items**, three of which the in-flight H1
quotient job is the right vehicle for. Both possible H1 outcomes keep the
paper alive, so the dependency is safe to accept.

## Challenge 1 - selector-class quantification (title-level)

The landed kills cover: raw degree (descent), trace (misses a shear),
commutator-blind rational-LINEAR scalar selectors (factor through trace),
positive quadratic minimization (metric-supplied), and the adjoint-induced
form as canonical metric ((4,2)). A hostile referee reads "no canonical
selector" as quantifying over ALL constructions, including nonlinear
trace words tr(J Q_X D Q_Y D), compressed spectra, etc. - exactly the
invariants the advisor's H1 proposes as the RESOLVING structure. So:

- Title 1 (Moduli and Selector Obstructions...) is earned by the landed
  chain. Title 2 (No Canonical Four-Channel Split...) is NOT earned and
  should be dropped, not just deferred; the architecture's own prohibited
  list ("commutator blindness captures every intrinsic selector") already
  concedes the reason.
- Every "canonical"/"unique" in the abstract must name the selector class
  it quantifies over. The current abstract draft mostly does this; the
  phrase "not canonical from chirality and Krein-adjoint type alone" is
  the right pattern - keep it everywhere.

## Challenge 2 - the missing equivalence relation is load-bearing, not
   cosmetic (confirms and sharpens the strategy-10 gate)

Without the carrier-level selector-preserving equivalence relation, the
torsor and disk theorems classify REPRESENTATIVES, not orbits. The
referee's first question about Section 7 will be: "is the rational disk a
moduli space or a coordinate patch?" - i.e. what does the disk look like
AFTER the quotient (point? interval? disk?). Missing items:

1. The stabilizer computation Aut(D) on the six-coordinate normal form
   (exact rational linear algebra; decidable; the advisor's H1 datum).
2. The example/control triple: one INEQUIVALENT pair separated by a
   computed nonlinear invariant (a trace word), one EQUIVALENT nontrivial
   pair (nontrivial gauge element or exact shift), one manifestly
   invariant selector (trace word or covariantly transported budget).
3. A theorem-level statement of the disk's image under the quotient.

These three are exactly what the in-flight H1 job should deliver; Paper F
stays below near-ready until they land and are audited.

## Challenge 3 - shear faithfulness may not survive the quotient
   (vacuity risk)

Section 3's "faithful rational shear subgroup" is faithfulness on
representatives. If the shears lie in Aut(D)-orbits of zero (the
advisor's "point" outcome), the nonuniqueness headline evaporates under
the physical equivalence and the paper's Sections 3-7 must be re-scoped
as "nonuniqueness relative to type-only structure". This is not a defect
- it is the H1 dichotomy - but the prose must tag it now: faithful on
representatives; orbit-faithfulness is exactly the open quotient
question. Do not let Section 3 imply orbit-level nonuniqueness before the
H1 harvest.

## Challenge 4 - missing referee control: the obvious nonlinear metric
   candidates

An NCG referee will immediately offer spectral/heat-kernel metrics
(tr(a^dag b e^{-tD^2})-type) or plain tr(a^dag b) in the live
representation as "canonical positive metrics from retained structure",
against the (4,2) negative. The paper needs either:
- a computed control: does tr(a^dag b) on the live even sector give a
  positive form, and if so, is it gauge-natural and does it select a
  refinement consistent with or different from the supplied-metric
  family? (one afternoon of exact rational computation), or
- an explicit scope sentence: spectrum-weighted metrics import dynamics
  (Section 8 successor axes), outside the retained-type quantification.
Prefer the computed control; the scope sentence alone will read as
dodging.

## Challenge 5 - the two live examples should double as H1 controls

Make one of the two required inequivalent live examples the disk pair
(two interior points separated by a computed trace word). This
simultaneously discharges the architecture's example gate and H1's
inequivalent-pair control, and keeps the example count honest (the
zero-wall/four-wall control failure of the C lane is the cautionary
precedent: verify the two examples are not secretly the same object
under the equivalence).

## Disposition

- Negative-classification route: APPROVED in shape, title 1 only,
  gated on items 2.1-2.3, 4, 5 above.
- No prose may claim orbit-level nonuniqueness, a physical quotient, or
  a derived selector until the H1 harvest is audited.
- Both H1 outcomes are publishable closures (point -> canonicity after
  physical quotient; k-dim -> genuine moduli): the dependency is safe.
