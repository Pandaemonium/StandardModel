## Objective

Build on the completed controlled HNU/pi composite and the exact HNU endpoint.
Replace the free constant spectral complement by the smallest explicit finite
parent or boundary architecture that keeps the selected HNU sector while
placing compensating zero/pi content in a visible second sector. If the
requested combination is impossible in the current rank/range, prove a sharp
minimality no-go.

## Required ladder

1. Define all parent, boundary, and complement spaces and projectors
   explicitly. No unnamed quotient or projected-away sector.
2. Prove full-operator unitarity and exact restriction to the selected HNU
   endpoint.
3. Give a state-level census of every `+1` and `-1` eigensector on the closed
   momentum cube, including explicit nonzero witnesses.
4. State and prove the strongest honest locality statement available. A
   constant `-I` block alone is only a spectral control and is not success.
5. Track the opposite chirality or pi contribution in one finite ledger. Do
   not infer a winding number from endpoint eigenvalues alone.
6. If a rank-one selected line plus the present complement cannot satisfy all
   gates, prove which gate fails and the minimum additional layer, register,
   or range the algebra forces.
7. Add standard-three guards and scope every conclusion.

## Boundary

This task must distinguish a finite spectral parent from a real-space local
bulk-boundary construction. It may return a theorem, a counterexample, or a
sharpened missing axiom, but not a renamed free complement. No proof
placeholders, compiled evaluation, or new assumptions.
