# Q8. The quantum theory: second quantization on a finite Krein complex

The carrier is first-quantized: one operator on one finite Krein space. Nature is
a quantum field theory. Build the bridge at full precision - or show which
standard piece fails and what replaces it.

## Setup

Assets: the finite carrier D (Krein-self-adjoint, chirality-graded); the
flat-sector/quotient positivity program (Q1) which, if it succeeds, yields a
physical Hilbert quotient V'/N with an induced self-adjoint Hamiltonian-shaped
operator; the checkerboard path-sum reading (finite null zigzags with turn
weights) as the would-be functional integral; retarded transports as the
causality/ordering structure.

## The questions

1. THE FOCK FUNCTOR ON KREIN DATA. For a FINITE Krein one-particle space
   (V, J, D), what is the correct fermionic second quantization? Spell out the
   Kugo-Ojima-style construction: Fock space over V (indefinite), the quartet
   mechanism decoupling the kappa negative directions, the BRST-shaped charge Q
   whose cohomology is the physical Fock space, and the statement
   "H_phys = Fock(V'/N)" (does second quantization commute with our
   Gupta-Bleuler quotient? state the precise finite theorem, which would be a
   beautiful Lean target).
2. DYNAMICS AND UNITARITY. With D as the one-particle generator, define the
   finite S-matrix (interaction picture on the complex: transport = free,
   turn = interaction, or a better split you propose). State the exact finite
   theorem "S is unitary on the physical quotient" with its hypotheses
   (J-unitarity of the one-particle evolution? positive-energy condition?).
   Where does the Krein structure threaten unitarity and what exactly saves it?
3. THE PATH SUM. Prove-or-refute at the finite level: the checkerboard-style sum
   over null zigzag configurations with corner weight phi REPRODUCES the
   second-quantized transition amplitudes of construction (1) (a finite
   fermionic Feynman-Kac/Lie-Trotter statement). Which sign/ordering structure
   (fermionic minus signs from crossing zigzags) implements antisymmetry, and
   is that where spin-statistics enters a null-edge world? State the smallest
   complex on which this equality is checkable by explicit computation.
4. INTERACTIONS BEYOND YUKAWA. The turn operator gives fermion-scalar coupling;
   the closure slot couples to gauge data. What is the finite-complex origin of
   4-point interactions and of the gauge self-coupling - are they generated by
   the SQUARE structure (D^2 cross terms) or must they be added? If the former,
   the framework has a preferred interaction structure: derive its qualitative
   coupling relations.
5. RENORMALIZATION AS COARSE-GRAINING. In the refinement category of Q6, second
   quantization should intertwine with coarse-graining. What is the finite
   statement of "integrating out a refinement layer produces the same effective
   couplings as the standard RG at one loop" in the smallest tractable case?
   Even the 1+1 Yukawa toy version is valuable.
6. LITERATURE: Kugo-Ojima; Strocchi's indefinite-metric QFT books; fermionic
   checkerboard/path-integral constructions; algebraic QFT on finite/discrete
   structures; causal fermion systems' measure-based quantization as a
   comparison. Exact citations.

## Success criterion

Construction (1) and theorem-shape (2) precise enough to formalize their finite
cores; a verdict on (3) with the smallest checkable instance; honest labeling
throughout. If the correct answer is "second quantization forces infinite
dimensionality somewhere and here is the exact spot the finite program must
hand off", that spot is the deliverable.
