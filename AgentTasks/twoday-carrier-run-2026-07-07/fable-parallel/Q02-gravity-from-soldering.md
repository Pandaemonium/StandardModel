# Q2. Derive gravity: field equations from the soldering-gradient slot

Aim: promote the E-slot from "the gravity-shaped remainder" to an actual discrete
gravitational theory with field equations and a continuum limit. This is the
general-relativity half of the unification target.

## Setup

In the varying-soldering regime the kernel-checked identity is
4 D^#D = Q_A^# + Q_C^# + 4 Q_T + 4 E_#, with E built from covariant gradients of
the soldering data: schematically E ~ sum contractions of [nabla_e, c(alpha_f)] -
the failure of the soldering to be covariantly constant. Our internal strategy
result decomposes 2E = C(T) + C(S) with T(e,f) = nabla_e alpha_f - nabla_f alpha_e
antisymmetric ("torsion-shaped") and S symmetric. The natural reading is
teleparallel: flat transport, gravity carried by torsion of the soldering
(Weitzenboeck connection), as in the teleparallel equivalent of GR (TEGR), where
Einstein-Hilbert = torsion scalar + total-derivative boundary term.

## The questions

1. ACTION AND VARIATION. Take the finite action S[alpha] = Tr(E-quadratic
   functional of the soldering) - propose the precise quadratic torsion
   contraction (the discrete analog of the TEGR torsion scalar with its three
   irreducible pieces and their 1 : 1/2 : -2 coefficients... derive what the
   discrete coefficients must be, do not assume). Vary with respect to the
   soldering data alpha_e at fixed complex and transports: what are the discrete
   field equations? Under what refinement do they converge to the teleparallel
   form of Einstein's equations?
2. BOUNDARY TELESCOPING (our conjecture - prove or kill). On a CLOSED finite
   complex, Tr E should reduce to pure quadratic torsion contractions; on a
   complex with boundary, the difference should telescope to a boundary
   functional - the discrete Gibbons-Hawking-York term of teleparallel gravity.
   Make this exact: what combinatorial structure (orientation, dual cells) does
   the telescoping need? Give the statement in a form checkable on a 2-torus
   and on a 2-disc.
3. BIANCHI AND CONSERVATION. What is the discrete Bianchi identity for T(e,f),
   and does it imply, via the field equations, a discrete covariant conservation
   law for the source (the Q_A + Q_C + Q_T stress data)? This is the
   consistency condition for "matter curves the soldering."
4. COUPLING CONSTANT. In the decomposition, the relative normalization of the
   E-slot against Q_C fixes Newton's constant against the gauge coupling at the
   finite level. What sets it? Is there a natural normalization (index-theoretic
   or heat-coefficient) under which the ratio is computable rather than free?
5. WHAT COULD MAKE THIS WRONG. The E-slot might be pure gauge (removable by
   redecorating), or might encode only a nondynamical background. State the
   sharpest finite test distinguishing "dynamical discrete gravity" from
   "bookkeeping remainder" - a quantity computable on a small complex whose
   nonvanishing certifies dynamics.
6. LITERATURE: TEGR (Aldrovandi-Pereira; Maluf; Bahamonde et al review),
   discrete/Regge torsion formulations, Dimakis-Mueller-Hoissen discrete
   differential geometry, anything doing teleparallelism on complexes/graphs.
   Exact citations.

## Success criterion

The discrete field equations (exact finite statements), the boundary-telescoping
verdict with the needed hypotheses, and a 3-lemma formalization ladder for the
2-torus check. A demonstration that the E-slot CANNOT be dynamical gravity, with
the precise obstruction, is equally valuable.
