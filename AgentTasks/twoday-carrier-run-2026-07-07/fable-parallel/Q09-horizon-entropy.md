# Q9. Horizons, entropy, and the boundary of the E-slot

Null edges are the native substrate of horizons (horizon generators ARE null
congruences). If the program is right that gravity lives in the soldering
gradient, then black-hole thermodynamics should be a THEOREM FAMILY about
boundary functionals of finite complexes. Find the first such theorem.

## Setup

Assets: the E-slot (soldering-gradient/torsion block) with our conjecture that on
bounded complexes its trace telescopes to a boundary functional (discrete
Gibbons-Hawking, Q2); the kernel-checked strong-coupling Wilson-loop area law in
the same repository (a statistical area-proportional functional, currently a
gauge-theory fact, not a gravity fact); the aperture identity making
null-direction spread an exact mass; the index machinery.

## The questions

1. THE AREA OPERATOR. On a finite null-edge complex, what is the natural area
   functional of a codimension-1 cut - edge counting weighted by soldering
   (sum over pierced edges of |alpha_e|-type data)? Relate it to the aperture
   block: is "area of a cut" literally Q_A restricted to the cut's edges? Make
   the candidate definition exact and basis-independent.
2. ENTROPY PROPORTIONAL TO AREA. Take the simplest finite horizon model: a
   bipartition of the complex with the cut playing the horizon. Compute (or
   reduce to a finite computation) the entanglement entropy of the flat/ground
   sector across the cut. Under what hypotheses is it proportional to the edge
   count of the cut with a universal coefficient - the finite
   Bekenstein-Hawking shadow (Sorkin's entanglement-entropy origin;
   Srednicki-style area law, combinatorialized)? State the theorem shape.
3. THE COEFFICIENT. Both the (conjectured) Gibbons-Hawking boundary functional
   of Tr E and the entanglement entropy of question 2 are area-proportional.
   The physics claim "horizon entropy = boundary gravitational action" is a
   RATIO statement: their coefficients are locked together (the 1/4). What
   fixes the ratio in a finite complex - the same normalization freedom as
   Newton's constant in Q2? Give the sharpest finite identity that would lock
   it (even schematically), and what could make the lock impossible
   (species-problem analog: does the coefficient depend on decoration content?).
4. FIRST LAW. A finite first law: perturb the turn/aperture data crossing the
   cut (throw mass through the horizon) and track the change of the boundary
   functional. delta(Area-functional) = (coefficient) x delta(mass-form flux)?
   What plays surface gravity - a soldering eigenvalue at the cut? Jacobson's
   derivation (Einstein equations from delta Q = T dS on local horizons)
   suggests the profound version: if the finite first law holds for ALL cuts,
   do the Q2 field equations FOLLOW? That would derive gravity from
   thermodynamics inside our own framework - assess honestly whether the finite
   version of Jacobson's argument can be a theorem.
5. THE AREA LAW WE ALREADY HAVE. Is there a real bridge (not a pun) between the
   kernel-checked Wilson-loop area law and horizon entropy - e.g. both as
   avatars of one boundary functional in the strong-coupling/high-temperature
   corner? Or is the resemblance superficial? A precise verdict either way
   prevents a future overclaim.
6. LITERATURE: Jacobson 1995; Sorkin entanglement entropy 1983/2014; Srednicki;
   Bianchi-Myers; causal-set horizon entropy (Dou-Sorkin); LQG isolated-horizon
   counting for contrast; Wall's proofs of horizon theorems. Exact citations.

## Success criterion

The exact finite definitions (area functional, cut entropy), one theorem-shaped
statement each for questions 2 and 4 with hypotheses displayed, and the verdict
on the Jacobson upgrade. Label speculative steps loudly; this question has the
highest hype-risk of the ten and therefore the highest honesty bar.
