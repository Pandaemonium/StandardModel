# Q3. The unification audit: which no-go theorems bite, and what exactly CAN be true

The maximal claim the program could ever make: ONE finite Krein carrier
D = sum_e c(alpha_e) nabla_e + Gamma phi on one decorated 2-complex, whose
refinement limit yields (a) Einstein gravity from the E-slot, (b) Yang-Mills from
Q_C, (c) Higgs + Yukawa from Q_T, (d) chiral fermion content from ind(D), with
(e) a positive physical sector. Before we spend years on it: audit it against
everything known to forbid such things. Be the harshest referee alive; then tell
us the strongest statement that SURVIVES.

## The questions

1. COLEMAN-MANDULA. Our "unification is decomposition" happens at the level of
   one operator identity, not a symmetry group mixing spacetime and internal
   charges. Does Coleman-Mandula (or its Lorentzian/S-matrix hypotheses) even
   apply to a finite Krein complex with emergent spacetime? State precisely what
   the theorem forbids in the continuum limit and which of its hypotheses
   (Poincare invariance, particle finiteness, analyticity) our construction
   would/would not satisfy. If it constrains us, does it push the construction
   toward a graded (supersymmetric) escape - and is the Gamma-grading of the
   carrier already secretly that?
2. WEINBERG-WITTEN. If the graviton emerges from the E-slot of a theory whose
   fundamental description is a finite spin system on null edges, does
   Weinberg-Witten (no massless spin-2 with a Lorentz-covariant conserved
   stress tensor built from the same dynamics) forbid it? Walk through the
   loophole taxonomy (emergent/composite gravitons, no exact continuum stress
   tensor, background independence, holography) and say which loophole, if any,
   the null-edge construction naturally occupies.
3. NIELSEN-NINOMIYA. Chiral fermions from a finite complex face doubling. Our
   assets: retarded (forward) transports break the relevant reflection;
   Ginsparg-Wilson/overlap chirality is already kernel-checked in the
   repository; the index-protection family fixes the protected chiral count.
   State the precise finite-complex version of the doubling theorem (which
   hypotheses: locality, hermiticity, translation invariance, exact chirality)
   and identify the exact hypothesis our carrier violates. Is retardedness
   functioning as a Wilson term, as an overlap kernel, or as something new?
4. CONNES' AXIOMS. Compare the carrier to a finite Lorentzian/Krein spectral
   triple: first-order condition, real structure J, KO-dimension, the
   fermion-quadrupling problem of Euclidean NCG and its Lorentzian resolutions
   (Barrett; van den Dungen-van Suijlekom; Bizi-Besnard-Brouder). Which NCG
   axioms does the null-edge carrier satisfy, which does it violate, and is any
   violation fatal to reproducing the Standard Model coupling structure - or is
   it exactly the freedom we need?
5. THE SURVIVING STATEMENT. After the audit: write, in one page, the strongest
   unification claim consistent with all four theorem families - stated as a
   sequence of finite theorems plus limit statements with explicit hypotheses,
   each labeled T|H / C with a kill-condition. This becomes our program charter;
   make every word count.
6. LITERATURE: exact citations for each theorem, its sharpest modern form, and
   the best existing emergent-gravity/emergent-gauge constructions that survived
   similar audits (e.g. induced gravity a la Sakharov, analog gravity limits,
   Levin-Wen emergent gauge, causal fermion systems, causal sets + matter).

## Success criterion

A verdict table (theorem x applies?/hypothesis-violated/loophole) with one
paragraph of justification per cell, plus the one-page surviving charter. If you
conclude the maximal claim is unrescuable, the charter should be the strongest
rescuable weakening - and say explicitly what was lost.
