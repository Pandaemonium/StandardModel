# Q5. Three generations: mechanism, minimal model, kill-conditions

Why does the massless-protected content of nature come in THREE copies with
identical charges and wildly different masses? In our frame the question becomes
combinatorial-algebraic: what natural structure on a null-edge complex forces
exactly three protected chiral families?

## Setup

Assets: the chiral index is kernel-checked deformation-invariant (so "number of
protected families" is a topological count: engineering ind = 3 x (one-generation
block) is well-posed); the turn operator phi is the mass channel (Q_T = phi^2);
the octonion/triality toolkit is formalized in the repository; the checkerboard
program gives turn-graphs whose cycles carry the mass data.

## The questions

1. MECHANISM TAXONOMY. Evaluate the candidate mechanisms for a forced
   multiplicity 3, each with its sharpest finite statement and its most likely
   failure: (a) covering degree - the physical complex is a 3-fold cover of a
   base, generations = deck transformations; (b) Spin(8) triality acting on the
   three 8-dimensional representations, generations = triality orbit
   (Boyle-style E8 or octonionic constructions); (c) cycle/homology structure -
   ind localizes on H_1 of the turn graph with a rank-3 sector; (d) three
   embeddings of C into H or O (Manogue-Dray-Wilson-style); (e) exceptional
   Jordan algebra J_3(O) with its three off-diagonal blocks (Todorov,
   Dubois-Violette, Boyle). Rank them by: naturalness on a 2-complex,
   compatibility with our index machinery, and falsifiability.
2. THE MINIMAL TOY. Design the smallest finite model (explicit dimensions and
   decorations) in which the protected chiral content is EXACTLY three
   isomorphic families - not 1, not 2, not 4 - as a consequence of the chosen
   mechanism rather than by hand-tuning three copies. What theorem would
   certify "three is forced" (e.g. index = 3 AND automorphism group acts
   transitively on the families)?
3. MASS SPLITTING WITHIN THE MECHANISM. For your preferred mechanism: does it
   naturally produce a hierarchical spectrum for phi^dagger phi across the three
   families (covering monodromy eigenvalues? triality-breaking chain?), or must
   hierarchy come from elsewhere? Connect to the empirical pattern (the three
   charged-lepton masses; do NOT assume the Koide relation but state what the
   mechanism says about it).
4. MIXING. In this picture CKM/PMNS mixing = misalignment between two turn
   structures (up-type vs down-type phi on the same complex). What does each
   mechanism predict qualitatively for mixing-angle sizes and why is lepton
   mixing large while quark mixing is small? Even a sharp toy-level statement
   is valuable.
5. KILL-CONDITIONS. For each surviving mechanism: the cheapest finite
   computation that could falsify it inside our framework (dimensions small
   enough to formalize), and the observational fact most dangerous to it.
6. LITERATURE: exact citations for each mechanism family, plus the strongest
   known no-go against generation-counting mechanisms (family symmetries and
   their anomaly constraints).

## Success criterion

A ranked mechanism verdict, one fully-specified minimal toy for the winner with
its certifying theorem stated finitely, and the kill-condition table. "Three
cannot be forced by any local mechanism on a 2-complex; here is why and here is
the smallest structure that does force it" is an equally successful answer.
