# Q12 (round 2). The chirality-solder audit: confirm the defusal or break it

Round-1 (section 5e) flagged one check as "the single most dangerous internal"
one for the generations mechanism: if the physical grading Gamma is ever
forced to couple to internal chirality, the per-sector index degrades
3 -> 1 - 1 + 1 = 1 and triality-as-monodromy dies as a THREE-generation
mechanism. Our own follow-up analysis claims a partial defusal; your job is to
confirm it adversarially, break it, or sharpen its boundary.

## Our analysis (build on it or overturn it loudly)

In the COMPOSED architecture - strand pentad `Lambda(C^3 + C^2)` for one
generation's content (5d), tensored with a family delivery factor `C[Z/3]`
carrying the monodromy tau as the cyclic SHIFT (TOY-A form) - the internal
parity `(-1)^F` lives on the strand factor while tau lives on the family
factor: `[Gamma_total, 1 (x) tau] = 0` by construction. Moreover for exactly
tau-equivariant decorations (circulant phi, equivariant transports), tau
commutes with D, hence with any Ginsparg-Wilson-descended grading of the
schematic form `Gamma' = Gamma (1 - a D)`; equivariance breaking makes the
family sectors approximate (physical flavor) while the total index stays
rigid. The danger is therefore LIVE only in the PURE INTERNAL variant -
families = 8v, 8s, 8c inside one Spin(8) module, where tau genuinely cycles
the two internal half-spinor parities - and that variant is independently
disfavored by the gauge-outer rail and the self-conjugacy (Distler-Garibaldi)
index lemma.

## The questions

1. ADVERSARIAL CONFIRMATION. Is the defusal argument correct AND complete for
   the composed model? Hunt specifically for indirect soldering channels we
   may have missed: (a) does the L4c local-triality identity (the
   octonion-multiplication equivariance that earns the name "triality") force
   the family factor to interact with internal chirality after all when the
   octonionic seed V_O = O^3 replaces the plain C[Z/3]? (b) does the Krein
   structure (the # slot, or the J_R of Q11) couple the factors - e.g. if J_R
   involves degree reversal on the strand factor AND edge reversal on the
   base, does the composite forced by consistency touch the family factor?
   (c) do the U1/U3 charter obligations (Gupta-Bleuler quotient; GW descent
   with index = ind) force any cross-factor coupling on the PHYSICAL sector
   even when the pre-quotient architecture is split?
2. THE ANOMALY ROUTE. Section 5e's original danger was "a future
   anomaly/continuum requirement". Make that concrete: run 't Hooft
   anomaly-matching / mod-2 (Witten) bookkeeping per family sector for the
   composed model. Does consistency of the GW chiral measure (Luescher-type
   existence, section 5d row C6) impose conditions PER tau-eigensector that
   the composed model can fail while the total passes? If yes, that is the
   sharp modern form of the solder danger - state it as a finite condition.
3. THE BOUNDARY. Characterize exactly which architectures are in the danger
   zone: give the general criterion ("the per-sector index degrades iff tau
   fails to commute with the projection onto the physical grading's
   eigenspaces iff [structural condition on how families are delivered]"),
   with the pure-Spin(8) variant as the worked example of failure and the
   composed model as the worked example of safety. A one-page decision
   procedure an executor can run on any future proposal.
4. TOY CERTIFICATION. Specify the exact finite commutation lemmas (on
   TOY-A (x) strand-fiber dimensions: 3 x 2 base blocks x 32 fiber) whose
   kernel-checking certifies the composed model's safety - including the
   equivariant-breaking probe orders (our registered expectations: total ind
   rigid at all eps; family commutant 3 -> 1 at generic eps;
   theta_23 - pi/4 = O(eps^2)). Confirm or correct those orders.
5. If you find the composed model UNSAFE, say so first, exhibit the smallest
   dimensions where the per-sector count degrades, and state the repair (or
   the verdict that the mechanism only ever yields ind = 3 without three
   IDENTIFIABLE families).

## Success criterion

A verdict on the defusal (confirmed / broken / boundary-corrected) with the
decision procedure of question 3 and the certification lemma list of
question 4. Breaking our analysis with a concrete counterexample is the most
valuable outcome; confirming it with the anomaly route made precise is the
second most valuable.
