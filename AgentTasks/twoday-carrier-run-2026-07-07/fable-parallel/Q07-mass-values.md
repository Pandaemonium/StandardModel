# Q7. Mass values: from free decoration to computed spectrum (Layer 5)

The program's endgame honesty test: a mass EXPLANATION must eventually constrain
mass VALUES. Today the turn amplitude phi is a free decoration. Find the first
place where the framework itself computes a number.

## Setup

Mass channels: Q_T = phi^2 (turn), Q_A = Q(sum alpha) (aperture; kernel-checked
identification), Q_C (closure). The 1+1 dynamical layer is kernel-checked: the
chirality-flip amplitude equals the kinematic wedge (mass = rate of chirality
exchange). In the checkerboard reading, a particle's mass is the weight per
corner of its null zigzag; a composite's mass is pairwise null-direction
disagreement. Empirical targets at the bottom of the ladder: charged-lepton
ratios (the Koide combination Q = (sum m_i)/((sum sqrt(m_i))^2) = 2/3 to 1e-5,
status: unexplained numerology - treat with appropriate suspicion), quark/lepton
hierarchy spanning 6 orders, and the top Yukawa being O(1).

## The questions

1. WHERE THE FIRST NUMBER LIVES. In a framework whose only inputs are a finite
   complex, decorations, and normalizations, which dimensionless mass ratio is
   most plausibly COMPUTABLE first? Candidates: ratios of phi^dagger phi
   eigenvalues fixed by graph symmetry breaking chains; aperture/turn
   interference on specific small complexes; monodromy spectra of the turn
   holonomy around independent cycles. Pick the winner and design the minimal
   model where the ratio is a theorem, not a fit.
2. HIERARCHY MECHANISM. Exponential hierarchies from order-one discrete data:
   evaluate (a) tunneling/overlap suppression - masses as overlaps of localized
   chiral modes on the complex (the split-fermion/wavefunction-overlap
   mechanism, discretized), (b) power-of-holonomy - masses as n-th powers of a
   single sub-unit transport eigenvalue (Froggatt-Nielsen, combinatorialized:
   powers = path lengths in the turn graph), (c) near-null alignment - masses as
   wedges of nearly-collinear null directions (our aperture identity makes
   m ~ sin(theta/2) exact, kernel-checked at 1+1). Which one is native to null
   edges rather than imported? What does each predict for the RUNNING of ratios
   (do our finite ratios match masses at what scale)?
3. KOIDE AS A TEST CASE, NOT A TARGET. If the charged leptons are three
   protected modes of one mechanism from Q5, what does each Q5 mechanism imply
   for the Koide combination - generic value, or exactly 2/3 under a stated
   symmetry (the known phase-democracy derivations), or nothing? Give the
   honest verdict on whether Koide is signal or noise from our vantage.
4. WHAT IS NOT COMPUTABLE. Which observed mass facts can this framework never
   compute even in principle (e.g. the absolute scale - if everything is ratios
   against the complex's step, say so), so we never claim them. The boundary
   between computable and free is itself a deliverable.
5. FALSIFIABILITY GATE. Design the pre-registered test: the smallest model +
   quantity such that IF the framework is right about mass origin, the computed
   ratio must land within a stated window, with the kill-condition explicit.
6. LITERATURE: wavefunction-overlap/split-fermion mass mechanisms,
   Froggatt-Nielsen, Koide-relation derivations and critiques, deconstruction
   (Arkani-Hamed-Cohen-Georgi) as the closest existing "physics from a graph"
   mass framework. Exact citations.

## Success criterion

One minimal model with one computable dimensionless ratio and its
theorem-statement; the hierarchy-mechanism verdict; the computable/free boundary
stated crisply. A proof-shaped argument that NO ratio is computable without
additional principles - identifying them - is equally valuable.
