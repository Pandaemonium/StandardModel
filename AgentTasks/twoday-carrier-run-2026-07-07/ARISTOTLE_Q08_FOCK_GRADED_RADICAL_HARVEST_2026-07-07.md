## What Landed

- `bilinDS`: orthogonal direct sum of bilinear forms on a Lean `DirectSum`.
- `bilinDS_apply_lof_left` and `bilinDS_apply_lof_right`: homogeneous-generator
  evaluation lemmas.
- `bilinDS_ker_iff`: the radical of an orthogonal direct-sum form is detected
  component by component.
- `bilinDS_isSymm`: direct sums of symmetric forms remain symmetric.
- `bilinDS_nondegenerate_iff`: the direct-sum form is nondegenerate exactly when
  every summand form is nondegenerate.
- `fockGradedForm`: the all-particle-number orthogonal direct sum of the
  degreewise Gram-determinant forms `exteriorForm n h`.
- `fockGraded_radical_componentwise`: for symmetric `h`, an element of
  `DirectSum n, exteriorPower K n W` lies in the radical of the graded Fock form
  iff every homogeneous component lies in the kernel of the corresponding
  exterior-power projection onto the radical quotient.

## Claim Boundary

This is finite/graded multilinear algebra over a field.  It upgrades the prior
fixed-degree `FockQuotientPairing.exteriorForm_radical_eq` theorem to a
componentwise all-degree direct-sum statement.

It does not prove positivity, Hilbert completion, a physical-sector quotient,
or the stronger span/ideal theorem saying that the graded kernel is generated by
decomposable wedges with at least one radical factor.
