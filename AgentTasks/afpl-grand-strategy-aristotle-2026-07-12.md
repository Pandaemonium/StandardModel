# Aristotle grand strategy job: AFPL five-year physics program

## Mission

The Autonomous Fundamental Physics Lab aims within five years to develop and
test a complete finite null-information candidate description connecting
relativistic quantum dynamics, interacting QFT, gauge/chiral structure,
continuum Lorentz symmetry, gravity, and absolute scales. The lab values bold
synthesis only when every headline has an explicit theorem, supplied-input
ledger, nondegenerate witness, convention audit, empirical consequence, and
kill condition.
